# The Simple (& Proven Way) To Make $100K From Social Media

## Overview

This money teaching outlines a social media strategy for generating at least $100,000 per year in income from audiences of any size, including under 100 followers. Based on Simon Squibb's journey from zero to 17 million followers over six years, the core principle is that follower count alone does not determine earning potential — a direct connection with at least 100 followers who understand why they follow you (measured by a minimum 5% engagement rate on your posts, meaning at least 5 likes, comments, or shares per 100 followers) is sufficient to build a profitable income stream. The framework emphasizes treating your social media presence as a low-stakes experiment where you post at least once every 24 hours with content you write yourself, and leveraging live streaming and/or point-of-view (POV) content to grow a personal brand.

## When to Follow These Money Teachings

- When you have zero followers and want to start building an audience and monetizing within 6–12 months
- When you have under 100 followers and want to start earning at least $1,000 per month from that audience
- When you are uncomfortable showing your face on camera and want to build a personal brand using POV (screen-sharing) content instead
- When you want to replace chasing follower counts with posting one piece of content every 24 hours and tracking engagement rate as your primary success metric
- When you want to add one 30-minute live stream per week to your content schedule as a growth tool
- When you want to treat social media as a content experiment (test 3–5 formats, measure engagement rate over 30 days, double down on the best performer) without high-stakes pressure on any single post

## Steps

### Step 1: Adopt the Zero-to-Hero Mindset

Start from your current follower count — even zero followers — and treat social media as a testing ground, not a high-stakes performance. Follower count matters for reach and credibility, but it is not the primary indicator of income potential. You can earn $100,000 per year with 100 followers if those 100 followers trust you and can explain why they follow you (verified by a 5%+ engagement rate and direct messages from followers stating their reason for following). Focus on posting daily, creating content you genuinely believe in, and ignore low view counts or slow follower growth on individual posts.

### Step 2: Choose Your Content Format

Choose between appearing on camera or using a POV (point-of-view) format — showing your screen and speaking over it without showing your face. Appearing on camera builds audience trust 2–3x faster than POV-only content (measured by follower growth rate and direct message engagement in the first 90 days), so choose on-camera content if you are comfortable in front of a camera. If you feel awkward on camera, POV content achieves the same follower growth and income outcomes over a longer timeline (typically 6–12 months vs. 3–6 months for on-camera), and is a fully viable path to monetization.

### Step 3: Show Up Consistently Every Single Day

Post at least once every 24 hours on your chosen platform. Make content creation a scheduled daily habit — set a fixed time each day to film or edit your post, just as you would schedule a gym session. Daily posting builds a measurable audience growth rate (typically 1–5% follower growth per month when done consistently for 90+ days) that posting once per week or sporadically never achieves. Do not judge your success on any single day's post performance — measure success by the number of consecutive days you have posted, and let the cumulative audience growth from daily posting compound over months.

### Step 4: Get Into Live Streaming

Go live at least once per week for a minimum of 30 minutes per session. Add live streaming to your content calendar as a non-negotiable weekly commitment. Live streaming generates direct audience interaction measured by live concurrent viewers and real-time chat messages — both of which are the highest-signal engagement metrics on social platforms and the fastest driver of follower growth (live content typically converts passive viewers to followers at 3–5x the rate of pre-recorded content). Most creators avoid live streaming because it feels unscripted and exposing, but it is the single most effective growth lever available to creators with under 10,000 followers. Treat it as a required component of your weekly content plan.

### Step 5: Build a Personal Brand Through Authenticity

Express your genuine personality, background, and expertise in every post — do not copy content from other creators or adopt a persona you do not live. Whether on camera or through POV content, your specific experiences and perspective are the only differentiating factor between your account and every other account in your niche. The more accurately your content reflects who you actually are (verified by audience feedback: followers who comment "this is so you" or send DMs referencing specific personal details you have shared), the stronger your personal brand becomes, and the more your audience feels a direct one-to-one relationship with you — which is the condition required to convert followers into paying customers.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Small-Audience Monetizer

Sarah starts with 80 followers on Instagram. She posts one POV video per day showing her screen while learning web design, narrating her thought process as she works through tutorials. She replies to every comment with a personalized response and asks each commenter what specific web design skill they want to learn next. Within 60 days, she reaches 200 followers with a 12% average engagement rate (24+ total likes, comments, and shares per post). She launches a $49 mini-course on web design basics and sells 40 copies in her first week — earning $1,960. She did not need thousands of followers; she needed 100 followers who had told her directly (via comment or DM) why they were following her.

### Example 2: The Live-Streaming Fitness Coach

James starts on TikTok with zero followers. He posts one live workout per week (minimum 30 minutes) and one POV fitness tip video per day. He feels awkward on camera at first but posts every day without skipping. By month 3, he has 300 followers with a 9% engagement rate, and 40–60 of them attend each live session consistently. He monetizes through affiliate links for fitness gear (earning $15–$30 per sale) and a $19/month subscription for personalized workout plans. By month 12, he has 1,500 followers, 220 paying subscribers, and is earning $114,000 per year from subscription revenue alone — all built on a schedule of one live per week and one daily POV post, not a massive follower count.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Start from zero followers — having no audience means no existing expectations to meet, which removes pressure and lets you test content freely
- ✅ Prioritize your first 100 followers: identify which followers comment or DM most often, ask each of them directly (via comment reply or DM) why they follow you, and create more content that matches their stated reason
- ✅ Treat social media as a content experiment: test 3–5 different post formats, track which format receives the highest engagement rate over a 30-day period, and double down on that format
- ✅ Post at least once every 24 hours and schedule your content creation at the same time each day to make it a non-negotiable routine
- ✅ Go live at least once per week for a minimum of 30 minutes as a required part of your content calendar
- ✅ Use POV content if being on camera makes you uncomfortable — POV content achieves the same income outcomes as on-camera content over a 6–12 month timeline
- ✅ Use on-camera content if you are comfortable in front of a camera — on-camera content achieves the same income outcomes 2–3x faster (3–6 months vs. 6–12 months) based on follower growth rate

- ❌ Do not wait until you have more than 100 followers before launching your first monetization offer (a digital product, service, or affiliate link)
- ❌ Do not use follower count or view count as your primary success metric — use engagement rate (total likes + comments + shares ÷ follower count) and number of consecutive posting days instead
- ❌ Do not skip live streaming because it feels uncomfortable — live streaming is required to reach $100K/year income at follower counts under 10,000
- ❌ Do not post fewer than 4 times per month — daily posting (at least 30 posts per month) is required to compound audience growth over time
- ❌ Do not ignore comments or DMs from your first 100 followers — responding to each one within 24 hours is the highest-impact activity for building trust and converting followers to paying customers

## Keep In Mind

- Follower count increases your reach and credibility with potential collaborators or customers, but it is not the primary driver of income — a creator with 100 engaged followers (5%+ engagement rate) can earn the same $100,000/year as a creator with 100,000 passive followers (0.1% engagement rate) if both have a monetization offer (product, service, or affiliate link)
- You do not need to show your face on camera to build a profitable social media presence — POV content achieves identical income outcomes to on-camera content; it simply requires a longer timeline (6–12 months vs. 3–6 months) to reach the same follower and income milestones
- The goal is not a viral post — the goal is building an audience of at least 100 followers with a 5%+ engagement rate who can each explain why they follow you, tracked by asking followers directly in comments or DMs
- Daily posting (minimum 30 posts per month, one every 24 hours) compounds into measurable audience growth of 1–5% follower growth per month after 90 consecutive days of posting — sporadic high-effort posts (fewer than 4 posts per month) do not produce this compounding effect regardless of individual post quality
- Monetization is possible at any follower count if two conditions are met: (1) you have at least 100 followers with a 5%+ engagement rate, and (2) you have launched at least one monetization offer (product, service, or affiliate link) and shared it with your audience

## Security & Safety Notes

- During live streams, do not display your full home address, real-time GPS coordinates, phone number, or bank account, credit card, or payment receipt details on screen or in the live chat
- When posting affiliate links or sponsored content, include a clear disclosure in every post caption (e.g., "#ad" or "This post contains affiliate links — I may earn a commission if you purchase through my link") to comply with FTC (U.S.), ASA (U.K.), and equivalent regulations in your country
- When filming POV content outdoors or at a specific property, confirm in writing (or by signage) that you have permission to film on that property — do not film inside private businesses, on private land, or through the windows of private residences without explicit permission from the owner
- Secure all social media accounts with a unique password of at least 16 characters (mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols) and enable two-factor authentication (2FA) via an authenticator app (e.g., Google Authenticator, Authy) — do not use SMS-based 2FA, which is vulnerable to SIM-swap attacks

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Waiting to monetize until you have more than 1,000 followers, missing the opportunity to earn from your first 100 engaged followers (measured by 5%+ engagement rate).
  **Solution:** Launch your first monetization offer (a $1–$99 digital product, a service, or an affiliate link) as soon as you have 100 followers with a 5%+ engagement rate — regardless of your total follower count.

- **Problem:** Using follower count or view count as your primary success metric, which leads to discouragement, burnout, and inconsistent posting schedules.
  **Solution:** Track two metrics only: (1) your engagement rate per post (total likes + comments + shares ÷ follower count, target: 5% or higher), and (2) your current streak of consecutive daily posts. Measure weekly progress on both metrics, not on follower count.

- **Problem:** Skipping live streaming because it feels intimidating, missing the highest-growth content format available to creators with under 10,000 followers.
  **Solution:** Schedule one 30-minute live session per week on your calendar and post the first one even with zero viewers — live streaming converts passive viewers to followers at 3–5x the rate of pre-recorded content, and this conversion rate cannot be replicated through any other content format.

- **Problem:** Posting on-camera content when being on camera makes you visibly uncomfortable (e.g., stiff body language, monotone voice, avoidance of eye contact), which audiences detect and respond to by unfollowing or disengaging.
  **Solution:** Switch to POV content (sharing your screen while narrating over it without showing your face) — POV content achieves the same follower growth rate and income outcomes as on-camera content; it simply takes 6–12 months instead of 3–6 months to reach the same milestones.
