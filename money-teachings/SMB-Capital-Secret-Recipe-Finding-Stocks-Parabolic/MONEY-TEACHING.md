# The Secret Recipe to Finding Stocks BEFORE They Go Parabolic

## Overview

Learn to identify high-probability catalyst plays before they go parabolic by combining pre-market volume scanning, earnings inflection analysis, theme alignment, and market environment assessment. This teaching reveals the five-point grading framework used by SMB Capital traders to evaluate catalyst plays and identify stocks with year-making potential.

## When to Follow These Money Teachings

- When you need to find high-probability catalyst plays before they go parabolic
- When working with earnings-based trades and need to filter candidates by institutional volume signals
- When the user asks about screening stocks for pre-market/extended-hours volume, earnings inflection, or market theme alignment

## Steps

### Step 1: Scan for abnormal pre-market/extended hours volume

Use a pre-market/extended-hours volume-to-average-daily-volume scanner (a tool that shows what percentage of a stock's typical daily volume has traded in the pre-market or extended-hours session) to identify stocks doing at least 20% of their 20-day average daily volume in extended hours. Higher percentages indicate stronger institutional interest and higher probability of trending intraday.

### Step 2: Verify volume holds at market open

Once the market opens, confirm the stock maintains high volume. This is a key criteria for grading the play. Look for the stock to trade at least 30 times its 20-day average daily volume right off the open. Consistent volume above this threshold signals institutional participation and sustained interest.

### Step 3: Analyze the earnings report for an inflection quarter

Look at headline revenue and earnings-per-share numbers first, then build a story by reviewing the last five quarters of results. Identify whether the current quarter is an "inflection acceleration quarter" — a period where revenue growth accelerates significantly after the first quarter in which the company reported clean profitability (GAAP profit without one-time charges or adjustments). This combination of a clean profit beat followed by accelerating revenue growth signals a potential trend change in the business.

### Step 4: Check higher time frame technicals

Ensure the stock has room to run on the chart. Look for base breakouts on weekly or daily timeframes. Confirm the stock is breaking out of a base and above a breakout level.

### Step 5: Align with market themes

Determine which sector or thematic category the stock belongs to (e.g., digital infrastructure, AI infrastructure, semiconductors, clean energy). Use a theme tracker — a tool or watchlist that tracks which market sectors are currently in favor based on price action and sector ETF flows — to verify the stock fits into winning categories rather than sectors facing headwinds (e.g., enterprise software being disrupted by AI-driven tools).

### Step 6: Assess market environment

Evaluate whether the overall market environment supports this type of play. Review how other recent earnings names and breakout candidates have performed — if most are holding gains and not fading after the first 30 minutes, the environment is supportive. An A+ grade means all five checks align and the market is supportive; an A grade means the setup is valid but the broader market environment is weakening potential returns.

### Step 7: Confirm with price action

Wait for the market to confirm your thesis. Look for the stock to break above its pre-market high on the first 2-minute candlestick of the regular trading session, with volume at least 50% above the 20-day average volume per minute. If the price holds above that level on subsequent candles without fading, your thesis is confirmed.

### Step 8: Execute and manage the position

Enter at the pre-market high break or on controlled dips that pull back to within 1-2% of the Volume-Weighted Average Price (VWAP) — the average price weighted by volume throughout the current session. Accumulate on these significant dips rather than chasing extended moves. Use a period where the stock consolidates above VWAP for 15-30 minutes as an opportunity to add to the swing position or capture profit on a separate day trade.

## Examples

### Example 1: Fastly Earnings Play

The traders identified Fastly by its 200% pre-market/extended hours volume relative to its 20-day average daily volume. The stock had its first clean profitable beat quarter in Q3 2025, and the following quarter showed a 50% acceleration in revenue growth. It fit the digital infrastructure theme and was not enterprise software. The market environment was mixed, downgrading it from A+ to A. Price confirmed with a massive first 2-minute candle breaking the pre-market high, and they accumulated on dips of 1-2% back to the Volume-Weighted Average Price (VWAP).

### Example 2: Scanner Filter for Earnings Season

During earnings season with hundreds of reports, use a pre-market/extended-hours volume-to-average-daily-volume scanner to narrow down to names doing >20% of their 20-day average daily volume. Anything over 50% is notable, and 80%+ is exceptional. This scanner helps identify stocks with institutional interest before the market opens.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Use a pre-market/extended-hours volume-to-average-daily-volume scanner as your primary filter
- ✅ Look for at least 20% of the 20-day average daily volume in extended hours before investigating further
- ✅ Build a story by reviewing the last five quarters of earnings
- ✅ Verify the stock fits into a winning market theme
- ✅ Wait for price confirmation at the open before entering
- ❌ Don't trade earnings plays based on headline numbers alone
- ❌ Don't ignore the market environment overlay
- ❌ Don't chase without volume confirmation

## Keep In Mind

- Elevated pre-market/extended-hours volume (20%+ of 20-day average daily volume) is a direct signal that institutions are accumulating or distributing before the broader market participates — follow that activity into the regular session.
- The five grading checks are: (1) pre-market/extended-hours volume, (2) open volume confirmation, (3) earnings inflection analysis, (4) higher-timeframe technical setup, (5) market theme and environment alignment.
- Most of these grading criteria can be evaluated before the regular market session opens, so you can prepare a watchlist the night before or early in the pre-market.
- Stocks with short interest above 12-13% (measured as the percentage of float sold short) can experience more explosive first-day moves when the setup is valid, because short sellers covering their positions add buying pressure to the move.

## Security & Safety Notes

- Always use proper risk management and position sizing
- Never enter a position without price confirmation
- Be aware that pre-market trading has different liquidity characteristics

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Trading based only on headline beat/raise without deeper analysis
  **Solution:** Always review the last five quarters to identify inflection points and acceleration trends

- **Problem:** Misidentifying the stock's theme and market alignment
  **Solution:** Use a theme tracker to correctly categorize the stock and understand whether it benefits from current market trends

- **Problem:** Ignoring market environment when grading setups
  **Solution:** Apply the market environment as an overlay factor that can upgrade or downgrade your final grade

- **Problem:** Entering without volume confirmation
  **Solution:** Wait for the pre-market high break on the first 2-minute candlestick with volume at least 50% above the 20-day average volume per minute before committing capital
