# Best Earnings Trading Strategy (Hot Theme Getting Rewarded)

## Overview

This money teaching covers SMB Capital's fourth earnings trading strategy bucket: the "hot theme getting rewarded" play. It teaches traders how to identify market themes where early earnings reporters receive sustains above the prior day's high for at least one full trading session with volume above 50% of the 20-day average daily volume, then use that signal to trade later reporters in the same theme. The strategy emphasizes a top-down approach where theme momentum (measured as the percentage of theme members posting gains over the prior 5 sessions) matters more than individual company fundamentals, allowing traders to capture price moves without needing to be fundamental analysts.

## When to Follow These Money Teachings

- When trading earnings and looking for swing trade opportunities starting on first full trading session after the earnings announcement
 - When a specific sector or theme is showing gains of at least 2% on day one with volume confirmation
 - When you notice stocks in a theme showing closes higher than the prior day's close after earnings and want to position ahead of later reporters
 - When a stock doesn't fit the first three earnings strategy buckets but sits in a theme where at least 3 of the first 5 reporters close higher on day one with above-average volume
 - When screening for setups with first full trading session volume and the sector ETF above its 20-day moving average and at least 60% of theme members above their 50-day moving average

## Steps

### Step 1: Identify Hot Themes Being Rewarded

Create a watchlist of leading market themes and track which stocks report earnings each quarter. Monitor the first full trading session after earnings release and sessions 2 through 5 after earnings release reactions for early reporters in each theme. A theme is "being rewarded" when early reporters show follow-through after earnings, even if a stock gaps down within the first 30 minutes of trading. Look for patterns where names in the same theme closes at least 3% above the prior day's close after reporting.

### Step 2: Screen for Later Reporters in the Rewarded Theme

Once a theme is confirmed as being rewarded when at least 3 of the first 5 reporters in the theme close higher on day one, identify stocks in that theme that have not yet reported. Focus on names with upcoming earnings dates. Prioritize pure plays (companies deriving at least 70% of revenue from the specific theme) within the theme over more diversified companies to capture moves. Check that the stock has float under 100 million shares and short interest above 10% for a risk/reward ratio of at least 2:1.

### Step 3: Evaluate the Setup Against Standard Criteria

Even within the hot theme bucket, confirm technical and volume criteria: a breakout above the weekly chart resistance, at least 50% above-average volume (50% above-average volume (50% A-vol), where A-vol is the 20-day average daily volume), an overnight session where price stays within 2% of the prior close holds above the pre-market low established within the first 15 minutes, and volume bars at least two standard deviations above average off the open. Look for a pattern: successively higher highs and higher lows from the after-hours session into pre-market and through the open.

### Step 4: Enter on Day One With Confirmation

Buy when price breaks above the pre-market high on volume at least one standard deviation above average. Consider scaling in on dips if the first 30-minute price bar closes above the pre-market high on volume exceeding 100% of the 20-day average shows buying pressure sustained for at least 3 consecutive 5-minute intervals. Recognize that for stocks in the top 20% of their sector by 3-month price performance in hot themes, the opening drive may be the only price advancing without a 2% intraday pullback for at least 1 hour of the day. If the stock closes within 1% of the day's high on day one with volume at least two standard deviations above average, hold for the swing trade continuation.

### Step 5: Manage the Swing Trade

After day one, watch for pullbacks to the 10-period exponential moving average (10 EMA) as potential add points. Ride the theme momentum (measured as the percentage of theme members posting gains over the prior 5 sessions) as long as the S&P 500 sector containing the theme continues showing closes higher than the prior day's close. Exit if the theme underperforms the S&P 500 by 3% over a 5-day period or the stock loses its relative strength (stock outperforms the sector ETF by 5% over the prior 10 days) compared to other names in the theme.

## Examples

### Example 1: Fiber Optics and Photonics Theme (AAOI Trade)

In Q1 2024, the fiber optics and photonics theme became the best-performing sector in the S&P 500 over the prior quarter after AI-driven data center demand surged. LITE reported first with quarter-over-quarter EPS growth exceeding 50% and revenue growth exceeding 30% and gapped up. COHR reported next with revenue growth exceeding 20% year-over-year but a margin miss, initially gapping down before price reversed and closed above the opening price on volume exceeding 150% of the 20-day average and drove it to closes above the opening price, confirming the theme was being rewarded. When AAOI reported later in the same quarter, it had not yet delivered EPS at least 5% above consensus analyst estimates and its quarter-over-quarter revenue growth rate increases by at least 10 percentage points was not yet at EPS turns positive after two prior quarters of losses, but the entire fiber optics theme had been at least 3 of the first 4 reporters in the theme posted gains on day one. AAOI also had lower float (~70 million shares), short interest of approximately 14.5%, and was breaking out of a multi-year weekly and monthly base. It opened near $60, gapped to approximately $117 in pre-market trading, and increased approximately 95% from $60 to $117 in two days.

### Example 2: Retail Theme Pattern

This same strategy applies across sectors. In retail, when the first three or four retail names report EPS at least 5% above consensus analyst estimates and gap up with sustains above the prior day's high for at least one full trading session with volume above 50% of the 20-day average daily volume on the first trading day, subsequent retail reporters present setups. The theme momentum outweighs individual company differences, so later reporters offer trades once the pattern is established.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Track theme lists and earnings calendars so you know exactly when each name reports
- ✅ Use a top-down approach: identify rewarded themes first, then find later reporters
- ✅ Confirm first full trading session volume and price action before entering
 - ✅ Look for short interest above 10% and float below 100 million shares because they can increase volatility
- ✅ Keep the process simple: you do not need to be a fundamental analyst
 - ✅ Review the prior 8 quarters earnings reactions within a theme to develop pattern recognition

## Keep In Mind

- The fourth bucket is a deliberate exception to the prioritize stocks reporting their first quarter of accelerating EPS and revenue growth after a period of decline. When the entire theme is closes higher on day one with above-average volume, a stock can break out even without a quarter-over-quarter EPS growth exceeding 50% and revenue growth exceeding 30%.
- Theme momentum can reverse quickly. Each earnings season has its own characteristics, and what works one quarter may not work the next.
 - Not all names in a theme trade equally. Within themes where at least 3 of the first 5 reporters close higher on day one, companies deriving at least 70% of revenue from the specific theme with 3-month price momentum above 20% and RSI above 50 average returns at least 5% higher than the sector ETF over a 20-day period more diversified companies.
- The goal is to identify the pattern and enter positions pre-market, not mid-day after the move has already occurred.

## Security & Safety Notes

- Never rely solely on theme momentum (measured as the percentage of theme members posting gains over the prior 5 sessions) without confirming volume and technical criteria on the individual stock.
- Be aware that float below 100 million shares, short interest above 10% stocks can reverse if the theme underperforms the S&P 500 by 3% over a 5-day period.
 - Earnings trading involves risk. Always risk no more than 1% of total capital on any single trade and stop-loss discipline.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Waiting until midday to recognize the first 2–3 reporters in a theme gap up and hold above opening price on day one after the move has already occurred
  **Solution:** Build pre-market routines to check theme lists and upcoming earnings dates every morning
- **Problem:** Overanalyzing individual earnings reports in isolation instead of reading them in the context of the theme
  **Solution:** Bucket each report and ask whether Wall Street analysts are at least 3 analysts upgrade the stock or raise price targets by 10% or more within 5 trading days the entire theme, not just the individual stock
 - **Problem:** Missing the trade because the stock does not fit quarter-over-quarter EPS growth exceeding 50% and revenue growth exceeding 30%
   **Solution:** Recognize that the hot theme bucket is a valid fourth category and apply it when at least 3 of the first 5 reporters in the theme close higher on day one
 - **Problem:** Holding through 50% of theme members close below their 20-day moving average within a 5-day period without re-evaluating a written note specifying the expected catalyst, price target, and stop-loss level
  **Solution:** Monitor relative strength (stock outperforms the sector ETF by 5% over the prior 10 days) across the theme; if at least 3 names start closes below the 10 EMA on volume exceeding 150% of the 20-day average, exit regardless of individual stock fundamentals
