# The highest revenue-generating RSI strategy used by our traders

## Overview

This Money Teaching explains SMB Capital's highest revenue-generating trading strategy: a mean reversion short that exploits overextended stocks and commodities using a customized RSI setup. The strategy focuses on identifying rapidly accelerating price runs where acceleration makes the move unsustainable, then shorting the stock after the rapidly accelerating price advance becomes unsustainable. Traders learn to configure RSI for extreme outlier detection rather than normal market conditions and confirm signals across multiple time frames before entering.

## When to Follow These Money Teachings

- When you need to short a stock or commodity that has gained more than 20% over the past month and is showing overbought signals on daily, intermediate, and short-term time frames
- When working with a mean reversion playbook rather than trend following
- When the user asks about the #1 RSI strategy used by professional traders to capture sharp, unsustainable price highs (blowoff tops)
- When looking for an A+ setup with four checks in favor aligned before entering a trade
- When filtering for outlier situations where price action has become extreme and unsustainable

## Steps

### Step 1: Configure RSI With Custom Settings

Change the default RSI parameters to detect extreme conditions rather than normal market activity. Set the length/period from 14 to 20, and change the thresholds from 70/30 to 80/20. This makes the signal harder to trigger and filters out noise. On a daily chart, the 20-period RSI covers approximately one calendar month of trading days, and aligns with other 20-period indicators you may already use.

### Step 2: Monitor Multiple Time Frames for Alignment

Track the RSI across at least three time frames: daily, intermediate (such as 130-minute), and short-term (such as 30-minute). The 20-period RSI on the daily chart covers approximately one month of trading days. The intermediate and shorter time frames show whether the rate of price increase is accelerating, with each bar printing a higher high than the previous bar over the last 4 periods. You want the RSI to light up in overbought territory on all three time frames simultaneously, confirming the rapidly accelerating nature of the move.

### Step 3: Identify the Outlier Setup and Acceleration

Look for a stock or commodity that is overextended on multiple time frames and experiencing acceleration, not just a steady grind higher. Use tools such as Keltner channels (2 ATRs above a 20-period moving average) to compare current behavior to blowoff tops such as those seen in silver and gold. A normal trend with linear price appreciation is less attractive than one where the slope increases. The move must display characteristics of an unsustainable advance: short covering rallies where short sellers are forced to buy back shares at rising prices, and long buyers entering with optimism despite the overextended price.

### Step 4: Wait for the A+ Setup With Checks in Favor

Do not short on the first sign of overbought RSI. Wait until four checks align. These include RSI reading above 80 on the 20-period setting across daily, intermediate, and short-term time frames, an accelerating price path with higher highs each of the last 4 bars, a gap up into the day, and at least three consecutive days with a close greater than the previous close. If any of the four checks are missing, the trade may not be a full A+ setup. Wait for the opening range breakdown or another confirming entry signal. Always mentally recalibrate the setup score to determine position size. Grade the trade as A+ if all checks align: RSI extreme on daily, intermediate, and short-term time frames; accelerating price path; gap up into the day; and at least three consecutive days with a close greater than the previous close. Grade the trade as B if three of four checks are present. Grade the trade as C if only two checks are present.

### Step 5: Use Opening Range Breakdown as Entry Trigger

If the market gaps up and is already extended across daily, intermediate, and short-term time frames, watch for the opening range to break down. That opening range breakdown is the preferred entry signal for this strategy. You can also trade intraday as the setup unfolds throughout the session. Time-Weighted Average Price (T-WaP) off the open or enter on the first 5-minute candle that closes below the opening range low, but cancel the trade if price reclaims the opening range high or the prior day high before your entry signal triggers.

### Step 6: Manage Risk and Exit Quickly

Do not overstay the trade. If the setup invalidates and price reclaims the prior day high or the opening range high, exit immediately. These blowoff trades can reverse quickly and sharply in the opposite direction. Size the position based on how many of the four key checks are present: RSI reading above 80 on the 20-period setting across daily, intermediate, and short-term time frames, an accelerating price path with higher highs each of the last 4 bars, a gap up into the day, and at least three consecutive days with a close greater than the previous close. If all four checks are present, take the trade. If only two or three checks are present, reduce your planned position size. Always calculate the risk-reward ratio mentally before and during the trade by comparing your entry price, stop-loss price, and target price to ensure the potential profit is at least twice the risk.

## Examples

### Example 1: Silver (SLV) Christmas Short

SLV broke out of a weekly base during Thanksgiving week. By Christmas week, the SMB Capital desk noticed acceleration on Friday with a green candle closing within the top 10% of the day's range. On the daily, 130-minute, and 30-minute charts, the RSI with 20-period and 80/20 settings all lit up pink. The gap up into Monday followed by a flush created the opening range breakdown, and the SMB Capital desk executed the short. This was a mean reversion trade, not a fundamental view on silver.

### Example 2: Gold (GLD) Overextension

GLD exhibited the same pattern as SLV. The daily chart was extended relative to its 20-period moving average, but the power of the multiple time frame signal was evident when all three time frames aligned at the high. Using default 14-period RSI with 70/30 thresholds would have produced early false signals. With the custom 20/80 settings, the signal only appeared at the high, giving a cleaner entry.

### Example 3: MicroStrategy (MSTR) Unsustainable High Reversal

MSTR showed the same pattern as SLV and GLD. Daily, intermediate, and short-term time frames became overbought at the high of the move. On the 30-minute chart, the RSI lit up at the high, providing a precise intraday short entry. Earlier 30-minute signals were ignored because the daily and 130-minute were not yet aligned. The setup was cataloged as a repeatable example of the playbook.

### Example 4: SMCI Rapid-Acceleration Short

SMCI engulfed the previous two trading days in a single candle. The SMB Capital desk did not hold calls overnight but entered the short on the opening range breakdown. The position size was large because the RSI was aligned across all time frames and the acceleration was obvious. The options used with zero Days to Expiration (DTE) went from one dollar to 13 to 30 in minutes, making it one of the largest individual trades on the desk at the time.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Use RSI with 20-period length and 80/20 thresholds for outlier detection
- ✅ Require RSI alignment across daily, intermediate, and short-term time frames before entering
- ✅ Treat indicators as data points and context filters, not standalone entry signals
- ✅ Size positions based on the setup score: increase position size for A+ setups (all four checks present), maintain position size for B setups (three checks present), and decrease position size for C setups (two checks present).
- ✅ Exit immediately if the trade invalidates and price reclaims the opening range high or the prior day high.
- ✅ Combine RSI with Keltner channels, gap analysis, and consecutive days up as additional checks
- ✅ Look for acceleration, not just a static overbought reading
- ✅ Trade as a mean reversion short with no fundamental view required

## Keep In Mind

- The RSI alone is not enough; it is one component of a multi-factor playbook
- Not all trades within the strategy are created equal; some are A+ setups and some are lower-probability trades
- The setup is rare, approximately one or two per quarter per trader, but accounts for a large portion of quarterly profit and loss.
- Commodities can extend to more extreme levels than stocks because of fundamental drivers including central bank buying, so do not assume a top based solely on RSI
- If you are taking this setup more than once a week, review your filters because the signals should be rare

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Shorting on default RSI settings (14-period, 70/30 thresholds) produces false signals on the daily chart in stocks in strong uptrends.
  **Solution:** Use 20-period RSI with 80/20 thresholds to capture only extreme outliers.
- **Problem:** Entering when only one of the three time frames (daily, intermediate, or short-term) shows overbought RSI, leading to front-side losses from entering before the setup aligns.
  **Solution:** Wait for RSI to light up across daily, intermediate, and short-term time frames before entering.
- **Problem:** Treating RSI as a standalone entry signal without confirming price action, gaps, or acceleration.
  **Solution:** Use RSI as a context filter and require the four key checks: RSI extreme across daily, intermediate, and short-term time frames, accelerating price path, gap up, and at least three consecutive days with a close greater than the previous close. Use Keltner channels as additional context.
- **Problem:** Overstaying the trade after it invalidates, hoping it will reverse.
  **Solution:** Exit immediately if price reclaims the opening range high or the setup no longer fits the criteria.
- **Problem:** Taking the trade before the four checks align because of fear of missing out (FOMO) during the acceleration phase.
  **Solution:** Follow the process and do not enter until the A+ conditions are met; reduced-size positions are acceptable but respect risk limits.
