# Why "A" Students Work For "C" Students - Robert Kiyosaki

## Overview

This Money Teaching explains how understanding the three parts of your brain—left brain, right brain, and subconscious mind—can help you overcome fear and greed to succeed financially. Robert Kiyosaki reveals why the school system rewards left-brain thinkers (A students) while C students can thrive in the real world by applying creative thinking and interpersonal skills. The core insight is that financial success requires aligning your analytical, creative, and instinctive thinking so that fear does not override logical decision-making.

## When to Follow These Money Teachings

- When you feel fear or panic about investing, starting a business, or taking financial risks
- When your academic record limits your perceived ability to build wealth
- When the economy is declining and many people are panicking but you want to see opportunity
- When you want to understand why highly educated people with stable jobs still struggle financially

## Steps

### Step 1: Understand the Three Parts of Your Brain

Know that your brain has three parts that control different functions:

- **Left brain** — analytical, logical, handles reading, writing, and math; the "school brain"
- **Right brain** — creative, spatial, handles art, music, and problem-solving through visualization
- **Subconscious (reptilian) brain** — primal, reactive, handles fear and greed; does not think, only reacts

Recognize that traditional education primarily rewards left-brain strengths, which is why A students can excel in classrooms but struggle in the real world.

### Step 2: Identify Your Dominant Intelligence

Assess which brain and intelligence type you rely on most:

- If your strongest skills are reading, writing, and math, you are likely left-brain dominant (typical A student).
- If your strongest skills are hands-on work, art, music, or creative problem-solving, you are likely right-brain dominant (typically a C student in traditional school).
- If you have strong intrapersonal and interpersonal communication skills, you have a natural advantage in business and investing.

Weakness in left-brain skills does not prevent financial success.

### Step 3: Recognize When Your Reptilian Brain Is in Control

Watch for the signs that your subconscious mind has taken over:

- You feel sudden fear, panic, or greed when faced with a financial decision.
- You hear internal thoughts like "What if I fail?" or "I might lose everything."
- You want to quit or run away instead of analyzing the opportunity logically.

When this happens, blood drains from the front of your brain to the reptilian brain region, and your ability to think clearly is impaired. This is the fight-or-flight response described in the transcript.

### Step 4: Align All Three Brains Before Making Financial Decisions

Before investing, starting a business, or making a major money move, confirm that all three parts of your brain support the decision:

- **Left brain** confirms the numbers, logic, and strategy make sense.
- **Right brain** visualizes the creative path and sees the bigger opportunity.
- **Subconscious mind** feels calm and does not trigger fear or greed.

If any part of your brain is in reactive mode, pause to acknowledge and manage that fear before proceeding.

### Step 5: Train Your Subconscious Mind to Support Wealth Building

Actively rewire your reptilian brain's default reactions:

- When you feel fear about money, acknowledge it, then consciously choose to act anyway.
- When you feel greed, recognize it as a signal to slow down and think, not to speed up.
- Repeat affirmations and actions that align with your financial goals so your subconscious accepts them as normal.

With consistent practice, this reduces panic responses and helps you make better decisions under pressure.

### Step 6: Use Your Right-Brain Strengths to Solve Financial Problems

When you face a financial challenge that your left brain cannot solve using analytical methods alone, switch to creative thinking:

- Visualize the deal or investment structure in your mind before calculating the numbers.
- Use spatial reasoning to see relationships and patterns that many people miss.
- Draw, sketch, or map out the problem to engage your creative side.

This is how C students can outperform A students in real-world business.

### Step 7: See Economic Decline as Opportunity, Not Threat

Train yourself to view a recession or market downturn as a buying opportunity rather than a time to panic:

- When markets crash and many people are fearful, assets become undervalued.
- Some people sell out of fear; you should think logically and buy.
- Remember that emotional reactions in many people create opportunity for you when you think clearly rather than react emotionally.

### Step 8: Build Your Financial IQ by Developing All Three Brains

Financial IQ is not measured by academic grades alone. Develop all three areas:

- Left brain: Learn accounting, math, and financial statements.
- Right brain: Practice creative deal structuring and visualization.
- Subconscious: Master your emotions so fear and greed do not sabotage your decisions.

A high financial IQ requires all three brains working in harmony.

## Examples

### Example 1: Buying Real Estate in a Recession

The economy is experiencing a downturn and many people are panicking about real estate values. You feel fear but use the three-brain method: your left brain analyzes the numbers and sees the property is undervalued, your right brain visualizes the cash flow potential, and your subconscious mind stays calm because you have practiced handling fear. You buy while many people sell.

### Example 2: Starting a Business With No Savings

You want to leave your job and start a business but your reptilian brain triggers fear and asks "What if I fail?" Use the three-brain method: confirm the business model makes logical sense, visualize the creative path to customers, and calm your fear through repeated small actions. When all three brains support the decision, you take action despite the fear.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Learn to identify when your reptilian brain has taken over by recognizing fear and greed responses
- ✅ Practice calming techniques so you can return to analytical thinking quickly
- ✅ Use creative visualization and drawing to solve problems your analytical mind cannot solve
- ✅ Study financial statements and numbers to strengthen your left-brain financial skills
- ✅ View declining markets as buying opportunities when you think clearly rather than react emotionally
- ✅ Accept that being a poor student in traditional school does not limit your financial potential

## Keep In Mind

- The school system was designed in 1983 to produce traditional employees, not entrepreneurs.
- An A student does not guarantee financial success, and a C student does not guarantee financial failure.
- Your most important personal skill for entrepreneurship is managing your own fear and greed.

## Security & Safety Notes

- Do not dismiss your reptilian brain's warnings entirely—fear exists for survival. The goal is to manage it, not eliminate it.
- Verify financial opportunities with logic and professional advice before acting.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Letting fear or greed override logical analysis during a market downturn
  **Solution:** Use the three-brain alignment method before making any financial decision
- **Problem:** Believing low grades mean you cannot succeed financially
  **Solution:** Apply your creative and interpersonal strengths in business
- **Problem:** Trying to solve every financial problem with left-brain analysis alone
  **Solution:** Engage your right brain using visualization, drawing, and creative problem-solving
- **Problem:** Ignoring the subconscious mind and pretending emotions do not affect decisions
  **Solution:** Train your reptilian brain through repeated exposure and small actions to reduce fear responses
