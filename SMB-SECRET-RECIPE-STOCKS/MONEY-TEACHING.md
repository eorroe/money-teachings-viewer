# SMB Secret Recipe to Finding Stocks Before They Make Moves

## Overview

This teaching reveals SMB Capital's five grading checks plus intraday confirmation for identifying catalyst (earnings) plays before stocks make directional moves. It walks through how to use pre-market volume scanners, check higher time frame technicals, analyze earnings reports for inflection quarters, map stocks to market themes, assess market environment, and confirm trades with intraday price action.

## When to Follow These Money Teachings

- When you need to identify earnings/catalyst plays before they gap up
- When working with pre-market scanners to find stocks with significant pre-market volume
- When the user asks about grading a catalyst setup or finding stocks before they make moves

## Steps

### Step 1: Scan for Pre-Market Volume Using the Relative Volume Scanner

Use a pre-market extended hours relative volume scanner (percentage of average daily volume, or ADV: the average number of shares traded per day over the past 20 trading days) to identify stocks with volume above 20% of ADV before the regular session market open (09:30 ET). Stocks doing less than 20% of ADV during pre-market extended hours (4:00 AM – 9:30 AM ET for U.S. equities) should generally be filtered out from earnings-play analysis. Stocks doing 20–50% of ADV should prompt a review of headline numbers and the daily chart. Stocks doing at least 80% of ADV should prompt a review of the earnings report categories and weekly chart. Stocks doing at least 200% of ADV warrant immediate analysis of earnings report categories, theme alignment, and intraday setup preparation. Stocks doing at least 200% of ADV tend to show higher relative volume than stocks below 200% of ADV, making them more likely to exhibit significant intraday price movement.

### Step 2: Analyze the Earnings Report for Inflection Acceleration

Look at the earnings per share (EPS) exceeding analyst estimates, revenue exceeding analyst estimates, and future-period guidance issued above prior consensus estimates, but continue beyond headline metrics to classify the earnings quarter. Review the last five quarters of earnings reports to classify the report. The key categories are (use this decision tree in order): (1) First Clean Profitable Beat Quarter (excluding one-time gains or accounting adjustments) — the first time within the reviewed five-quarter window that the company generates positive GAAP net income; (2) Acceleration Inflection Quarter — an acceleration of at least 5 percentage points in year-over-year revenue growth (measured sequentially quarter-over-quarter) such as the acceleration from 12% year-over-year in Q3 2024 to 23% year-over-year in Q3 2025 (Fastly, following a first clean profitable beat quarter); (3) Continued Beat Quarter — earnings beats for exactly the two immediately preceding quarters without a new acceleration in year-over-year revenue growth. The acceleration inflection quarter is the primary target setup.

### Step 3: Check Higher Time Frame Technicals

Review the weekly and daily charts to confirm there is no resistance level within 10% above the current price. A catalyst event requires the stock to be closing above the consolidation high (the highest high of the prior consolidation pattern on the daily chart) by at least 3% with volume at least 100% above the average 2-minute bar volume.

### Step 4: Understand the Theme and Business Driver Behind the Beat

Dig into the conference call and report details to answer: why did the company beat? What is the actual business driver? Map the stock to a market theme taxonomy based on its primary revenue driver, customer segment, or supply chain position to see if it belongs to a sector that is experiencing price appreciation of at least 5% relative to the SSexperiencing positive price momentum over the prior two weeksP 500P 500 over the prior 10 trading days or one that is experiencing negative price momentum. For example, Fastly was misidentified as software, but it is actually cloud infrastructure/digital infrastructure. Knowing the correct theme helps assess whether the beat aligns with current market conditions.

### Step 5: Assess Market Environment as a Final Overlay

Check how other stocks that reported earnings in the prior five trading sessions and gapped at least 5% in the same GICS sector have been trading over the prior two weeks. The market environment can lower the grade by one level (e.g., from A+ to A) if more than 30% of earnings-related stocks in the sector have declined by more than 5% from their pre-market highs over the prior two weeks. If stocks that reported earnings in the prior five trading sessions are gapping down by at least 3% below the prior regular-session close and closing below their most recent earnings announcement pre-market high, use 50% of the standard A+ position size for A setups or avoid the trade. Always complete Steps 1 through 5 before the regular session market open (09:30 ET) so you know what to watch at the open.

### Step 6: Confirm with Intraday Price Action

Wait for intraday price action to confirm whether your pre-market analysis is correct. The confirmation signals are: a break at least 0.5% above the current trading day's pre-market high on the first two-minute bar of the regular session (09:30–09:32 ET), volume at least 100% above the average 2-minute bar volume on that bar, and the stock holding above VWAP (Volume-Weighted Average Price) with no 2-minute bar closing below VWAP for at least five consecutive 2-minute bars. If you get a first two-minute bar (09:30–09:32 ET) with volume at least 200% of the average 2-minute bar volume that breaks above the pre-market high, the bar closes at its high, and the stock does not drop back below the pre-market high, continued upward movement is the expected outcome when all confirmation signals are present. Accumulate on dips to within 1% above VWAP for swing positions or take day-trade profits on the opening upward price move for day trades.

## Examples

### Example 1: Fastly (FSLY) - A+ Catalyst Play Adjusted to A

Pre-market volume reached 200% of ADV, flagging it for review. The earnings report showed an actual EPS of 12 cents versus an estimate of 6 cents, revenue of 173 million versus an estimate of 161 million, and guidance above prior estimates for the next quarter and fiscal year. This was the first clean profitable beat quarter (Q3 2025). Revenue growth accelerated from 2% to 8% to 12% to 15% year-over-year in prior quarters, then to 23% year-over-year in the acceleration inflection quarter, an 11 percentage-point increase from the prior quarter. Theme analysis revealed Fastly is cloud infrastructure (edge computing), not software, placing it in a sector that was experiencing price appreciation of at least 5% relative to the SSexperiencing positive price momentum over the prior two weeksP 500P 500 over the prior 10 trading days. Higher time frame technicals showed a breakout from a consolidation pattern. Market environment was mixed: earnings-related stocks in the sector were gapping down and closing below their pre-market highs, so the setup was adjusted from A+ to A. Intraday, the stock broke its pre-market high on the first two-minute bar with volume reaching at least 30 times ADV (an exceptional outlier beyond the standard 2× confirmation threshold), gained 90% from the prior close, including the gap, on day one, and held above VWAP, confirming the pre-market analysis and offering swing and day-trade opportunities.

### Example 2: Pre-Market Scanner Workflow

On the trading day when earnings are reported before the market opens, run the relative volume scanner in pre-market. Filter for stocks doing at least 20% of ADV. Identify the stocks with the highest relative volume (at or above 200% of ADV). Review their headline numbers, then prioritize the ones with at least 50% volume for initial review of headline numbers and the daily chart, with deeper analysis of earnings report categories for those above 80%. This narrowing process helps you focus on setups with volume at least 50% of ADV rather than wasting time on stocks with volume below 50% of ADV.

## Best Practices

- Use the pre-market extended hours relative volume scanner to scan for volume from institutional traders before the regular session market open (09:30 ET)
- Look for stocks doing at least 20% of their ADV in extended hours (pre-market and regular after-hours)
- Review five quarters of earnings reports to classify reports and identify inflection acceleration
- Verify the theme and business driver behind the earnings beat and confirm it aligns with market themes that are experiencing price appreciation of at least 5% relative to the SSexperiencing positive price momentum over the prior two weeksP 500P 500 over the prior 10 trading days
- Wait for price action confirmation (break at least 0.5% above pre-market high, first two-minute bar with volume at least 100% above the average 2-minute bar volume, and holding above VWAP with no 2-minute bar closing below VWAP for at least five consecutive 2-minute bars) before entering
- Complete all analysis before the regular session market open (09:30 ET) so you know what you are watching
- Do not just read headlines and earnings numbers without deeper analysis into quarter category
- Do not ignore market environment even if the catalyst is strong
- Do not misclassify stocks into the wrong theme category without checking the conference call
- Do not chase without volume at least 100% above ADV and a break at least 0.5% above the current trading day's pre-market high on the first two-minute bar of the regular session (09:30–09:32 ET) at the open

## Keep In Mind

- Stocks doing less than 20% of ADV during pre-market extended hours should generally be filtered out from earnings-play analysis
- The acceleration inflection quarter is the target setup; use this decision tree: (1) Is this the first profitable quarter in the 5-quarter window? If yes → First Clean Profitable Beat Quarter. (2) If no, does it have ≥5 pp YoY revenue acceleration? If yes → Acceleration Inflection Quarter. (3) Otherwise → Continued Beat Quarter (excluding one-time gains or accounting adjustments)
- A stock can be downgraded from A+ to A based on market environment, where A+ means all five grading checks are positive; A means exactly one check is negative; A- means exactly two checks are negative; C means three or more checks are negative or the setup should be avoided
- High short interest (measured as short interest as a percentage of float; such as the 12–13% observed with Fastly) can coincide with stronger day-one moves, but is not required for a successful trade
- Pre-market work provides the primary inputs for the trade decision; intraday action confirms or invalidates the pre-market analysis

## Security & Safety Notes

- Always use 50% of the standard A+ position size for A setups
- Do not allocate more than 5% of your total account equity to a single position until the stock breaks at least 0.5% above the pre-market high on the first two-minute bar with volume at least 100% above ADV
- Be aware that even A+ setups can fail; the market's actual price action determines whether the setup succeeds or fails
- The Fastly earnings play was executed as a swing trade
- Never enter a position without knowing your exit plan before the regular session market open (09:30 ET)

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Trading based only on headline earnings numbers without understanding the earnings quarter category
  **Solution:** Review five quarters of earnings reports and classify each report as first clean profitable beat (excluding one-time gains or accounting adjustments), acceleration inflection, or continued beat quarter before deciding to trade
- **Problem:** Misidentifying a stock's theme (e.g., calling Fastly software when it is cloud infrastructure)
  **Solution:** Use a system for tracking market themes (such as SMB Capital's internal theme taxonomy spreadsheet) and dig into the CEO's conference call to understand the actual business driver and correct market category
- **Problem:** Ignoring market environment and taking A+ setups when earnings-related stocks in the sector are gapping down and closing below their pre-market highs
  **Solution:** Check how other earnings-related stocks and breakouts in the sector have been trading over the prior two weeks; lower the grade if the environment is unfavorable because earnings-related stocks are gapping down and closing below their pre-market highs
- **Problem:** Chasing without pre-market preparation
  **Solution:** Complete Steps 1 through 5 before the regular session market open (09:30 ET) so you know exactly what to watch and at what level to enter
- **Problem:** Not understanding volume significance
  **Solution:** Use the relative volume scanner; stocks doing at least 200% of ADV in pre-market extended hours tend to show higher relative volume than stocks below 200% of ADV, making them more likely to exhibit significant intraday price movement, and any stock doing less than 20% of ADV in pre-market extended hours should be filtered out
