# SMB Secret Recipe to Finding Stocks Before They Go Parabolic

## Overview

This teaching reveals SMB Capital's five-check grading system for identifying high-probability catalyst (earnings) plays before stocks make parabolic moves. It walks through how to use pre-market volume scanners, analyze earnings reports for inflection quarters, map stocks to market themes, assess market environment, and confirm trades with intraday price action.

## When to Follow These Money Teachings

- When you need to identify high-probability earnings/catalyst plays before they gap up
- When working with pre-market scanners to find institutional volume
- When the user asks about grading a catalyst setup or finding stocks before parabolic moves

## Steps

### Step 1: Scan for Pre-Market Volume Using the Percent AVAL Scanner

Use a pre-market extended hours percent AVAL (average volume) scanner to identify names with abnormal volume before the market opens. Stocks doing less than 20% of their average daily volume in pre-market or after-hours are generally not worth analyzing for earnings plays. Look for 20-50% as interesting, 80% as significant, and 200%+ as exceptional. The stocks with the highest extended-hours volume typically become the most "in play" intraday.

### Step 2: Analyze the Earnings Report for Inflection Acceleration

Look at the headline numbers (EPS beat, revenue beat, guidance raise) but do not stop there. Build a story of the last five quarters to classify the report. The key categories are: (1) First Clean Profitable Beat Quarter - the first time the company turns profitable; (2) Acceleration Inflection Quarter - a huge jump in revenue growth year-over-year after profitability has been established; (3) Crushing It Quarter - continued strong beats but without the same rate of acceleration. The acceleration inflection quarter is the "bread and butter" setup.

### Step 3: Check Higher Time Frame Technicals

Review the weekly and daily charts to confirm there is room to run. Look for base breakouts, resistance levels, and whether the stock can actually move. A strong catalyst means little if the chart is extended or facing major resistance. Confirm the stock is breaking out of a base or weekly consolidation area.

### Step 4: Understand the Theme and Story Behind the Beat

Dig into the conference call and report details to answer: why did they beat? What is the actual business driver? Map the stock to your market theme taxonomy to see if it belongs to a hot sector or one being punished. For example, Fastly was widely misidentified as software but is actually cloud infrastructure/digital infrastructure. Knowing the correct theme determines whether the beat aligns with the current market narrative.

### Step 5: Assess Market Environment as a Final Overlay

Check how other earnings plays and breakouts have been trading. Are gap ups failing? Are breakouts generally working? The market environment can downgrade an otherwise A+ setup. If earnings names and breakouts are struggling in your sector, factor that into position sizing and risk. Always complete these five checks before the market opens so you know exactly what to watch at the open.

### Step 6: Confirm with Intraday Price Action

Wait for the market to confirm your thesis. The key confirmation signals are: a break above the pre-market high on the first two-minute bar, strong volume on that bar, and the stock holding above VWAP. If you get a "power ignition candle" right off the open with massive volume and no immediate fade, the probability of a runner is much higher. Accumulate on dips back to VWAP for swing positions or scalp the initial drive for day trades.

## Examples

### Example 1: Fastly (FSLY) - A+ Catalyst Play Downgraded to A

Pre-market volume reached 200% of average daily volume, immediately putting it on the radar. The earnings report showed an EPS beat of 12 cents vs 6 cents, revenue of 173 million vs 161 million, and upside guidance for the next quarter and fiscal year. This was the first clean profitable beat quarter (Q3 2025). Revenue growth had accelerated from 2% to 8% to 12% to 15% to 23% year-over-year, representing a 50% rate of change - the acceleration inflection quarter. Theme analysis revealed Fastly is cloud infrastructure (edge computing), not software, placing it in a sector rewarded by the current market. Higher time frame technicals showed a breakout from a weekly base. Market environment was mixed: earnings plays and breakouts had been struggling in tech, so the setup was downgraded from A+ to A. Intraday, the stock broke its pre-market high on the first two-minute bar with massive volume, ran roughly 90% on day one including the gap, and held above VWAP, confirming the thesis and offering swing and day-trade opportunities.

### Example 2: Pre-Market Scanner Workflow

On an earnings morning with hundreds of names reporting, run the percent AVAL scanner in pre-market. Filter for names doing at least 20% of average daily volume. Identify the top 5-10 names with the highest relative volume. Review their headline beats quickly, then prioritize the ones with 50%+ volume for deeper analysis. This narrowing process helps you focus on "overwhelmingly clear" setups rather than wasting time on marginal reports.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Use the pre-market extended hours percent AVAL scanner to hunt for institutional volume before the market opens
- ✅ Look for stocks doing at least 20% of their average daily volume in pre-market or after-hours
- ✅ Build a story of the last five quarters to classify earnings reports and identify inflection acceleration
- ✅ Verify the theme and story behind the earnings beat and confirm it aligns with current hot market themes
- ✅ Wait for price action confirmation (break of pre-market high, strong first two-minute bar) before entering
- ✅ Complete all analysis before the market opens so you know exactly what you are watching
- ❌ Don't just read headlines and earnings numbers without deeper analysis into quarter category
- ❌ Don't ignore market environment even if the catalyst is strong
- ❌ Don't misclassify stocks into the wrong theme category without checking the conference call
- ❌ Don't chase without volume and price confirmation at the open

## Keep In Mind

- Stocks doing less than 20% of average daily volume in extended hours are generally not worth analyzing for earnings plays
- The acceleration inflection quarter is the "bread and butter" and follows a first clean profitable beat quarter
- A stock can be downgraded from A+ to A based on market environment even with a perfect catalyst
- High short interest (12-13%) can amplify day-one moves but is not required
- The pre-market work determines 90% of the trade decision; intraday action confirms or denies your thesis

## Security & Safety Notes

- Always use stop losses and control position size based on the setup grade (A+ gets full size, A gets reduced size)
- Do not allocate full position size until price action confirms your thesis at the open
- Be aware that even A+ setups can fail; the market always has final say
- Earnings plays are swing trades requiring wider stops and more patience than pure day trades
- Never enter a position without knowing your exit plan before the bell rings

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Trading based only on headline beat/raise without understanding the earnings quarter category
  **Solution:** Build a five-quarter story and classify each report as first clean profitable beat, acceleration inflection, or crushing it quarter before deciding to trade
- **Problem:** Misidentifying a stock's theme (e.g., calling Fastly software when it is cloud infrastructure)
  **Solution:** Use a theme tracker or database and dig into the CEO's conference call to understand the actual business driver and correct market category
- **Problem:** Ignoring market environment and taking A+ setups in a hostile market
  **Solution:** Check how other earnings names and breakouts are trading; downgrade grade and reduce size if the environment is unfavorable
- **Problem:** Chasing without pre-market preparation
  **Solution:** Complete all five checks before the market opens so you know exactly what to watch and at what level to enter
- **Problem:** Not understanding volume significance
  **Solution:** Use the percent AVAL scanner; extended hours volume correlates strongly with intraday "in play" status, and anything under 20% should be filtered out
