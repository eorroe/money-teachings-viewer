# SMB Secret Recipe to Finding Stocks Before They Make Moves

## Overview

This teaching reveals SMB Capital's five grading checks plus intraday confirmation for identifying catalyst (earnings) plays before stocks make directional moves. It walks through how to use pre-market volume scanners, check higher time frame technicals, analyze earnings reports for inflection quarters, map stocks to market themes, assess market environment, and confirm trades with intraday price action.

## When to Follow These Money Teachings

- When you need to identify earnings/catalyst plays before they gap up
- When working with pre-market scanners to find stocks in play
- When the user asks about grading a catalyst setup or finding stocks before they make moves

## Steps

### Step 1: Scan for Pre-Market Volume Using the Percent AVAL Scanner

Use a pre-market extended hours percent AVAL (average volume) scanner to identify names with volume above 20% of average daily volume before the market opens. Stocks doing less than 20% of average daily volume in pre-market or after-hours are not worth analyzing for earnings plays. Names doing 20-50% of average daily volume warrant screening headline numbers and the daily chart. Names doing 80% warrant reviewing the earnings report categories and weekly chart. Names doing 200% or more warrant immediate analysis of earnings report categories, theme alignment, and intraday setup preparation. Names doing 200% or more of average daily volume show strong correlation with high intraday volume.

### Step 2: Analyze the Earnings Report for Inflection Acceleration

Look at the earnings per share (EPS) beat, revenue beat, and guidance raise but do not stop there. Review the last five quarters of earnings reports to classify the report. The key categories are: (1) First Clean Profitable Beat Quarter - the first time the company turns profitable; (2) Acceleration Inflection Quarter - a jump in year-over-year revenue growth after a first clean profitable beat quarter; (3) Continued Beat Quarter - ongoing earnings beats without a new jump in year-over-year revenue growth. The acceleration inflection quarter is the primary target setup.

### Step 3: Check Higher Time Frame Technicals

Review the weekly and daily charts to confirm there is room on the chart for the stock to run. A catalyst event has limited trading value if the stock is not breaking out. Confirm the stock is breaking out of a consolidation pattern.

### Step 4: Understand the Theme and Business Driver Behind the Beat

Dig into the conference call and report details to answer: why did they beat? What is the actual business driver? Map the stock to a market theme taxonomy (such as a theme tracker or database) to see if it belongs to a sector that is currently being rewarded by the market or one that is being punished. For example, Fastly was misidentified as software, but it is actually cloud infrastructure/digital infrastructure. Knowing the correct theme helps assess whether the beat aligns with the way the market is acting right now.

### Step 5: Assess Market Environment as a Final Overlay

Check how other earnings plays and breakouts have been trading. The market environment can adjust the grade of an otherwise A+ setup. If earnings names and breakouts are failing in the stock's sector, reduce position size or avoid the trade. Always complete Steps 1 through 5 before the market opens so you know what to watch at the open.

### Step 6: Confirm with Intraday Price Action

Wait for intraday price action to confirm whether your pre-market analysis is correct. The confirmation signals are: a break above the pre-market high on the first two-minute bar, volume above average daily volume on that bar, and the stock holding above VWAP (Volume-Weighted Average Price). If you get a large first two-minute bar right off the open with volume above average daily volume that breaks above the pre-market high, the bar closes near its high, and the stock does not drop back below the pre-market high, the probability of continued upward movement is higher. Accumulate on dips to the VWAP area for swing positions or take day-trade profits on the opening advance for day trades.

## Examples

### Example 1: Fastly (FSLY) - A+ Catalyst Play Adjusted to A

Pre-market volume reached 200% of average daily volume, immediately flagging it for review. The earnings report showed an actual EPS of 12 cents versus an estimate of 6 cents, revenue of 173 million versus an estimate of 161 million, and guidance above prior estimates for the next quarter and fiscal year. This was the first clean profitable beat quarter (Q3 2025). Revenue growth accelerated from 2% to 8% to 12% to 15% year-over-year in prior quarters, then to 23% year-over-year in the acceleration inflection quarter, a 50% rate of change from the prior quarter. Theme analysis revealed Fastly is cloud infrastructure (edge computing), not software, placing it in a sector that was being rewarded by the market during that period. Higher time frame technicals showed a breakout from a consolidation pattern. Market environment was mixed: earnings plays and breakouts were failing in tech, so the setup was adjusted from A+ to A. Intraday, the stock broke its pre-market high on the first two-minute bar with volume reaching 30 to 40 times the average daily volume, ran 90% on day one including the gap, and held above VWAP, confirming the pre-market analysis and offering swing and day-trade opportunities.

### Example 2: Pre-Market Scanner Workflow

On an earnings morning with names reporting, run the percent AVAL scanner in pre-market. Filter for names doing at least 20% of average daily volume. Identify the highest relative volume names. Review their headline numbers, then prioritize the ones with at least 50% volume for analysis of earnings report categories. This narrowing process helps you focus on setups with volume at or above 50% of average daily volume rather than wasting time on reports with volume below 50% of average daily volume.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Use the pre-market extended hours percent AVAL scanner to hunt for institutional volume before the market opens
- ✅ Look for stocks doing at least 20% of their average daily volume in pre-market or after-hours
- ✅ Review five quarters of earnings reports to classify reports and identify inflection acceleration
- ✅ Verify the theme and business driver behind the earnings beat and confirm it aligns with market themes that are currently being rewarded by the market
- ✅ Wait for price action confirmation (break of pre-market high, first two-minute bar with volume above average daily volume) before entering
- ✅ Complete all analysis before the market opens so you know what you are watching
- ❌ Don't just read headlines and earnings numbers without deeper analysis into quarter category
- ❌ Don't ignore market environment even if the catalyst is strong
- ❌ Don't misclassify stocks into the wrong theme category without checking the conference call
- ❌ Don't chase without volume above average daily volume and a break above the pre-market high on the first two-minute bar at the open

## Keep In Mind

- Stocks doing less than 20% of average daily volume in extended hours should not be analyzed for earnings plays
- The acceleration inflection quarter is the target setup and typically follows a first clean profitable beat quarter
- A stock can be adjusted from A+ to A based on market environment
- High short interest (such as the 12-13% observed with Fastly) can amplify day-one moves but is not required
- Pre-market work provides the primary inputs for the trade decision; intraday action confirms or invalidates the pre-market analysis

## Security & Safety Notes

- Always use stop losses and control position size based on the setup grade (A+ or A)
- Do not allocate the planned position size until the stock breaks above the pre-market high on the first two-minute bar with volume above average daily volume
- Be aware that even A+ setups can fail; the market's actual price action determines whether the setup succeeds or fails
- This earnings play was executed as a swing trade.
- Never enter a position without knowing your exit plan before the bell rings

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Trading based only on headline earnings numbers without understanding the earnings quarter category
  **Solution:** Review five quarters of earnings reports and classify each report as first clean profitable beat, acceleration inflection, or continued beat quarter before deciding to trade
- **Problem:** Misidentifying a stock's theme (e.g., calling Fastly software when it is cloud infrastructure)
  **Solution:** Use a system for tracking market themes and dig into the CEO's conference call to understand the actual business driver and correct market category
- **Problem:** Ignoring market environment and taking A+ setups when earnings names and breakouts are failing
  **Solution:** Check how other earnings names and breakouts are trading; adjust the grade if the environment is unfavorable because earnings names and breakouts are showing a high failure rate
- **Problem:** Chasing without pre-market preparation
  **Solution:** Complete Steps 1 through 5 before the market opens so you know exactly what to watch and at what level to enter
- **Problem:** Not understanding volume significance
  **Solution:** Use the percent AVAL scanner; extended hours volume at or above 200% of average daily volume shows strong correlation with high intraday volume, and any stock doing less than 20% of average daily volume in extended hours should be filtered out
