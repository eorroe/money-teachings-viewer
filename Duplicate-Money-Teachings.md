## Summary of Findings

All overlaps are with the two Dan Martell files, the Silicon Valley Girl AI skills file, and a small overlap with Morgan Housel's teachings. ## Additional Notes

- **High-duplication risk (Groups 1 and 5):** The "pre-sell before building" and "manual outreach before automating" teachings appear in both Dan Martell files with nearly identical phrasing. These two files may have been generated from the same source material. Consider merging them or clearly differentiating the DM-Make-Money-WITH-AI file (which adds the 9-step sales system) from the DM-Start-1-Person-Business-AI file (which adds the 3-question framework).

- **Medium-duplication risk (Groups 2, 3, 7):** The "start with the problem," "painkiller vs. vitamin," and "unfair advantage" teachings overlap conceptually with the SVG-AI-SKILLS file but with different framing (skills/career vs. business validation). These are related but not direct duplicates.

- **Low-duplication risk (Groups 4, 6):** The AI red-teaming teaching is unique to the new file. The house-as-liability teaching is referenced broadly across the collection but is only central to Morgan Housel's and Jaspreet Singh's files.

- **No action required** for biblical sermon files, Napoleon Hill files, Jim Rohn files, TDOAC-Patricia-Bright, TDOAC-John-Caudwell, TDOAC-Raoul-Pal, TDOAC-Barbara-Corcoran, TDOAC-Stephen-Bartlett, TDOAC-Cathie-Wood, TDOAC-Jeremy-Grantham, TDOAC-The-Psychology-of-Money, and TDOAC-Early-Retirement-Expert files — none contain teachings substantially overlapping with the new file.
