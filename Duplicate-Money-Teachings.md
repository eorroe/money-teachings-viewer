# Duplicate Money Teachings Analysis

## Summary

After analyzing all 40 `.md` files in the workspace that follow the money-teaching-template.md structure, **no exact or near-exact duplicate money teachings were found**. All files contain unique content with distinct steps, examples, and emphases.

However, several thematic clusters of related teachings were identified where multiple files cover similar topics from the same source or perspective. These are listed below for your review.
