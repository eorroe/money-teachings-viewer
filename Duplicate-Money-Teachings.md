# Duplicate Money Teachings Analysis

## Summary

After reading all 47 money teaching files, the following groups of files were identified as covering **similar or duplicate core concepts, principles, or advice**. Files that share a core source (e.g., multiple adaptations of the same book or sermon series) are grouped together.

---

## Group 1: Jim Rohn — Personal Development as the Foundation of Wealth

| File | Core Overlap | Key Differences |
|------|-------------|-----------------|
| `Jim Rohn/JRRS-IF-TRULY-WANT-WEALTHY-SUCCESSFUL/If You Truly Want to be Wealthy & Successful.md` | Both teach that wealth comes from personal development, not external circumstances; success is attracted by who you become; take full responsibility for your situation. | Uses the **seasons metaphor** (winter/spring/summer/harvest) with 8 detailed steps. Emphasizes handling winters, seizing spring opportunities, and reaping harvest without complaint. |
| `Jim Rohn/JR-GrindBuddy-BE-WEALTHY-HAPPY/If You Want to Be Wealthy & Happy.md` | Same core message: income rarely exceeds personal development; success is attracted not pursued; work on yourself first. | Uses a **3-step framework** (Find Out How Things Work / Go to Work / Don't Try to Beat the System). Adds practical methods for acquiring ideas (read, listen, observe successful people, keep a journal). Includes the "million-dollar windfall" example showing income reverts without personal growth. |

## Notes

- **No exact duplicates** were found — each file has unique examples, structure, or author-specific framing.
- The highest degree of overlap exists in **Group 2** (Think And Grow Rich variants) and **Group 3** (Charles Stanley sermons), where multiple files are adaptations of the same source material.
- **Group 6** is notable because one file (David Bach) takes the *opposite* position on homeownership from the other three files in the group.
- Several files share the theme of "don't buy a house as an investment" without being grouped here because their primary focus is broader (e.g., Ramit Sethi's file covers 10 money rules, not just housing).
