## Group 1: Napoleon Hill "Think And Grow Rich" — Near-Duplicates

| File | Title | Lines |
|------|-------|-------|
| 1 | JeremyWarren-Think-And-Grow-Rich | 109 |
| 2 | MKS-Think-And-Grow-Rich | 145 |
| 3 | CBC-THINK-AND-GROW-RICH | 132 |

### Similarities
- All three cover **Napoleon Hill's "Think And Grow Rich"** principles
- Same core structure: Overview → When to Follow → Steps → Examples → Best Practices → Keep In Mind → Security & Safety Notes → Common Pitfalls
- **Identical Examples**: All three feature Edwin C. Barnes/Thomas Edison, Henry Ford, and Charles M. Schwab/U.S. Steel Corporation with nearly identical text
- **Overlapping Steps**: Fix a definite amount of money, determine what you will give in return, establish a deadline, create a plan, write a statement, read aloud twice daily, auto-suggestion, faith, mastermind group, imagination, decisions, persistence
- **Shared Bible verses and principles**: Auto-suggestion, subconscious mind, mastermind principle, applied faith

### Differences

| Aspect | File 1 (JeremyWarren) | File 2 (MKS) | File 3 (CBC) |
|--------|----------------------|--------------|--------------|
| **Steps Count** | 8 steps | 16 steps | 11 steps |
| **Step 1 Focus** | Pocket notebook + major desired purpose | Fix exact amount of money | Fix exact amount of money |
| **Step Structure** | Simpler, more narrative | More detailed with sub-points | Detailed with auto-suggestion emphasis |
| **Examples** | Kate Smith/Ted Collins, Henry Ford/Wife | Edwin Barnes, Charles Schwab, Henry Ford | Edwin Barnes, Henry Ford/Model T, Charles Schwab |
| **Best Practices** | 10 bullet points | 11 bullet points | 13 bullet points |
| **Keep In Mind** | 7 bullet points | 7 bullet points | 10 bullet points |
| **Common Pitfalls** | 9 problem/solution pairs | 6 problem/solution pairs | 7 problem/solution pairs |
| **Unique Content** | Pocket notebook focus, going the extra mile (7-day exercise), pleasing personality, self-discipline | Sex energy transmutation, sixth sense, specialized knowledge | Self-confidence formula, six basic fears, imagination workshop |
| **Title** | "Master the Mind Power Principles" | "13 Principles to Accumulate Wealth" | Generic "Think And Grow Rich" |

**Verdict**: These are **near-duplicates** covering the same source material with different editorial choices. They could be consolidated or kept as separate perspectives on the same core content.
