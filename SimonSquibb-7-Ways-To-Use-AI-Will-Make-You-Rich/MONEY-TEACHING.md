# These 7 Ways To Use AI Will Make You Rich In 2026

## Overview

This money teaching distills seven actionable AI strategies from entrepreneur Dan Murray, who scaled Heights to $30 million and now uses AI to compress years of business work into days. Each teaching shows founders how to validate demand, choose channels, build audiences, design products, define company values, and iterate messaging faster than competitors. Following these steps helps you start, grow, and scale a business using AI as a force multiplier instead of replacing the human insight behind it.

## When to Follow These Money Teachings

- When you need to validate a new business idea or product before investing time and money
- When you are choosing distribution channels, building an audience, or scaling marketing output
- When the user asks how to use AI to make money, start a business, or stay ahead of competition in 2026

## Steps

### Step 1: Start With Why and Validate Demand Using AI

Your "why" is not something AI replaces, but AI can dramatically speed up the market research that confirms whether people actually want what you plan to build. Open Gemini or ChatGPT and use a research-oriented prompt that asks for buyer counts, search volumes by region, and trend data. Where this used to take weeks, you can now get answers in minutes.

### Step 2: Validate the What With AI-Generated Customer Personas

Once you know who you want to target, do not spend months on manual interviews. Prompt an AI tool to take on the persona of hundreds of specific users. Specify demographics, behaviors, and pain points, then ask the model to generate generalized data insights about how those groups think and respond. This compresses hundreds of interviews into minutes while still giving you directional accuracy.

### Step 3: Choose Your Where Using AI Channel Analysis

With so many channels available, founders often spread too thinly. Use AI to evaluate where to win first. Prompt for a breakdown of channels suited to your product and customer insight, then ask for rough startup costs, opportunities, and challenges for each. Use that output to pick one primary channel for year one rather than trying to be everywhere at once.

### Step 4: Market With AI Content and Ad Systems

Marketing is where many founders hesitate, but AI removes most execution barriers. Build an email list before launch and nurture it with content so you own the customer relationship. For social, shoot with a phone, use tools like Opus Clip for editing, and prompt AI to generate hooks, scripts, and multiple ad variations. Generate 10 to 20 versions of a concept with different starts, soundtracks, and endings so you can test what resonates at scale.

### Step 5: Use AI to Elevate Product Design and Customer Experience

Packaging and design are not afterthoughts. Study category leaders, then use AI design tools to generate unique packaging and product designs that stand out. Prompt for multiple design directions and, just as importantly, prompt for ideas on how to deliver a better end-to-end customer experience than the top 10 brands in your space.

### Step 6: Use AI to Clarify Company Values and Hire Accordingly

The heart of your company comes from you, so authenticity matters. If you are unsure of your values, use AI to suggest psychological or values frameworks, take the one that fits, and then prompt AI to generate interview questions that test whether candidates align with those values. This replaces hours of manual question writing and helps you hire people who fit the real culture.

### Step 7: Innovate or Die With AI-Driven Messaging Iteration

Most companies plateau because they stop iterating. If your messaging is stuck, use AI to generate alternative product names, headlines, and landing page concepts. Then test them quickly using ad platforms like Meta Andromeda with small budgets per variant. Let conversion data decide what works rather than relying on internal opinion. Renaming Heights' product from "the smart supplement" to "Vitals" is a direct example of this principle in action.

## Examples

### Example 1: Validating an Insomnia Nutrition Brand

Dan Murray used AI to confirm demand for Heights before building it. He prompted an AI research partner for search volumes and buyer counts across Europe for insomnia-related topics. The result showed enough demand to move forward, and the research that used to take weeks was done in minutes.

### Example 2: Scaling Social Ads for a Supplement Launch

Heights needed thousands of ad variations across social channels. Instead of hiring a large content team, they prompted AI to generate multiple video concepts, hooks, and scripts, then turned one idea into 10 variations. This allowed them to test more creative combinations without proportional increases in time or cost.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Prompt AI with specific roles and constraints, such as "act as a market researcher with 10 years at McKinsey"
- ✅ Always test AI-generated personas, names, and messaging with real data before fully committing
- ✅ Build an email list early and nurture it; social platforms do not give you owned customer data
- ✅ Use cheap, quick A/B tests with small budgets per variant to let data decide winners
- ❌ Do not skip defining your "why" and assume AI will create authentic demand for you
- ❌ Do not spread across too many channels at once; pick one primary channel for year one
- ❌ Do not let perfectionism stop you from publishing; AI lets you iterate fast, so ship and improve

## Keep In Mind

- AI compresses time, but it does not replace strategy, taste, or authentic founder insight
- The best prompts include context, constraints, and desired output format
- Customer data privacy is a real concern when feeding large datasets into public AI tools
- Domain names and owned channels still matter more than rented social attention

## Security & Safety Notes

- Be cautious about uploading sensitive customer data or proprietary business information into public AI models
- Review AI-generated legal, financial, or compliance-related outputs before acting on them
- Keep credentials, API keys, and business secrets out of prompts and shared tools

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Relying on AI personas as if they are real customer interviews
  **Solution:** Use AI personas for directional insight, then validate with a small number of real conversations before scaling
- **Problem:** Testing too many channels at once because AI makes options feel easy
  **Solution:** Commit to one primary channel for year one, prove it works, then expand
- **Problem:** Letting AI generate messaging without testing conversion data
  **Solution:** Use small-budget A/B tests on landing pages or ad platforms and let performance metrics choose the winner
