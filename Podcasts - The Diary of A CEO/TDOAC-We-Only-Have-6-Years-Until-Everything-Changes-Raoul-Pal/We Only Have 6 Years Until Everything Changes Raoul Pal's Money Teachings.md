# We Only Have 6 Years Until Everything Changes: Raoul Pal's Money Teachings

## Overview

This Money Teaching distills Raoul Pal's urgent warning that traditional wealth-building systems—banks, real estate, pensions—are no longer reliable, and the next six years represent a pivotal window where conventional financial security will likely erode further. It provides a step-by-step framework for protecting and growing wealth in an era of currency debasement, AI disruption, and exponential technological change. The core thesis is that the only assets likely to outperform the ~11% annual loss of purchasing power are technology and crypto, particularly Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Solana.

## When to Follow These Money Teachings

- When you need to build long-term wealth without relying on traditional real estate or pension systems
- When you want to understand how to invest in crypto without risking more than 5-10% of your total net worth
- When your income is limited but you still want exposure to high-growth assets
- When the user asks about protecting savings from currency debasement
- When you're in your 20s or 30s and trying to avoid the first generation that won't be as rich as their parents

## Steps

### Step 1: Secure Your Income Foundation

Without income, you cannot invest or pursue opportunities. Focus on becoming an expert in a specific field while maintaining broad generalist knowledge. Work 40+ hours per week in your 20s to build skills, experience multiple setbacks, and prioritize knowledge acquisition over work-life balance during this foundational decade. An expert skill set commands premium pay and creates excess cash flow.

### Step 2: Reverse Engineer Your Future Self

Envision where you want to be in five to ten years. Identify what success looks like in measurable terms, then deconstruct the path backward to today. Ask yourself what specific skills, connections, and knowledge you need to acquire to bridge the gap. This reverse-engineering approach ensures your daily actions align with your long-term vision rather than following generic advice.

### Step 3: Follow Secular Trends and Play High-Leverage Games

Identify long-term secular trends—such as digitalization, AI, health and wellness, or renewable energy—and apply your expertise within those growing markets. Avoid crowded, low-return activities like buying multiple residential properties or working in industries with declining profit margins. Instead, find underserved market segments where your expertise commands higher compensation and buyers demonstrate willingness to pay premium prices. The highest returns come from applying the same skill set to markets with the greatest variance in outcome.

### Step 4: Allocate to Crypto with a Long-Term Time Horizon

Start with Bitcoin as your primary allocation because it is widely accessible and easy to purchase. Add Ethereum and Solana to capture broader blockchain adoption. Invest no more than 5-10% of your net worth initially, ensuring you can withstand a 60-70% portfolio decline without needing to liquidate for living expenses. Set up automatic monthly purchases regardless of price to build savings in an exponential asset. Commit to a minimum 10-year horizon; cyclical 70-80% drawdowns are normal and should be treated as buying opportunities, not reasons to sell.

### Step 5: Secure Your Holdings and Avoid Common Mistakes

Store your crypto on a hardware wallet (such as a Ledger device) with a seed phrase kept offline in multiple secure locations. Never leave large amounts on exchanges. Do not use leverage, margin, or borrowed money to buy crypto. Do not chase meme coins or altcoins beyond a small speculative allocation. Do not obsessively check prices; the strategy only works if you hold through volatility. Your one job is not to lose your tokens.

### Step 6: Treat Your Home as a Lifestyle Bank, Not a Wealth Engine

Do not buy a house primarily for wealth creation. With home prices at eight to ten times salary versus three and a half times in the past, mortgages now consume lifetimes of income mostly toward interest. Houses are illiquid, leveraged, and vulnerable to job loss or economic downturns. Rent housing in a stable market with strong tenant protections instead, and allocate excess capital to productive assets. Use investment gains to buy a home only when you can pay it down quickly or own it outright, turning it into a lifestyle asset rather than a financial burden.

### Step 7: Understand Currency Debasement and the "Everything Code"

Since 2008, governments worldwide have operated with debt exceeding 100% of GDP, forcing central banks to print liquidity every four years to service interest payments. This debases currency by approximately 8% annually, resulting in roughly 11% annual loss of purchasing power when combined with official inflation. The S&P 500's ~11% return barely offsets this erosion, while real estate and gold fail to generate real wealth for most people. Only technology (NASDAQ at ~18%) and crypto (Bitcoin at ~145% annually) have consistently outpaced this hurdle rate.

### Step 8: Invest in Yourself and Embrace Lifelong Learning

Because AI is eliminating knowledge-based roles—from truck drivers and delivery drivers to lawyers and coders—the scarcity of knowledge that once commanded high salaries is collapsing. The solution is not to resist this shift but to continuously upgrade your skill set, follow emerging trends, and remain adaptable. Treat learning as a lifelong process, and build businesses or careers that leverage uniquely human traits such as outdoor experiences, community building, or high-touch services that AI cannot replicate.

## Examples

### Example 1: The 20-Year-Old With $500 to Invest

A 20-year-old with $500 should open an account with a major crypto provider such as Coinbase or Kraken, purchase Bitcoin and Ethereum, and set up a recurring monthly buy regardless of price. They should avoid checking the portfolio daily, understanding that 70% drawdowns are part of the cycle. Over a 10-year horizon, this simple strategy can turn $500 into $50,000 or more, far outperforming a savings account or S&P 500 index fund.

### Example 2: The 30-Year-Old Caught in the Housing Trap

A 30-year-old who feels pressured to buy a house should instead rent in a stable location, allocate excess income to a crypto portfolio, and only consider purchasing property once investments have grown enough to allow a large down payment or outright purchase. This preserves capital mobility and avoids becoming a prisoner to a mortgage that consumes decades of income.

### Example 3: The Expert Seeking Higher Returns

A skilled professional earning good money but stuck in a saturated market should identify a niche within a secular trend where their expertise commands higher value. For example, a social media expert might serve pre-IPO tech companies instead of fashion brands, increasing earnings by an order of magnitude. The key is applying the same skill to a market with higher variance and fewer competitors.

## Security & Safety Notes

- Never keep large amounts of cryptocurrency on centralized exchanges; use a hardware wallet such as a Ledger device
- Write down your seed phrase on paper and store it in multiple secure physical locations—never in digital form
- Avoid using leverage, margin, or credit cards to purchase crypto; borrowed money amplifies losses and can wipe you out during drawdowns
- Government deposit insurance typically only covers up to $100,000; amounts beyond that in banks are at risk during bail-ins or systemic crises
- Blockchain transactions are irreversible; always verify wallet addresses before sending funds
- Be aware that regulatory environments are evolving; comply with local tax and reporting requirements for crypto holdings

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Buying a house early in life because "that's what everyone does" and becoming trapped by a massive mortgage relative to income
  **Solution:** Rent stable housing instead, build an investment portfolio first, and only buy property once you can pay it down aggressively or own it outright

- **Problem:** Selling crypto during a 60-80% drawdown because the portfolio value looks bad on paper
  **Solution:** Commit to a 10-year time horizon before investing; drawdowns are normal and historically followed by new all-time highs within a few years

- **Problem:** Chasing meme coins and speculative altcoins after seeing others make rapid gains, ultimately losing most or all of the portfolio
  **Solution:** Allocate 80-90% of your crypto portfolio to Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Solana; keep only 10% for speculative bets that you are prepared to lose entirely

- **Problem:** Obsessively checking crypto prices daily, making emotional decisions, and trading in and out of positions
  **Solution:** Buy and hold; check the portfolio only occasionally. The strategy works because of long-term compounding, not short-term trading skill

- **Problem:** Believing the S&P 500, real estate, or gold will preserve wealth against currency debasement
  **Solution:** Understand that traditional assets barely outpace the ~11% annual erosion of purchasing power; only technology and crypto have consistently produced real returns

- **Problem:** Putting all faith in a single prediction or price target, then becoming emotionally devastated when reality diverges
  **Solution:** Focus on the probability-weighted outcome and the magnitude of the trend, not specific price levels. Avoid building mental models around exact numbers
