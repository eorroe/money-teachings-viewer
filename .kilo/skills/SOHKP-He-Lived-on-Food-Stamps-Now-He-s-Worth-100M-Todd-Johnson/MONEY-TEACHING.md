# He Lived on Food Stamps… Now He’s Worth $100M | Todd Johnson

## Overview

This money teaching synthesizes the financial principles shared by Todd Johnson, who grew up on food stamps and later built companies across tech, construction, real estate, and supplements. The core lesson is that financial freedom starts with mindset and deliberate action, not circumstance. You should use these teachings when you need to build wealth from humble beginnings, identify opportunities in emerging markets, or develop the mental framework required for long-term entrepreneurship.

## When to Follow These Money Teachings

- When you are starting from limited financial means and need a path forward
- When you want to identify and capitalize on emerging market trends
- When you need to overcome fear of criticism or failure
- When building a vision bigger than your current circumstances
- When the user asks about entrepreneurship, mindset, or wealth-building from scarcity

## Steps

### Step 1: Let Pain Drive Your Vision

Identify the aspects of your current situation that you refuse to accept. Use that discomfort as fuel rather than letting it paralyze you. Write down exactly what you will never allow your life to look like, and let that clarity become the foundation of your ambition.

### Step 2: Build a Forward-Looking Resume

Instead of waiting for opportunities to come to you, create a living document that maps the skills and credentials you need to reach your income goals. Every time you interview or research a role, ask what it would take to earn the amount you want, then add those requirements to your development plan.

### Step 3: Adopt a "Zero Options" Mindset

Treat your current situation as if you have no alternative path. Comfort is the enemy of progress. When you believe you must succeed because you have no fallback, you will outwork everyone who is simply experimenting with entrepreneurship.

### Step 4: Expose Yourself to Greater Levels of Wealth

You cannot build what you cannot see. Deliberately place yourself in environments where higher levels of success are visible—visit successful neighborhoods, study billionaires, or surround yourself with people operating at a level above your own. Your vision expands to match what you are exposed to.

### Step 5: Chase Trending Markets With Conviction

When a new trend emerges—such as AI—do not wait to figure out the perfect angle. Choose a segment, go all in, and develop deep expertise. A blind person can still succeed in a gold rush if they simply pick a lane and commit fully.

### Step 6: Refuse to Fear Critique or Failure

People will talk about you whether you win or lose. Do not let the fear of judgment prevent you from taking action. Criticism is a sign you are in the game; irrelevance is the real failure.

## Examples

### Example 1: Starting from Scarcity

A person who grew up using food stamps turned that humiliation into relentless ambition. Instead of accepting the limits of their environment, they launched multiple businesses by solving problems they observed in their own community and then expanded into trending industries like technology.

### Example 2: The Forward-Looking Resume

Someone who wanted to earn a military-general-level salary created a resume that listed the skills they needed to develop rather than only what they had already achieved. Each job interview became a roadmap for their own education, and they checked off skills until they were qualified to build their first company.

### Example 3: Exposure as a Growth Lever

After moving to a wealthy area, an entrepreneur drove through upscale neighborhoods and asked what people did for a living. That curiosity led them to found a company that eventually interviewed and learned from the most successful people in those communities, accelerating their own growth.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Use pain as a strategic advantage, not a reason to quit
- ✅ Create a forward-looking resume that tracks skills you need, not just what you have
- ✅ Expose yourself to higher levels of success regularly
- ✅ Choose a trending market and commit fully instead of dabbling in multiple areas
- ✅ Ignore critics and focus on execution
- ❌ Do not wait until you feel "ready" to start
- ❌ Do not let small thinking limit your vision
- ❌ Do not accept the career path that others prescribe for you

## Keep In Mind

- Your background does not determine your ceiling
- Vision must be accompanied by relentless learning and execution
- What looks like overnight success is usually years of invisible work
- The people around you shape what you believe is possible

## Security & Safety Notes

- Always verify business opportunities before committing capital
- Be cautious when sharing financial details with strangers
- Consult qualified professionals before making major business or legal decisions
- Never invest more than you can afford to lose in speculative ventures

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Allowing fear of what others think to block action
  **Solution:** Remember that people will talk regardless; focus on results over reputation

- **Problem:** Staying in a comfort zone that limits growth
  **Solution:** Treat every day as if you have no fallback option; let urgency drive you

- **Problem:** Having a vision that is too small to sustain motivation
  **Solution:** Write your goals in terms that scare you slightly; if they do not excite and intimidate you, they are too small

- **Problem:** Waiting for the "right" moment to enter a market
  **Solution:** Trends reward speed of execution, not perfection. Pick a niche and learn by doing.
