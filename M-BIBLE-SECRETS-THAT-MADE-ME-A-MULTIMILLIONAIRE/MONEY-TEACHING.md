# Bible Secrets That Made Me a Multimillionaire - King Solomon's Business Model

## Overview

This teaching distills the biblical business principles that transformed Myin Golden's life from financial ruin to generating over $50 million in business revenue, based entirely on patterns found in 1 Kings 3-10 regarding King Solomon. The core message is that the Bible is an instruction manual for practical life success, including entrepreneurship and wealth creation, when read and applied correctly rather than relying on handed-down religious doctrine. Solomon's model combined total surrender to God, wisdom through prayer, strategic partnerships, content creation, consulting at premium rates, crowd teaching, and recurring revenue streams to become the wisest and wealthiest man on earth.

## When to Follow These Money Teachings

- When you are an entrepreneur or business owner seeking a biblical framework for building wealth
- When you have been taught that the Bible contradicts prosperity and want to understand the full context of wealth scriptures
- When you feel spiritually inadequate or unqualified and need to understand that God qualifies the called
- When you want to build a business that creates generational wealth for your family rather than just survival income
- When you are ready to shift from trading time for money to building systems that generate recurring revenue
- When you want to serve people through your gifts and be compensated at a premium based on the value you deliver

## Steps

### Step 1: Read the Bible as a Practical Instruction Manual for Life

Read the Bible not as a religious text alone but as a practical guidebook with specific principles that produce specific outcomes when applied. When you find a principle, test it by applying it to real-life situations. Myin Golden's first tested principle was Proverbs 15:1, "A soft answer turns away wrath," which he applied to a conflict with his brother and saw immediate positive results. Every time he applied a scripture, it produced exactly what God said it would do. Do not rely on "hand-me-down doctrine" from others who may have misinterpreted scriptures. Study the Bible yourself using tools like Strong's Concordance, Treasury of Scripture Knowledge, and Bible dictionaries to verify every principle before applying it.

### Step 2: Reframe Your Relationship With Money

Do not love money. Money is a side benefit, not the primary goal. It is gravy on mashed potatoes, icing on the cake, not the main course. Do not use people to get money; instead, use money to love and serve people. Do not trust in money as your source or security because riches make themselves wings and fly away (Proverbs 23:5). Money is a depreciating asset, not a resource. God is the source. You are the resource. Trust in the Lord who will not fail when money fails. Understand that "labor not to be rich" in context means do not try to get your hands in rich people's pockets or covet what belongs to others (Proverbs 23:4-5 in context), not that you should avoid building wealth through honorable work.

### Step 3: Form Strategic Partnerships That Multiply Results

Solomon made an affinity with Pharaoh, king of Egypt, and took his daughter as a wife, procuring powerful partnerships before he even built his business. The principle from Ecclesiastes 4:12 is that "two are better than one, and a three-fold cord is not quickly broken." In practical terms: if you have an offer but no audience, partner with an individual or business that has built an audience. Mix your offer with their audience and split the revenue so both parties make more than either would alone. The scripture says if one person chases a thousand, two will put ten thousand to flight. Half of ten thousand is five times more than one hundred percent of one thousand. Partnering with the right person multiplies your output far beyond what you could achieve alone.

### Step 4: Present Yourself as a Living Sacrifice

Solomon offered one thousand burnt offerings upon the altar, which represents total sacrifice. A burnt offering is a picture of giving everything and keeping nothing for yourself. This corresponds to Romans 12:1: "I beseech you therefore, brethren, by the mercies of God, that you present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy and acceptable unto God, which is your reasonable service." Sacrifice means letting go of something of a lower nature to take hold of something of a higher nature. Burn up your energy and life force living for the purpose of the One who created you. When you fully surrender, God has room to work in your weakness.

### Step 5: Pray for Wisdom to Serve Others, Not for Personal Gain

Solomon's prayer in 1 Kings 3:9 was not for long life, not for riches, and not for the life of his enemies. The only thing he asked for was an understanding heart to judge and discern between good and bad so he could serve the people God put him there to serve. Adopt this prayer daily: "Dear Lord, give me the wisdom to fulfill the specific purpose you put me on this earth to accomplish in a way that pleases you and serves the people you placed me here to serve." If you feel inadequate, incapable, or insufficient, that is not a disqualification; that is the exact qualification for God to work. Your weakness is where God has room to operate. God does not call the qualified; He qualifies the called.

### Step 6: Create Content That Demonstrates Your Wisdom

Solomon spoke 3,000 proverbs and wrote 1,005 songs. He taught about trees, animals, insects, fish, and birds, and people came from many nations to hear his wisdom. He showed uncommon lessons drawn from natural world examples, such as teaching the wisdom of preparation from ants, compensation from rock badgers, cooperation from locusts, and leverage from spiders. Create content that demonstrates your expertise in the specific domain or craft you are called to master and serve. Whether through writing, speaking, video, or audio, produce content so compelling that people cannot stop talking about it and seek you out to learn more. Reputation should precede you, but the actual experience of you should exceed the reputation.

### Step 7: Consult Other Practitioners at Premium Rates Based on Value

Solomon was a consultant to other kings and monarchs in the same business he was running. He charged them based on the results they would receive, not based on hours spent. The Queen of Sheba alone gave him 120 talents of gold, which at 70 pounds per talent and $3,100 per ounce equals approximately $416,640,000 in today's dollars for a single consultation. All the kings of the earth came to him and paid him to learn his wisdom (1 Kings 10:23-24). If you know how to do something well, you can build a business by teaching others how to do it. Charge based on the payoff your client will receive, not on the time it takes you to deliver it. If you show a client how to make a million dollars and they think that is only worth a few thousand dollars, their logic is faulty; work with clients who value the transformation you provide.

### Step 8: Teach Crowds Through Seminars and Public Events

Solomon was a communicator to crowds, holding seminars before modern speakers like Tony Robbins. He taught in coliseums that held 10,000 to 30,000 people at a time, and they paid to attend. Create public events, seminars, or courses to teach the principles you have mastered. Solomon had "top 10 hits" across education and entertainment fronts so his impact was inescapable. Use available platforms, including social media, to reach a mass audience. If you have something of value, charge for it. The wisdom God puts in your heart does not need to be given away for free simply because it is spiritual. Solomon charged for his wisdom and used the funds to support widows, orphans, and his family.

### Step 9: Build Recurring Revenue Through Continuity

Solomon's clients paid him "year by year" (1 Kings 10:25). Once he taught them his principles, they came back annually for continued guidance and paid him again. Build continuity into your business model. Create a coaching program, membership, subscription, or ongoing advisory relationship that generates recurring revenue. Myin Golden runs a coaching program for entrepreneurs at $55,000 per year teaching King Solomon's business model, and people who believe in God and love God pay it, and people who do not believe in God also pay it because they see the value in what he has built.

### Step 10: Build Generational Wealth Through Inheritance

The Bible says "house and riches are the inheritance of fathers" (Proverbs 13:22). A good man leaves an inheritance to his children's children. Build wealth not for personal consumption alone but as a legacy for future generations. Teach your children financial responsibility before you hand them wealth. Myin Golden's children are more financially responsible than he is because they were taught the principles before receiving any money. Do not worry about money ruining your children if you have taught them how to handle it. Work not because you need the money, but because work is how you exercise your godlikeness.

### Step 11: Use the Poor Wise Man Principle to Value Wisdom Over Appearance

Ecclesiastes 9:15-16 describes a poor wise man who by his wisdom delivered a besieged city, yet no one remembered him. The passage concludes: "Wisdom is better than strength, but the poor man's wisdom is despised." Value wisdom over financial appearances. The poor wise man defeated the wealthy king. Do not let someone's lack of financial status cause you to despise their wisdom. If you are the one with wisdom but little money, continue to operate in wisdom because it will eventually deliver you. If you are wealthy, seek wisdom regardless of who it comes from. Wisdom is better than strength.

## Examples

### Example 1: Myin Golden's Entrepreneurial Journey

Myin Golden struggled in entrepreneurship for 14 years. In 1999, he had his first six-figure year ($48,000 the year before, then over $100,000). Within a couple of years, his business was doing over one million dollars in annual revenue. Then a seven-year period of compounded personal, financial, and business losses began: his oldest son Adam died in a car accident in 2008, the Great Recession destroyed his client base, the IRS audited him in 2010 and claimed he owed $1.65 million, and his Mercedes was repossessed in 2012. He sold his house in 2012 for $634,000 (a short sale after buying it for $1.027 million in 2008) and lived on borrowed money for two years. In 2015, he discovered King Solomon's business model in the Bible and applied it. Ten years later, his business has made over $50 million. He now runs a $55,000 per year coaching program teaching Solomon's model, holds live events like "Content to Cash Flow Live" ($297 tickets, 261 attendees), and has created generational wealth for his children and grandchildren.

### Example 2: Martha Stewart Built a Billion-Dollar Empire From Homemaking Content

Martha Stewart is a business magnate, author, and television personality who taught millions of people how to do homemaking and domestic arts. She wrote books on homemaking, made videos on homemaking, and created courses on homemaking. She became a billionaire. This is exactly King Solomon's model: identify what you know how to do, create content around it, teach it to others, and monetize through multiple channels. Solomon wrote 3,000 proverbs and 1,005 songs, taught in seminars, consulted with kings at premium rates, and received recurring payments year by year. Martha Stewart applied the same model using modern media instead of ancient coliseums.

## Keep In Mind

- The Bible contains both warnings about the dangers of wealth and clear instructions for building wealth; both are true in their proper contexts. The warnings are about loving money and trusting in it; the instructions are about honoring God with your work.
- "Labor not to be rich" (Proverbs 23:4) in context means do not covet or try to get your hands in other people's pockets, not that you should avoid building wealth through honest enterprise.
- Money is a depreciating asset and not your source. God is the source. You are the resource.
- Work is not just for earning money; work is how you exercise your godlikeness and fulfill your purpose on earth.
- Even if you start with nothing, wisdom will deliver you. The poor wise man in Ecclesiastes 9:15-16 delivered a city from a mighty king through wisdom alone.
- Generational wealth is biblical: "House and riches are the inheritance of fathers" (Proverbs 13:22) and "a good man leaveth an inheritance to his children's children" (Proverbs 13:22).
- You do not need to be a pastor or in full-time ministry to be used by God powerfully in the marketplace.

## Security & Safety Notes

- Do not borrow money to finance your business or lifestyle. Myin Golden lived on borrowed money for two years after his house short sale and learned the hard way. He explicitly stated in the video: "Don't borrow money."
- The IRS audited Myin Golden in 2010 and claimed he owed $1.65 million, which he could not pay. Maintain proper financial records and tax compliance.
- The Great Recession destroyed Myin Golden's business because his clients went bankrupt and could not pay him. Diversify your client base and do not rely on a limited pool of high-value clients, because losing even a handful of them can collapse your revenue.
- Money can be lost quickly through unexpected tragedies, market crashes, or health crises. Do not make money your security. Trust in God.
- Do not co-sign loans or lend money to friends or family that you cannot afford to lose. Myin Golden had to depend on financial support from a close personal friend during the two-year period he lived on borrowed money after selling his house in 2012.
- Keep an emergency fund and live below your means so you can survive periods of reduced income without borrowing.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Accepting hand-me-down doctrine without personal Bible study, which leads to misapplying scriptures like "labor not to be rich" or "money is the root of all evil" out of context.
  **Solution:** Read the full context of every scripture. Use study tools like Strong's Concordance, Treasury of Scripture Knowledge, and Bible dictionaries. Test every principle you hear by applying it in real life.

- **Problem:** Making money the primary goal rather than serving people, which leads to unethical behavior, burnout, and spiritual emptiness.
  **Solution:** Reframe money as a side benefit or bonus. Make serving people and pleasing God your primary aim. If you do what God called you to do, the money will follow as a byproduct.

- **Problem:** Trusting in money as a source of security instead of trusting in God, which leads to fear and anxiety when financial losses occur.
  **Solution:** Internalize that money is a depreciating asset and not your source. God is the source. Practice praying "Give us this day our daily bread" and trusting that God will provide even when money fails.

- **Problem:** Trading time for money as the sole income model, which limits earning potential and prevents scalability.
  **Solution:** Build a business model based on teaching/consulting (premium one-on-one), crowd teaching (seminars/events), content creation (passive reach), and recurring revenue (continuity programs) as Solomon demonstrated.

- **Problem:** Working alone instead of partnering, which limits growth to what one person can produce alone.
  **Solution:** Find partners who have what you lack (audience, offer, skills). Combine forces so that together you produce multiples of what either could alone.

- **Problem:** Charging too little based on time instead of value, which undervalues your expertise and prevents wealth accumulation.
  **Solution:** Price based on the results and transformation your client receives, not on the hours you work. If you deliver a million-dollar result, charge accordingly.

- **Problem:** Spending all money on personal consumption instead of building generational wealth.
  **Solution:** Follow Proverbs 13:22: build house and riches as an inheritance for your children's children. Teach your children financial responsibility before they receive money.

- **Problem:** Allowing internal resistance from religious programming to block you from receiving biblical prosperity teachings.
  **Solution:** Recognize that internal resistance often comes from well-meaning family members or a pastor who misapplied prosperity scriptures decades ago, teaching that wealth and faith are incompatible.
