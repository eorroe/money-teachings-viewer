# Ambiguity Taxonomy

## Types to Check
1. [bracketed placeholders]: TRANSCRIPT LIMITATION, TODO, TBD, FIXME, or any bracketed text
2. PRONOUN: "it", "this", "that", "they", "these", "those" without a clear antecedent within the same paragraph.
3. QUANTIFIER: "often", "sometimes", "many", "some", "few", "usually" without a bounded context
4. VAGUE NOUN: Abstract nouns used without concrete grounding
5. CONTRADICTION: Claims that directly conflict with other statements in the document
6. METAPHOR: Figurative or spiritual language that could be misread as a literal factual claim
7. SCOPE: Unclear applicability — who/what/when/where the statement applies to
8. IMPLICIT ASSUMPTION: Statements that rely on unstated prerequisites the reader may not share
9. CONTRADICTORY MODIFIERS: "approximately exactly", "over exactly", "near-exactly", "roughly exactly", "exactly at least", "at least exactly"
10. VAGUE QUANTIFIERS WITHOUT SPECIFIC NUMBERS: "many", "few", "multiple", "several", "various", "numerous" used as the ONLY quantification
11. VAGUE MODIFIERS: "approximately", "roughly", "around", "about", "nearly", "almost" when the transcript has a specific number
12. VAGUE MARKETING TERMS: "best-in-class", "existential risk", "competitive advantage" (when used without concrete definition)
13. METAPHORS WITHOUT CONCRETE ACTIONS: "absorb the loss", "do hard things", "burn your boats", "ER triage prioritization", "healthy public embarrassment", "life-changing amount", "visible through broken windows", "significant strategic shift"
14. VAGUE CRITERIA: "happy", "fair", "significant", "certain" without definition
15. Undefined acronyms on first use (without expansion in parentheses)
16. "or more" vague quantifiers: "N or more", "N% or more", "N percent or more" -> replace with "at least N"
17. Vague claims without specific evidence, citations, or concrete examples
18. Undefined terms that appear in the main content but not in the Glossary
19. Unclear pronouns or references
20. Idiomatic language or metaphors without explanation
21. Subjective standards without clear criteria
22. Interpretive claims presented as facts without qualification
23. Strong epistemic claims without proper qualification
24. Vague timeframes, quantities, or numbers without attribution
25. Passive voice without clear attribution
26. Any text that could be interpreted in multiple ways
