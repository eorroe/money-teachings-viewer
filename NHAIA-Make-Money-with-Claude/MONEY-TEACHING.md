# How to Make Money with Claude as an AI Consultant

## Overview

This Money Teaching outlines a practical path to generating income with Claude by becoming a Claude AI consultant, either as a freelancer or as an in-house specialist. It addresses the shrinking opportunity in the AI agency market and the rising demand for internal AI expertise within companies. The core strategy is to identify a painful manual business problem, build a Claude-powered fix, prove it moves a specific metric, and then monetize that result.

## When to Follow These Money Teachings

- When you want to monetize your AI skills without running a full agency or managing clients long-term
- When you're currently employed and want to create a high-value AI role inside your existing company
- When you prefer freelancing but want to avoid the race-to-the-bottom on cheap automation builds
- When a business you know is stuck on AI pilots and needs someone to drive real, measurable results

## Steps

### Step 1: Pick One Painful Problem

Open a document and list every manual task you or your team completed last week. Circle the one that consumes the most hours. Before building anything, define the single metric you are trying to move — for example, hours spent, errors reduced, or revenue gained. This metric is what you will sell; the destination is always time saved, mistakes cut, or money made.

### Step 2: Build the Fix and Document It

Configure Claude with specific, tested instructions tied to your actual process documents, templates, and checklists. Work with Claude until it reliably produces output that matches your documented requirements every time, and build reusable Claude project configurations or instruction sets around that process. When the fix is working, record a short before-and-after demo showing the time or cost difference.

### Step 3: Deliver It and Prove the Number Moved

Present the result in a team meeting or to the business owner, quantifying the improvement — for example, reducing a 4-hour weekly report to 20 minutes. Ask one question: "Does anyone have a task they would want me to look at and see how I could automate it with AI?" Every new task follows the same loop: find the constraint, name the metric, build the fix, and prove the metric moved.

### Step 4: Turn It Into Money

If you are freelancing, start by building a proof-of-concept automation for someone you know at no charge, then sell paid AI workflow audits where you review a business process and deliver a written improvement plan, then sell paid AI automation builds where you implement the approved solution, and finally establish a monthly retainer for ongoing optimization. If you are in-house, bring your documented wins to your boss, present each win using metrics your boss already cares about — such as cost saved, revenue protected, or hours freed — so the result reads as your boss's win, and formalize the AI role. Even without an opening, you can formalize an AI specialist title and set of responsibilities by consistently being the person who delivers measurable AI-driven results.

## Examples

### Example 1: In-House Report Automation

An employee noticed they spent 4 hours every Friday building a manual report. They gathered past reports, the template, and a checklist of where each number came from, then configured Claude to generate the report using those materials. After proving the report now takes 10 minutes, they demonstrated the win in a team meeting and were asked to review other team tasks for automation.

### Example 2: Freelance Consultant Scaling to Retainers

A consultant started by building a Claude automation for a local business owner for free. Based on that result, they sold a paid audit, then a paid automation build, and then secured a monthly retainer to continue optimizing the business's AI workflows, eliminating the feast-or-famine cycle of project-only work.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Focus on one painful problem at a time and name the metric before you build
- ✅ Use real business documents and templates to train Claude, not just prompts
- ✅ Record a before-and-after demo so the improvement is visible and shareable
- ✅ Frame in-house wins as your manager's wins to accelerate buy-in
- ✅ Start with people who already know you before doing cold outreach as a freelancer
- ❌ Don't build automations just because they look impressive; build only what moves a specific number
- ❌ Don't skip documenting the process; the documentation is part of the deliverable
- ❌ Don't rely on a single client or project; move toward retainers or a stable internal role

## Security & Safety Notes

- Only use AI tools that your company has explicitly approved
- Never input company or customer data into AI tools that are not authorized for that data
- Verify compliance requirements before processing sensitive business information with Claude

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Automating tasks that don't move a clear metric, wasting time on "cool" automations
  **Solution:** Always define the specific time, error, or money metric before building anything
- **Problem:** Building a fix but never proving the result to anyone
  **Solution:** Create a short before-and-after demo and present it in a team meeting or to leadership
- **Problem:** Relying solely on cold outreach without leveraging existing relationships
  **Solution:** Start with people who already know you and your work before scaling to cold outreach
- **Problem:** Giving up after one client loss or one rejected proposal
  **Solution:** Use retainers or an official internal role to stabilize income and demand
- **Problem:** Forgetting that the role can be created from the inside even if no job posting exists
  **Solution:** Proactively become the AI person by repeatedly solving real business problems and associating yourself with those wins
