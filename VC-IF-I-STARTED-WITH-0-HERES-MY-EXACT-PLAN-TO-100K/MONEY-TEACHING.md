# If I Started With $0, Here's My Exact Plan To $100K

## Overview

Vincent Chan shares an eight-step roadmap to reaching the first $100k, which he identifies as the most transformative financial milestone. The teaching focuses on eliminating distractions, optimizing expenses, maximizing time, growing active income streams, investing for passive income, and avoiding lifestyle inflation to build lasting wealth.

## When to Follow These Money Teachings

- When you need to build wealth starting from zero with no existing savings
- When working toward your first $100k net worth milestone
- When the user asks about a step-by-step plan to achieve financial independence

## Steps

### Step 1: Eliminate Everything That Numbs You

Identify what is motivating you to reach $100k—whether it's a toxic job, desire to provide for family, or being tired of being broke—and use that pain as fuel. Eliminate all distractions and noise that temporarily numb that drive, such as mindless consumption of success content without taking action. The key is subtraction, not addition: remove everything that slows you down so you can maintain focus throughout the journey.

### Step 2: Cut Three Specific Types of Expenses

Apply the Pareto principle to cut only the biggest expenses: housing, transportation, and food. For housing, live at home if possible or get roommates to minimize costs. For transportation, use a fully paid-off used car, carpool, or public transit. For food, buy in bulk, meal prep, and avoid expensive daily purchases. Keep small pleasures like iced coffee to maintain happiness and avoid burnout.

### Step 3: Apply the 4-4 Framework

Your time is your most valuable asset. Unlock two 4-hour blocks (5-9 AM and 5-9 PM) by waking up earlier, sleeping later, or both—plus weekends. Use this dedicated chunk of time to work on your wealth-building activities. You will need the pain from Step 1 to push through this demanding schedule.

### Step 4: Focus on Your 9-5 Job

Active income from your job funds your path to $100k. Employers typically pay the minimum to retain workers, so negotiate for raises using market data (Glassdoor/Indeed) and a folder of accomplishments. If denied, consider job hopping for higher pay, better titles, more responsibility, or growth opportunities.

### Step 5: Build Another Active Income Stream

Use your identified time chunk to build a side income. Find your "profit zone" by drawing a Venn diagram with three circles: what you do for fun, what you're good at, and what people will pay for. Stack skills to create unique value and consistency. The side hustle should fit your schedule, not the other way around.

### Step 6: Turn Active Income Into Passive Income

Once you have active income, invest to build passive income streams. The lowest-effort path is investing in the stock market via low-cost index funds or ETFs like the S&P 500. Even investing $20/day can reach $100k in 8-9 years at 10% average returns. Start small ($5) and use fractional investing.

### Step 7: Avoid Lifestyle Inflation

When your income increases, cap lifestyle increases at 10-20% of the raise or bonus. Funnel the rest into investments, retirement, business, or education. The choice is between being rich and looking rich—short-term sacrifices lead to long-term freedom.

### Step 8: Understand Critical Mass Theory

$100k is the critical mass threshold. At $15k/year invested, it takes 5.36 years to reach $100k, but 80% comes from your contributions. Beyond $100k, compounding accelerates: each additional $100k takes progressively less time—3.53 years to reach $200k, 2.64 years to reach $300k, 2.11 years to reach $400k, and 1.75 years to reach $500k. Multiple income streams act like legs on a chair—more streams mean greater stability and faster wealth accumulation.

## Examples

### Example 1: Using Pain as Motivation

Vincent hated working 12-13 hour days in corporate America with toxic coworkers. That pain drove him to build wealth and become his own boss, allowing him to push through the difficult early stages of content creation and business building.

### Example 2: The Venn Diagram Profit Zone

Vincent's profit zone combined his interest in making videos, his skill in personal finance, and the market demand for teaching money topics. He stacked these skills to become a successful finance YouTuber, demonstrating how intersecting all three circles creates a sustainable, high-value income stream.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Use your pain and dissatisfaction as motivation to stay focused on your goals
- ✅ Cut only your largest expenses (housing, transportation, food) while keeping small pleasures
- ✅ Find your profit zone by intersecting what you enjoy, what you're good at, and what pays
- ✅ Negotiate your salary or job hop to increase active income before building passive income
- ✅ Invest consistently in low-cost index funds like the S&P 500, even with small amounts
- ❌ Don't consume success content without taking action—it numbs your drive
- ❌ Don't cut small daily expenses that bring you joy and cause burnout
- ❌ Don't accept low raises without negotiating or seeking better opportunities
- ❌ Don't inflate your lifestyle when your income increases

## Keep In Mind

- The first $100k is the hardest milestone due to the nature of compound interest and critical mass
- Time is money—unlock extra hours using the 4-4 framework to build active income
- Multiple income streams create financial resilience, like a chair with multiple legs
- Money starts working harder than you only after you pass the $100k threshold

## Security & Safety Notes

- Research any investment thoroughly before committing capital
- Verify side hustle income streams comply with local laws, taxes, and employment contracts
- Be wary of get-rich-quick schemes that promise passive income without upfront work

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Consuming motivational content without taking action
  **Solution:** Eliminate distractions and focus on executing the roadmap steps
- **Problem:** Burning out from cutting too many small expenses
  **Solution:** Apply the Pareto principle and protect small pleasures that sustain your happiness
- **Problem:** Quitting side hustles too early due to lack of immediate results
  **Solution:** Choose a profit zone venture aligned with your interests and skills to maintain consistency
- **Problem:** Lifestyle inflation eliminating investment capacity
  **Solution:** Cap lifestyle increases at 10-20% of any raise and automate the rest into investments
