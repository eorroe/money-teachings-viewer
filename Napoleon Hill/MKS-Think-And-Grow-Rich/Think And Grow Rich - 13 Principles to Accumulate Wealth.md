# Think And Grow Rich - 13 Principles to Accumulate Wealth

## Overview

This money teaching distills Napoleon Hill's "Think and Grow Rich" (1937) into actionable principles for accumulating wealth. The book presents 13 tested principles derived from analyzing over 500 successful Americans, including Andrew Carnegie, Thomas Edison, Henry Ford, and Charles M. Schwab. These principles teach you how to transmute a definite desire for money into its physical equivalent through controlled thought, faith, organized planning, and persistent action. The core philosophy is that all riches begin as thoughts, and by mastering these 13 principles you can develop the "money consciousness" required to attract and retain wealth.

## When to Follow These Money Teachings

- When you have a specific financial goal and need a proven framework to achieve it
- When you want to build wealth through thought, planning, and persistent action rather than relying on chance or luck
- When you are starting a business, pursuing a career, or building an investment strategy and need mental discipline
- When you have struggled to make money despite effort and need to identify what is blocking your success
- When you want to develop the mindset and habits of the world's most successful people

## Steps

### Step 1: Fix a Definite Amount of Money in Your Mind

Determine the exact amount of money you want to accumulate. Do not say "I want plenty of money" - be specific. Write down the exact figure. There is a psychological reason for definiteness: the subconscious mind needs a clear target to work toward. Example: "I want $10,000" not "I want to be rich."

### Step 2: Determine What You Will Give in Return

Money is always exchanged for something of value. Decide exactly what service, product, or effort you will provide in exchange for the money you desire. There is no such reality as something for nothing. Be specific about the value you will create.

### Step 3: Establish a Definite Deadline

Set a specific date by which you intend to possess the money you desire. This creates urgency and forces your subconscious mind to work toward a tangible timeline. Without a deadline, desire remains a wish rather than a goal.

### Step 4: Create a Definite Plan and Begin Immediately

Write down a clear plan for carrying out your desire. Begin putting the plan into action immediately, whether you feel ready or not. Do not wait for perfect conditions. Action, even imperfect action, activates the law of cause and effect.

### Step 5: Write Out a Clear, Concise Statement

Create a written statement that includes: (a) the exact amount of money you intend to acquire, (b) the time limit for its acquisition, (c) what you intend to give in return for the money, and (d) a clear description of the plan through which you intend to accumulate it. This written statement becomes your roadmap and a tool for autosuggestion.

### Step 6: Read Your Written Statement Aloud Twice Daily

Read your written statement aloud once just before retiring at night and once after arising in the morning. As you read, visualize yourself already in possession of the money. See it, feel it, and believe it is already yours. This practice saturates your subconscious mind with the desire and begins the process of transmutation.

### Step 7: Apply Autosuggestion to Convince Your Subconscious

Use autosuggestion - repeated affirmation or instruction to the subconscious mind - to convince yourself that you will receive the money you desire. Your subconscious mind will act on whatever belief you impress upon it. Mix emotion and feeling with your words; plain unemotional words do not influence the subconscious mind.

### Step 8: Develop Faith Through Repetition

Faith is a state of mind developed through repeated affirmation to the subconscious mind. The only known method of voluntarily developing faith is repetition of affirmation. As you repeat your statement with emotion, faith grows. Faith is the head chemist of the mind and opens a direct line of communication between your finite mind and infinite intelligence.

### Step 9: Build Specialized Knowledge Through Your Mastermind Group

You do not need to have all knowledge in your own head. An educated person knows where to get knowledge when needed and how to organize it into plans of action through a Mastermind group. Assemble a group of people whose knowledge complements your own, and use their collective intelligence to fill gaps in your expertise.

### Step 10: Use Imagination to Visualize Your Goal Before It Exists

All who have accumulated great fortunes first engaged in dreaming, hoping, wishing, desiring, and planning before they acquired money. You cannot see riches in your bank balance until you first see them in your imagination. Every great leader was a dreamer who had the vision and imagination to see realities in mental and spiritual form before they existed physically.

### Step 11: Form a Mastermind Group for Exchange of Ideas

Form a study club or alliance with people who are friendly and harmonious. Meet regularly (at least once per week) to read and discuss the chapters of this philosophy. Exchange ideas freely, make notes, and tap into the collective wisdom of the group. The Mastermind principle is the primary reason Andrew Carnegie accumulated his fortune and why he made millionaires of the men to whom he taught his secret.

### Step 12: Make Decisions Quickly and Stick to Them

Successful people make decisions quickly and change them slowly, if at all. Unsuccessful people make decisions slowly and change them quickly. Practice making firm decisions and standing by them. The habit of indecision keeps the majority of people in poverty.

### Step 13: Practice Persistence With a Burning Desire

Persistence is essential because no one has ever accumulated great riches without it. Edwin C. Barnes became a business associate of Thomas Edison not because he had money or connections, but because his desire was so definite and persistent that he was willing to travel by freight train and stand before Edison until he got what he wanted. Develop persistence by mixing your desire with faith and applying these principles repeatedly.

### Step 14: Transmute Sex Energy Into Creative Force

Sex desire is the most powerful human desire. When harnessed and redirected away from physical expression toward creative pursuits, it becomes a powerful motivating force that maintains keenness of imagination, courage, willpower, persistence, and creative ability. This transmutation of energy is essential for sustained achievement.

### Step 15: Use the Subconscious Mind as a Fertile Garden

Your subconscious mind is like a fertile garden. If you do not plant seeds of desirable crops, weeds will grow in abundance. Through autosuggestion, you plant the seeds of your desire. The subconscious mind recognizes and acts upon only thoughts that have been mixed with emotion or feeling. Be persistent in feeding your subconscious mind positive, goal-oriented thoughts.

### Step 16: Receive Inspiration Through the Sixth Sense

When your plans are ready, solutions and opportunities will flash into your mind through the sixth sense in the form of inspiration. This inspiration is a direct message from infinite intelligence. Treat it with respect and act upon it immediately. Failure to act on inspiration is fatal to success.

## Examples

### Example 1: Edwin C. Barnes - Becoming Edison's Business Associate

Edwin C. Barnes had a burning desire to become a business associate of Thomas Edison. He had no money, no connections, and no introduction. But his desire was definite - he wanted to work with Edison, not for him. He traveled to Edison's laboratory by freight train, presented himself, and announced he had come to go into business with the inventor. Edison later said: "When a man really desires a thing so deeply that he is willing to stake his entire future on a single turn of the wheel in order to get it, he is sure to win." Barnes persisted until Edison gave him the opportunity, and that opportunity led to a fortune.

### Example 2: Charles M. Schwab - Organizing U.S. Steel Corporation

Charles M. Schwab, a young man coached by Andrew Carnegie in the use of this formula, applied the secret to organize the giant United States Steel Corporation as Carnegie conceived it. This single application of the formula made Schwab a huge fortune in both money and opportunity, worth approximately $600 million. Carnegie's formula worked for Schwab because Schwab was ready for it and followed the instructions precisely.

### Example 3: Henry Ford - Specialized Knowledge Through Mastermind

During a libel trial, Henry Ford was asked foolish questions by attorneys trying to prove he was ignorant. Ford replied: "I have a row of electric push buttons on my desk and by pushing the right button I can summon to my aid men who can answer any question I desire to ask concerning the business to which I am devoting most of my efforts." Ford demonstrated that an educated person is one who knows where to get knowledge when needed and how to organize it into action through a Mastermind group. He accumulated massive wealth not by knowing everything himself, but by assembling specialists.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Being vague about the amount of money you want
  **Solution:** Write down the exact figure. "Plenty of money" is not a target your subconscious mind can act upon. Specify $50,000 or $100,000 or whatever exact amount you require.

- **Problem:** Failing to mix emotion with autosuggestion
  **Solution:** When you read your written statement, feel the emotion of already having the money. Visualize it, believe it, and let the feeling permeate your whole being. Plain unemotional words will not influence your subconscious mind.

- **Problem:** Waiting for perfect conditions before starting
  **Solution:** Create your plan and begin immediately whether you are ready or not. Perfect conditions never come. Action activates cause and effect; inaction guarantees nothing will happen.

- **Problem:** Changing your mind every time you face a setback
  **Solution:** Persistence means continuing despite temporary defeat. Edwin Barnes traveled by freight train and stood before Edison until he got his opportunity. Make decisions quickly and change them slowly, if at all.

- **Problem:** Trying to do everything yourself
  **Solution:** Build a Mastermind group. No single person has all the knowledge required for great success. Henry Ford succeeded because he knew where to get knowledge, not because he carried it all in his own head.

- **Problem:** Letting your subconscious mind grow weeds of poverty
  **Solution:** Feed your subconscious mind only thoughts of wealth, success, and abundance through daily autosuggestion. Whatever you repeatedly think and feel becomes your reality. Starve thoughts of poverty and feed thoughts of riches.
