# Think And Grow Rich

## Overview

Think And Grow Rich is a wealth philosophy that teaches how to transform a definite desire for money into its financial equivalent through a systematic process of thought, faith, organized planning, and persistent action. It exists because Napoleon Hill discovered that all great fortunes are built on the same underlying principles, which can be learned and applied by anyone regardless of education or starting circumstances. The book provides the exact step-by-step method used by history's most successful self-made millionaires to accumulate wealth.

## When to Follow These Money Teachings

- When you want to accumulate money in great abundance and are ready to define exactly what you want
- When you need to overcome procrastination and build the habit of reaching decisions promptly
- When you lack specialized knowledge and need to acquire or organize the expertise required for your goal
- When you feel discouraged by failure and need to develop the persistence to continue despite opposition
- When you want to harness the power of a mastermind group to amplify your efforts
- When you need to overcome fear, especially the fear of poverty, and develop money consciousness

## Steps

### Step 1: Fix a Definite Amount of Money

Fix in your mind the exact amount of money you desire. It is not sufficient merely to say "I want plenty of money." Be definite about the amount. Write it down as a clear, concise statement that includes the exact figure, the time limit for its acquisition, what you intend to give in return for the money, and the plan through which you intend to accumulate it.

### Step 2: Determine What You Will Give in Return

Determine exactly what you intend to give in return for the money you desire. There is no such reality as something for nothing. Your plan must include the service, product, or value you will provide to earn the money you seek. This creates the foundation for a fair exchange and ensures your mind is oriented toward contribution rather than mere taking.

### Step 3: Establish a Definite Date

Establish a definite date when you intend to possess the money you desire. A deadline creates urgency and forces your subconscious mind to work toward a specific target. Without a time limit, desire remains a wish rather than a goal with power behind it.

### Step 4: Create a Definite Plan and Begin At Once

Create a definite plan for carrying out your desire and begin at once whether you are ready or not to put this plan into action. Your plan does not have to be perfect when you start, but it must be definite. Ally yourself with a mastermind group of people you need for the creation and carrying out of your plan. Arrange to meet at least twice a week until you have jointly perfected the necessary plans.

### Step 5: Write Your Statement

Write a clear, concise statement of the amount of money you intend to acquire. Name the time limit for its acquisition. State what you intend to give in return for the money. Describe clearly the plan through which you intend to accumulate it. Keep this written statement somewhere you can read it daily.

### Step 6: Read Aloud Twice Daily with Faith

Read your written statement aloud twice daily. Read it once just before retiring at night and read it once after rising in the morning. As you read, see and feel and believe yourself already in possession of the money. This exercise communicates your desire directly to your subconscious mind in a spirit of absolute faith, creating the thought habits that will transmute desire into its monetary equivalent. This practice is a form of auto-suggestion, which is the process of feeding your subconscious mind repeated instructions until it accepts them as truth and acts on them.

### Step 7: Develop Self-Confidence Through Auto-Suggestion

Use the self-confidence formula to overcome doubt and build unshakable belief in your ability to achieve your goal. Auto-suggestion is the method of influencing your subconscious mind by repeating positive affirmations with faith and emotion. Write out a set of positive thought impulses, memorize them, and repeat them until they become part of the working equipment of your subconscious mind. The formula includes statements such as: "I know that I have the ability to achieve the object of my definite purpose in life," and "I will devote time every day to demanding of myself the development of self-confidence."

### Step 8: Use Your Imagination to Create Plans

Use your imagination as the workshop of your mind where all plans are created. Through synthesized imagination, arrange old concepts and ideas into new combinations. Through creative imagination, receive hunches and inspirations from infinite intelligence, which Hill describes as the collective intelligence or universal mind that connects all people and provides insights beyond individual reasoning. The imagination is the faculty that gives shape, form, and action to the impulse of desire.

### Step 9: Reach Decisions Promptly and Change Them Slowly

Develop the habit of reaching decisions promptly and changing them slowly. Analysis of over twenty-five thousand people who experienced failure reveals that lack of decision was near the head of the list of major causes of failure. Procrastination, the opposite of decision, is a common enemy that every person must conquer. Successful people reach decisions quickly and change them slowly.

### Step 10: Build Persistence Through the Mastermind

If you are weak in persistence, focus on surrounding yourself with a mastermind group. Through the cooperation of the members of this group, you can develop persistence. Follow the instructions in auto-suggestion and the subconscious mind chapters until you build up habits that convey to your subconscious mind a clear picture of the object of your desire. Persistence is an essential factor in the procedure of transmuting desire into its monetary equivalent. A mastermind group is a coordinated alliance of two or more people who work together toward a common goal, combining their knowledge, experience, and resources.

### Step 11: Master the Six Basic Fears

Clear your mind of the six basic fears that stand in the way of success: the fear of poverty, the fear of criticism, the fear of ill health, the fear of loss of love, the fear of old age, and the fear of death. Fears are states of mind subject to your control and direction. Nature has endowed human beings with absolute control over only one thing, and that one thing is thought. You can create nothing that you do not first conceive in the form of an impulse of thought.

## Examples

### Example 1: Edwin C. Barnes and Thomas Edison

Edwin C. Barnes had no money, no influential connections, and no formal education when he formed a burning desire to become the business associate of Thomas Edison. He defined his desire with definiteness of purpose, created a plan to reach Edison's laboratory, and persisted despite being told he looked like a tramp. He communicated his desire to his subconscious mind through auto-suggestion, visualized himself in partnership with Edison, and refused to quit. Within a few years, his desire was translated into reality: he was in business with Edison, proving that riches are not beyond reach.

### Example 2: Henry Ford and the Model T

Henry Ford's habit of reaching decisions quickly and definitely and changing them slowly was so pronounced that it gave him the reputation of being obstinate. He continued to manufacture the Model T in only one color—black—when all his advisers urged him to change. This firmness of decision, backed by persistence and a definite plan, yielded a huge fortune before the change became necessary, demonstrating that quick decision-making combined with slow changing is a formula for wealth accumulation.

### Example 3: Charles M. Schwab and U.S. Steel

Charles M. Schwab applied the Carnegie secret by conceiving and carrying out the organization of the giant United States Steel Corporation. The Carnegie secret refers to the principle that any person can achieve their goals by organizing a mastermind group and applying the steps of desire, faith, auto-suggestion, specialized knowledge, imagination, organized planning, decision, persistence, and control of the subconscious mind. Schwab's definite desire, organized planning, and ability to work with others through the mastermind principle made him a huge fortune in both money and opportunity. This single application of the secret was worth approximately 600 million dollars at the time, demonstrating the power of organized effort directed toward a definite end.

## Keep In Mind

- The secret refers to the complete set of principles described in these teachings; it cannot be given away in a single statement but must be discovered and applied through personal experience and effort
- Education has nothing to do with acquiring the secret—Thomas Edison had only three months of schooling, Henry Ford had less than a sixth-grade education
- Poverty and riches are both offspring of thought; choose which one you will nurture
- Money consciousness must be developed intentionally, while poverty consciousness develops without conscious effort
- Weak desires bring weak results just as a small amount of fire makes a small amount of heat
- The subconscious mind works continuously while you are awake and while you are asleep
- Nature has endowed human beings with absolute control over only one thing, and that one thing is thought
- Success requires no explanations; failure permits no alibis

## Security & Safety Notes

- Do not engage in any transaction that does not benefit all parties involved, as no wealth or position can last unless it is built upon truth and justice
- Ensure your mastermind group operates in perfect harmony; power cannot be maintained where disharmony prevails
- Protect your written statement of desire and your self-confidence formula from negative influences or people who would discourage you
- Be cautious of negative auto-suggestion; the subconscious mind takes any orders given to it, whether positive or negative, and translates them into their physical equivalent. Auto-suggestion is the process of consciously choosing which thoughts to feed your mind.
- Do not reveal your definite plans and purposes to people who do not believe in you or who would ridicule your ambitions, as negative suggestions from others can reach your subconscious mind and undermine your efforts

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Being vague about the amount of money you desire
  **Solution:** Fix in your mind the exact amount and write it down in a clear, concise statement. Do not say "I want plenty of money"—state the precise figure.

- **Problem:** Having no plan or a weak plan for acquiring money
  **Solution:** Create a definite plan and begin at once. If your plan fails, replace it with a new one, but never go without a plan.

- **Problem:** Lacking persistence and quitting at the first sign of opposition
  **Solution:** Build a stronger fire under your desires by following the six steps described in Chapter 3 with eagerness. Form a mastermind group to help sustain your persistence.

- **Problem:** Allowing fear of poverty or criticism to paralyze decision-making
  **Solution:** Take inventory of yourself, identify which fears are standing in your way, and use auto-suggestion to replace fearful thoughts with thoughts of courage and faith.

- **Problem:** Changing decisions quickly and often
  **Solution:** Develop the habit of reaching decisions promptly after gathering sufficient facts, and then stick to those decisions until new facts justify a change. This quality is preferable to slowness in reaching decisions and quickness in changing them.

- **Problem:** Neglecting to feed the subconscious mind with positive thoughts
  **Solution:** Follow the instructions for auto-suggestion daily. The subconscious mind is like a fertile garden—if you do not sow seeds of desirable crops, weeds will grow.

- **Problem:** Believing that knowledge acquired in school is enough
  **Solution:** Successful people never stop acquiring specialized knowledge related to their major purpose. Schools teach you how to acquire practical knowledge, but you must continue learning throughout your life.
