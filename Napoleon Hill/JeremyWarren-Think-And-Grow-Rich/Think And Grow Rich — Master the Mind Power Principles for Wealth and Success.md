# Think And Grow Rich — Master the Mind Power Principles for Wealth and Success

## Overview

This teaching distills Napoleon Hill's foundational principles from Think And Grow Rich, focusing on the power of the mind to create wealth, success, and personal fulfillment. It explains that true success begins with definiteness of purpose, a clear written desire, and the disciplined direction of mental energy toward goals rather than fears. By mastering principles such as the mastermind alliance, going the extra mile, applied faith, and a pleasing personality, anyone can unlock their potential and attract the riches—both material and spiritual—intended for them at birth. This framework exists because most people drift through life without clear purpose, allowing external circumstances and negative attitudes to dictate their outcomes rather than exercising their God-given power to choose and attain their own destiny.

## When to Follow These Money Teachings

- When you feel stuck in your current income level and want to promote yourself into a higher-paying position
- When you lack self-confidence to act on an idea that could benefit others and generate wealth
- When you want to improve your relationships and influence at work, home, or in business
- When you are ready to stop complaining about lack of opportunity and start creating your own favorable breaks
- When you want to build lasting success rather than relying on luck or temporary effort
- When you need a structured, step-by-step formula to turn your definite major purpose into reality

## Steps

### Step 1: Procure a Pocket Notebook and Write Your Major Desired Purpose

Get a neat, pocket-sized notebook. On page one, write down a clear description of your major desired purpose in life—the one circumstance, position, or thing you are willing to accept as your idea of success. Before you write, remember that your only limitations are those you set up in your own mind or permit others to set up for you. Be specific and definite; vague wishes do not activate the master key to success.

### Step 2: Write Down What You Intend to Give in Return

On page two of your notebook, write a clear statement of precisely what you intend to give in return for that which you desire from life. Do not wait until you have reached your goal to begin giving. Start right where you stand now to begin providing useful service to others. Wealth and success are attracted to those who create value, not merely to those who desire it.

### Step 3: Memorize and Repeat Both Statements Daily

Memorize both your desire statement and your giving statement. Repeat them at least a dozen times daily. Always anchor your repetitions with gratitude for the blessings with which you were gifted at birth. Use this prayer or a similar expression: "I ask not for divine providence for more riches, but more wisdom with which to accept and use wisely the riches I received at birth in the form of the power to control and direct my mind to whatever ends I desire."

### Step 4: Form a Mastermind Alliance with at Least Two People

Identify at least one person in your immediate family and at least one other person among those to whom you are selling your services or working alongside. Form a mastermind alliance with each—a harmonious partnership in which you work together for the attainment of a definite purpose. Through this alliance, you can borrow and use the education, experience, influence, and perhaps capital of others to accomplish in one year more than you could in a lifetime working alone. Control your mental attitude, make yourself friendly and agreeable, and ensure your purpose benefits all whom you influence.

### Step 5: Practice the Habit of Going the Extra Mile

Starting tomorrow, render some form of useful service to someone near you which you are not expected to render and for which you neither expect nor ask for compensation. Perform this service in a pleasing mental attitude that shows you enjoy doing it. Continue this practice for seven days in succession, without making your plan known to anyone. By the end of the seventh day, you will notice a changed atmosphere in your associations, and you will be happier and better liked by those around you. This habit places you in a position to write your own ticket because you are doing more than you are being paid for, giving you a justifiable reason to ask for promotion, higher pay, or better opportunity.

### Step 6: Cultivate Applied Faith Through Belief and Action

Know what you want and believe that you can and will get it. Give expressions of gratitude many times daily for having received that which you want, even before you physically possess it. Keep your mind open for hunches from within, and when inspired to action, move on your own personal initiative at once. Faith is not something you get; it is something you already have, but you may be using it in reverse by believing in the circumstances and things you do not want. When defeat comes, accept it as nothing more than a challenge to keep on trying. A burning desire, definiteness of purpose, belief, and continuous action are the starting points of all applied faith.

### Step 7: Develop and Maintain a Pleasing Personality

Your personality is your trademark and determines whether people are attracted to you or shy away from you. Ensure your mental attitude is positive, as other people sense this through tone of voice, facial expression, courtesy, and consideration. Cultivate flexibility of mental attitude so that no one can make you angry or irritable without your consent. Control and direct your enthusiasm, turning it on or off at will. Be sincere in all relationships, because sincerity cannot be faked and is quickly detected. Avoid destructive habits such as monopolizing conversations, sarcasm, vanity, indifference while others speak, undeserved flattery, fault-finding, openly challenging others without cause, volunteering unsolicited advice, speaking excessively of your own ailments and problems, conveying superiority through unfamiliar jargon, and envying the success of others.

### Step 8: Exercise Self-Discipline Over Habits and Speech

Gain mastery over your tongue by thinking first and speaking only after you are sure what you say will benefit you and others. A loose tongue is one's greatest liability. Exercise self-discipline in mastering the common tendency to strike back at those against whom you have a grievance, remembering that everything you do to others you do to yourself. Self-discipline is the force that makes the first five principles usable; without it, you cannot maintain the mastermind alliance, go the extra mile, or hold a positive mental attitude under pressure.

## Examples

### Example 1: Kate Smith and Ted Collins

When Kate Smith began her singing career, she had difficulty earning enough from her performances to cover living expenses and perhaps never would have made her singing pay if she had not discovered and applied the mastermind principle. She formed a mastermind alliance with Ted Collins, which gave her access to the master key to success. Through this alliance, Kate Smith earned upwards of fifteen million dollars and remained in the upper brackets of income. This example demonstrates how forming a harmonious partnership with the right person can multiply your earning capacity and propel you into success that would otherwise remain out of reach.

### Example 2: Henry Ford and His Wife

Henry Ford started with very little education and no operating capital, yet he built the great Ford industrial empire. At the beginning of his career, Ford lacked self-confidence and faced ridicule from relatives and neighbors who called his horseless carriage a waste of time. His wife inspired him with faith and courage to continue with the perfection of his vehicle. She formed a mastermind alliance with him, providing the emotional support and belief he needed to persist. Despite having thousands of employees with more education and more magnetic personalities, Ford possessed one simple quality the others lacked: applied faith directed toward a definite purpose. This example illustrates how a mastermind alliance and unshakable belief can overcome a lack of resources, education, and social confidence.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Write your major desired purpose in clear, definite terms in a dedicated notebook and review it daily
- ✅ Form a mastermind alliance with at least one family member and one coworker or business associate
- ✅ Begin giving useful service immediately, right where you stand, without waiting for a promotion or better circumstances
- ✅ Repeat your desire and giving statements with gratitude at least a dozen times every day
- ✅ Go the extra mile in service and mental attitude even when no one is watching or compensating you
- ✅ Control your mental attitude by choosing to think about the riches you want, not the poverty and fear you do not want
- ✅ Keep your mind open for inspired hunches and act on personal initiative without waiting to be told
- ✅ Maintain a pleasing personality by being flexible, enthusiastic, sincere, and a good listener
- ✅ Exercise self-discipline over your speech, emotions, and reactions to maintain harmony in your mastermind alliances
- ✅ View failure as temporary feedback and a challenge to keep trying, not as a reason to quit

## Keep In Mind

- Your only real limitation is the one you accept and set up in your own mind; you have complete control over your mental attitude.
- Whatever the mind can conceive and believe, the mind can achieve, regardless of past failures, education level, or current finances.
- Success is something that must be planned and earned in advance; luck is created by knowing and following the rules.
- Success in the higher brackets of achievement is had only by taking others along with you and helping them acquire what they want without violating their rights.
- The law of increasing returns aids those who render more and better service than is expected, just as nature rewards the farmer who goes the extra mile by returning many times the seed he planted.
- You are where you are and what you are because of your mental attitude in which you relate yourself to other people.
- There is no such thing as something for nothing; the price of success is the effort necessary to follow simple, definite instructions.

## Security & Safety Notes

- Do not share your definite major purpose, your written statements, or your mastermind strategy with anyone who does not share your positive mental attitude or whose interests are not aligned with yours. Negative influence can destroy your faith and deflect you from your purpose.
- Ensure that your definite major purpose and your mastermind alliances are beneficial to all whom you influence. A purpose built on harming or exploiting others will not endure and will ultimately destroy the mastermind alliance through disharmony.
- Guard your mind against feeding on poverty, fear, ill health, failure, and defeat. These are the circumstances you do not want, and giving them attention attracts them to you.
- Do not make known your plan when performing the seven-day extra-mile exercise; perform it naturally and quietly. Publicity at this stage can shift your motivation from service to seeking approval, which reduces the effectiveness of the habit.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** You set a vague or weak purpose and wonder why nothing changes.
  **Solution:** Write your major desired purpose in definite, clear terms in your notebook. A clear picture in your mind of precisely what you want from life is the starting point of all success.
- **Problem:** You neglect to give anything in return for what you desire, expecting something for nothing.
  **Solution:** Write down on page two of your notebook exactly what you intend to give in return for your desire, and begin giving service immediately from where you currently stand.
- **Problem:** You allow your mind to feed on fear, poverty, and worry, attracting the very things you do not want.
  **Solution:** Catch yourself whenever your mind dwells on undesirable circumstances and redirect your thoughts to the six riches you desire: sound health, peace of mind, a labor of love, freedom from fear and worry, a positive mental attitude, and material riches.
- **Problem:** You expect others to cooperate with you while maintaining a negative mental attitude or indifference.
  **Solution:** Control your mental attitude and make yourself friendly and agreeable with everyone with whom you are closely associated. Indifference cannot create a mastermind alliance; negativity brings nothing but failure.
- **Problem:** You perform the extra-mile habit for only a day or two and see no immediate reward, then quit.
  **Solution:** Commit to the practice for seven full days in succession without revealing your plan. The changed atmosphere and increased liking from those around you will become evident by the seventh day, and you will never desire to give up the habit.
- **Problem:** You wait to be told what to do instead of acting on inspired hunches from within.
  **Solution:** Cultivate applied faith by keeping your mind open for inner guidance and moving on your own personal initiative at once when inspired. Faith without action is ineffective.
- **Problem:** You believe success is due to luck rather than following known principles.
  **Solution:** Recognize that luck is something you create for yourself by knowing and following the rules of success. There is a known formula for attainment of success that is as definite and certain as the rules of mathematics or the principles of science.
- **Problem:** You try to go it alone, depending entirely on your own efforts, and accomplish little over a lifetime.
  **Solution:** Form a mastermind alliance. The mastermind principle allows you to accomplish in one year more than you could accomplish without it in a lifetime, by borrowing the education, experience, influence, and capital of others.
- **Problem:** You lack the capacity for belief and quit after a few failures.
  **Solution:** Remember that successful people see the donut around the hole, not just the hole. Thomas Edison failed over ten thousand times before perfecting the incandescent lamp because he believed he could. Develop belief in yourself and in your definite major purpose, and persist through temporary defeat as a challenge to keep trying.
