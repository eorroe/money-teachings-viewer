# Mastermind Principle and Applied Faith for Wealth

## Overview

The mastermind principle is the mechanism through which an individual accesses the full benefit of other members' experience, training, education, specialized knowledge, and influence, as if you possessed those resources directly. This teaching synthesizes the idea that wealth and success beyond what the average person achieves through individual effort alone are attained through an active alliance of two or more minds operating with full mutual trust and aligned intent toward a common objective, combined with applied faith—a positive mental attitude translated into consistent action that converts a clearly defined purpose into physical and financial results.

## When to Follow These Money Teachings

- When you lack education, knowledge, or influence in a field and need to obtain it through others
- When starting a business or professional practice and need to choose collaborators or employees
- When you want to build wealth or achieve beyond what the average person achieves through individual effort alone, and determine that you lack the necessary resources, knowledge, or capacity to do it alone
- When facing defeat, criticism, or adversity and need to maintain courage and persistence
 - When you want to convert setbacks into compensating benefits and keep moving toward a definite major purpose
- When building a marriage, family, or social alliance that can become a source of strength and opportunity
- When you need to select mastermind allies for financial or professional advancement

## Steps

### Step 1: Adopt a Definite Major Purpose

Know exactly what you want in life, including material goals such as a house, a car, or a wardrobe, education for your children, and business objectives. Write these down. A burning desire—a sustained, focused desire that occupies your thoughts and directs your daily actions—is the starting point from which faith is built. Without a clearly stated objective, faith has no direction.

### Step 2: Form a Mastermind Alliance

Begin with one person whose education, experience, and influence are directly relevant to the specific tasks required to achieve your stated major purpose. Let that person help select the next member, and let the growing group select subsequent members. The alliance must be active, not passive— every member must contribute in ways that align with the specific tasks assigned to their role in pursuit of the common objective—whether through mental effort, physical presence, financial resources, or a combination thereof—and must demonstrate commitment through consistent participation.

### Step 3: Select Members Based on Six Essential Traits

Evaluate every potential mastermind ally against these six qualifications. All six must be present; a person who lacks even one should not be invited into the alliance. Evaluate them in the following order:

1. **Dependability** — consistent follow-through on commitments, even when doing so is inconvenient or costly
2. **Loyalty** — commitment to people and obligations they have explicitly agreed to support, without hidden agendas
3. **Ability** — demonstrated capacity to perform the specific tasks the alliance requires
4. **Positive Mental Attitude** — consistently constructive rather than habitually pessimistic or critical in speech and action
5. **Willingness to Go the Extra Mile** — consistent completion of tasks beyond the minimum requirements of their assigned role
6. **Applied Faith** — demonstrated courage through consistent action despite obstacles or uncertainty

Do not invite someone who lacks even one of these traits. An alliance that lacks any one of these traits cannot maintain the conditions required for coordinated effort.

### Step 4: Establish Mutual Trust and Aligned Intent

Mutual trust and aligned intent exist only when each member's relationship to the others is such that every member is willing to share full information and resources toward the common objective without holding back. In a mastermind alliance, no single person dominates decision-making except as a coordinator acting on the group's agreed direction. When one member attempts to impose personal control over the group's decisions or resources, the alliance breaks down. Members should move and act with the shared understanding that the group's objective outweighs any individual preference.

### Step 5: Ensure Reciprocal Benefit

Each member of the alliance must receive a benefit from participation. The form of that benefit may be monetary gain, professional advancement, personal growth, or another form that the member values. Do not invite anyone to participate without first determining what benefit they will receive and confirming that the benefit is sufficient to motivate their continued participation given the time and effort they will commit to the alliance.

### Step 6: Develop Applied Faith Through Daily Practice

Applied faith is active faith, not mere belief. Practice these fundamentals daily:

- **Definiteness of purpose** supported by personal initiative and continuous action
- **A positive mind** free from fear, envy, hatred, jealousy, and greed
- **Mastermind alliance** with people who demonstrate courage through consistent action
- **Recognition** that every adversity contains at least one identifiable benefit that offsets the loss or advances you toward your goal
- **Habit of affirming** your definite major purpose through deliberate, uninterrupted reflection at least once daily
- **Recognition** that sustained, directed thought shapes outcomes
- **Inventory of past defeats** to confirm to yourself that every setback contained a lesson or opportunity that moved you forward

### Step 7: Keep Your Mind Fixed on What You Want

The only thing you have direct control over is your state of mind. To keep your mind from dwelling on things you do not want, redirect it toward things you do want. Discuss your goals and desired outcomes with trusted individuals, visualize yourself in possession of them, and focus on the outcome you intend to create. This directs your attention toward actions and opportunities related to your purpose.

### Step 8: Take Definite Daily Action

Do not let a single day pass without making at least one concrete move toward the attainment of your major purpose. Faith is a positive mental attitude expressed through action. Your actions reflect your mental attitude and produce results consistent with that attitude.

### Step 9: Surround Yourself with Positive Suggestions

Start in the place where you spend the most time—your home, office, or workspace. Put up images of people you consider role models, written reminders of your goals, or any object, image, or reminder that consistently prompts a specific thought related to achieving your major purpose. Design your physical environment to keep your attention directed toward your intended outcome.

## Examples

### Example 1: Andrew Carnegie's Steel Empire

Andrew Carnegie lacked direct knowledge of steelmaking and steel marketing. His contribution to the business was to keep the members of his mastermind alliance—a team of twenty people, including a metallurgist, chief chemist, plant works manager, legal advisor, and chief of his financial staff—working in alignment toward a shared objective. Their combined education, experience, and knowledge represented the full scope of what was known about making and marketing steel at the time. Carnegie's mastermind alliance enabled him to become one of the wealthiest individuals in history.

### Example 2: Franklin D. Roosevelt and the Banking Crisis

During FDR's first term, the mastermind principle was applied on a national scale to address the bank panic. Both houses of Congress worked in alignment with the president, newspaper publishers provided extensive and favorable coverage of recovery messaging, radio broadcasters cooperated despite political differences, and religious institutions across denominations coordinated their efforts. Within weeks, the immediate panic had subsided, and economic activity began to recover.

### Example 3: Napoleon Hill's Early Mastermind Alliance

 In the first decade of his writing career, Napoleon Hill formed a mastermind alliance with two people: Andrew Carnegie and Carnegie's stepmother. Through this alliance, he sustained his philosophy through years during which others criticized him for serving the richest man in the world for 20 years without pay. When he needed encouragement, he could turn to Carnegie or Carnegie's stepmother, who would say, "Stand by your guns," meaning to remain firm in your convictions despite opposition. "You'll come out on top. I know you will." That relationship enabled him to withstand opposition and eventually deliver his philosophy to the public.

### Example 4: Helen Keller's Applied Faith

Helen Keller lost her sight, hearing, and speech at 19 months old, yet she became one of the most formally educated women of her era, having earned a Bachelor of Arts degree from Radcliffe College, and accomplished more than the average person with full use of their senses. She learned to communicate verbally, delivered public speeches, and lived an independent life by sensing vibration through her fingertips. Her achievement demonstrates that directing your mind toward what you want—rather than what you lack—and applying faith through consistent action enables you to overcome significant obstacles.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Start with one mastermind ally and allow the alliance to grow through group selection
- ✅ Evaluate every potential ally against the six essential traits before inviting them in
- ✅ Ensure each member receives a benefit that motivates their continued participation
- ✅ Maintain aligned intent by sharing information openly and avoiding unilateral control
- ✅ Adopt a definite major purpose and write it down before seeking allies
- ✅ Reflect on your purpose daily through prayer or meditation while visualizing yourself in possession of it
- ✅ Take at least one concrete action every day toward your major purpose
- ✅ Keep your attention on what you want by discussing your goals with trusted individuals and expressing gratitude for that outcome as though it is already in your possession
- ✅ Convert every defeat into a benefit by immediately identifying the lesson or opportunity it contains
- ✅ Surround yourself with images of role models, written reminders of your goals, and other positive suggestions in your home and workspace
- ✅ Associate only with people who are dependable, loyal, and supportive of your purpose
- ✅ Do not disclose your aims to anyone who has not demonstrated dependability and loyalty

## Keep In Mind

- The mastermind principle gives you access to the problem-solving and strategic thinking capacities of other members, not just their technical knowledge or financial resources
- Mutual trust and aligned intent require deliberate effort to establish and maintain
- Applied faith means action—faith without corresponding action produces no result
- Your state of mind is the primary factor you control directly; your education, background, nationality, and creed do not determine your ability to achieve
- A burning desire is sustained and focused—it occupies your thoughts and directs your daily actions. Individuals who do not cultivate this sustained quality of desire remain limited to average results.
- Directed, repeated thought shapes your responses and actions; be deliberate about what you repeatedly focus on
- Sustained, directed effort shapes outcomes; your results are limited only by the boundaries you accept in your own thinking
- Faith is guidance, not a substitute for your own effort. You must take action yourself
- Any individual can direct their own mind toward achievement or failure
- Your mental attitude shapes whether adversities become setbacks or stepping stones
- Criticism does not have to slow your progress or harm your results

## Security & Safety Notes

- Do not disclose your definite major purpose, business plans, or ideas to anyone who has not demonstrated dependability and loyalty. People who learn of your plans may act on them before you do
- Be cautious of people who have five of the six essential mastermind traits but lack one; their missing trait can undermine the cooperation and productivity of the alliance
- Never invite anyone to participate in your mastermind alliance without first determining what benefit they will receive in return and confirming that the benefit is sufficient to motivate their continued participation given the time and effort they will commit
- Protect your mastermind alliance from outside interference by keeping its purpose and membership private
- Be cautious of people who are highly educated but not dependable or loyal—such individuals can cause severe damage by betraying trust, leaking confidential information, or withholding critical contributions during crucial moments

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Forming a mastermind alliance that is passive rather than active
  **Solution:** Ensure every member contributes through consistent participation toward the common objective. An alliance that is not active produces no result.

- **Problem:** Choosing mastermind members based on ability alone
  **Solution:** Place dependability and loyalty first, ability third. A skilled but undependable or disloyal person will damage the alliance.

- **Problem:** Expecting faith to produce results without action
  **Solution:** Faith is guidance, not a substitute for action. You must take concrete steps, follow relevant principles, and maintain consistent effort.

- **Problem:** Allowing a single member to dominate the alliance
  **Solution:** One person may serve as leader and coordinator, but must not impose unilateral control. Imposing personal control creates resistance and breaks the conditions required for coordinated effort.

- **Problem:** Giving up when defeated
  **Solution:** Every setback contains a lesson or opportunity. Temporary defeat is not failure unless you accept it as final. Identify the opportunity in every setback immediately.

- **Problem:** Maintaining a negative mental attitude
  **Solution:** Your state of mind is the primary factor you control. Guard it against fear, envy, hatred, jealousy, and greed. Use gratitude, affirmations, and constructive suggestion to keep it oriented toward your intended outcome.

- **Problem:** Waiting until times of need to reflect on your purpose
  **Solution:** Build the habit of reflection and gratitude during periods when you are not under immediate pressure or need. Focus on what you already have and express thanks daily. This orients your attention toward success consistently.

- **Problem:** Letting the mind dwell on what you do not want
  **Solution:** Redirect your focus toward what you do want. Discuss your goals and desired outcomes with trusted individuals, visualize it, write it down, and express gratitude for it as though you already possess it.
