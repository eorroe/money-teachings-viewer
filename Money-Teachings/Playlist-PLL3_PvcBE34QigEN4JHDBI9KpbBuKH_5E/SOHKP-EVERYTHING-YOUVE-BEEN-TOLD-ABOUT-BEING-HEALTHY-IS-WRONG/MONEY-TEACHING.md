## Steps

### Step 1: Recognize That Traditional Medicine Is Designed To Manage Symptoms, Not Cure Disease

Understand that the healthcare system profits from ongoing treatment rather than cures. Doctors are trained to prescribe pills for symptoms without asking about sleep, nutrition, daily habits, hormone levels, or toxin exposure. If you have a chronic condition, the system will manage it indefinitely rather than investigating and removing the root cause.

### Step 2: Stop Eating Processed Foods And Read Every Ingredient Label

Avoid cereals, packaged snacks, and any product with ingredients you cannot pronounce. Chemicals such as Yellow 6, Red 40, and artificial flavors accumulate in your body and cause inflammation, autoimmune dysfunction, and long-term disease. If you cannot read and understand every ingredient on a label, do not put it in your body.

### Step 3: Use Fasting As A Tool For Cellular Repair And Longevity

Fasting is not harmful; it is one of the oldest and most powerful health interventions in human history. Fasting triggers autophagy, the process by which your body eats its own damaged cells and regenerates healthy ones. Studies on calorie restriction and epigenetic clocks show that fasting extends lifespan and improves metabolic health when done correctly.

### Step 4: Understand Epigenetics To Realize You Control Your Health Destiny

Your DNA is not your destiny. Epigenetics shows that your environment, diet, and habits determine which genes are activated or silenced. Telling a patient that their disease is "in their DNA" is unfair because it removes their sense of control. You have the power to change your gene expression through lifestyle choices.

### Step 5: Address Magnesium Deficiency As A Foundational Health Issue

Magnesium deficiency is one of the most common and overlooked causes of modern illness. It contributes to headaches, high blood pressure, atrial fibrillation, and other serious conditions. If you have any of these symptoms, test your magnesium levels and supplement if needed—this single mineral can have a massive impact on your health.

### Step 6: Recognize That Autism And Chronic Disease Rates Are Rising Due To Environmental Toxins

In 1970, autism affected roughly one in 10,000 people. Today it is approximately one in 31. Similar increases are seen across cancer, autoimmune disease, and metabolic disorders. These shifts are not genetic—they are environmental, driven by the chemicals, pesticides, and processed foods that did not exist a century ago.

### Step 7: Build Your Own Health Protocol By Learning From Functional Medicine

Do not wait for the traditional system to save you. Learn functional medicine principles, study natural remedies, and work with practitioners who investigate root causes rather than writing prescriptions. Take responsibility for your own health outcomes by researching, testing, and iterating on what works for your unique body.
