# You Don't Need a College Degree to Get Rich: The Apprenticeship Model

## Overview

This Money Teaching explains why traditional college degrees are not required for building wealth and provides a proven alternative path: the apprenticeship model. Dean Graziosi shares how success is 90% mindset and 10% vehicle, emphasizing that finding the right mentor and working for free can condense decades of learning into days.

## When to Follow These Money Teachings

- When you feel held back by not having a college degree
- When you want to escape an unfulfilling career
- When you are ready to commit to entrepreneurship but lack direction
- When the user asks how to find a mentor or accelerate learning

## Steps

### Step 1: Root Out Limiting Beliefs

Identify the stories you have been telling yourself about why you cannot succeed. Common lies include "I don't have a college degree," "I didn't come from a good area," or "I'm not smart enough." Find proof that these stories are false by researching people who started from your exact position and succeeded.

### Step 2: Find Your Big Enough Why

Your purpose must outweigh fear, impostor syndrome, and peer judgment. Ask yourself what you would do if your brother needed a kidney and you had to get to him immediately. That same urgency must apply to your goals. Without a purpose bigger than the discomfort, you will stay stuck.

### Step 3: Model Proven Practices Through Apprenticeship

Find someone already doing what you want to do. Offer to work for them for free or minimal pay. Become the sponge. Make it so they cannot live without you. This is how you condense decades into days. Read the book Mastery to understand this model deeply.

### Step 4: Pick a Vehicle That Sparks Your Heart

Choose a path that you would stick with even when it gets hard. If you pick something only for the money, you will bail on it when things get difficult. Pick something that lights up your heart and has no glass ceiling.

### Step 5: Build an Unstoppable Mindset

Read personal development books monthly. Feed your mind with success stories. When you have an unstoppable mindset, you can make a bad opportunity work. When you have a weak mindset, you will fail even with an amazing opportunity. The ratio is 90% mindset, 10% vehicle.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Apprentice Approach

Dean's 18-year-old daughter wanted to be an interior designer. He had her research the top three interior designers in Scottsdale, Arizona. She DMed them offering to be the best intern they ever had for free. All three responded, and she had a phone call with one and was meeting another for coffee within days.

### Example 2: The Underdog Advantage

Dean wrote a book called The Underdog Advantage. He argues that every successful entrepreneur felt like an underdog and used it as fuel. When people say "that's not for people like us," you can either accept it or use it as motivation to prove them wrong. Walt Disney, Bill Gates, and every major founder started in garages with no advantages.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Root out subtle stories that hold you back
- ✅ Replace "I can't" with "watch me"
- ✅ Work for free under a master to learn their craft
- ✅ Read at least one personal development book per month
- ✅ Pick a vehicle that sparks your heart, not just your wallet
- ✅ Don't keep score in relationships
- ❌ Don't choose a career only for the money
- ❌ Don't stay in a job you hate because of comfort
- ❌ Don't let the loud minority stop you from your purpose

## Keep In Mind

- Success leaves clues
- 78% of college graduates don't use their degree
- 57% of degree holders hate their jobs
- Only 12% use their degree and like it
- Money is the great equalizer - when you stop worrying about it, you can focus on becoming the person you are meant to be

## Security & Safety Notes

- Don't confuse being "nice" with being "kind"
- Be firm in business decisions; kindness does not mean allowing people to take advantage of you
- Contracts are law - what is written down and signed matters more than verbal agreements

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Procrastination disguised as needing a time management program
  **Solution:** You need a bigger purpose, not better time management
- **Problem:** Trying to do everything yourself and refusing to hire
  **Solution:** You can get to $3-5 million alone, but to build an empire you must learn leadership and hire people who don't think like you
- **Problem:** Getting bored with current success and resenting it
  **Solution:** Don't start diversifying until your current business is profitable, running strong, and can grow without your obsessive focus
- **Problem:** Diversifying too early and becoming a master of nothing
  **Solution:** Perfect one thing first before bolting on another
