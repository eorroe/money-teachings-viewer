# How to Never Be Broke Again by Creating Offers Not Trading Time

## Overview

Myron Golden went from poverty to a world-renowned business consultant and bestselling author by mastering sales, persuasion, and the principle that income follows value created—not hours worked. He teaches that broke people stay broke because they trade time for money, believe time equals money, and refuse to learn from those ahead of them. This Money Teaching gives you the exact mental shifts and offer-creation skills needed to escape the employee mindset forever.

## When to Follow These Money Teachings

- When you feel trapped in a job and want to create income independent of your hours
- When learning to sell, persuade, or create high-value offers
- When you need to overcome the fear of rejection in business
- When the user asks why hard work alone does not make people rich

## Steps

### Step 1: Stop Trading Time for Money

Broke people trade time for money. Wealthy people create offers that buy the things they want. Every transaction in life is an offer: your job is an offer to work for a set wage, your business is an offer to solve a problem for a set price. The moment you realize you are making offers—not trading hours—you can design offers so valuable that people gladly pay you more than your time is worth. Use creativity as your infinite resource instead of labor as your finite one.

### Step 2: Master Persuasion, Not Convincing

Selling is persuasion. Persuasion helps someone make a decision they already want to make for their own reasons. Convincing tries to push someone to do what you want for your reasons. Find the millions of people who already want what you sell and make yourself findable to them. Stop looking for people to convince and start building authority through content, media, and trust so prospects say yes before you even pitch.

### Step 3: Apply the Law of Averages and Law of Large Numbers

Myron learned from Hubert Humphrey that every salesperson has an average close rate—on average, one sale out of ten calls. Most people never talk to enough people to discover their average. If you need ten calls to close one sale, talk to one hundred people and close ten. Volume negates luck. Increase your activity until the math works in your favor regardless of your skill level on any single call.

### Step 4: Serve More Than You Seek to Be Served

The fastest way to gain access to successful people is to seek to serve them before asking for anything. People can feel your vibe. If you approach a conversation thinking "what can I get," the other person senses it. If you approach thinking "how can I make their life better," doors open. Myron mentors people who first demonstrated they cared about him when he had nothing to offer in return.

### Step 5: Build Your Identity on Who You Are, Not Where You Are

Broke people identify with their environment. They see poverty and believe it is who they are. Wealthy people get their identity from God or an unshakable internal standard, so their activity produces prosperity no matter their starting point. Identity produces activity. Activity produces property. If you see yourself as a wealthy person, you will take actions that generate wealth. If you see yourself as poor, you will take actions that keep you poor.

## Examples

### Example 1: From Trash Man to Cash Man

Myron grew up poor, saw his father leave a job to work for himself as a plumber, and realized at age 25 that he did not need a traditional career. He attended a network-marketing opportunity meeting in 1985, met someone making $10,000 per month, and decided he could get rich too. It took him a year and a half to make his first sale, but he eventually became the top salesperson in his office. The turning point was not talent—it was deciding to work on himself instead of a job.

### Example 2: Content Creation Compounding From 400 Posts to Millions

Myron's team posted 400 Instagram videos and had only 50 followers. People mocked them in group chats. Five years later, they have 21 million followers across platforms and their first million-dollar revenue month. The compound momentum of consistent content creation—even when the algorithm and peers punish you—eventually creates unstoppable growth. Most people quit before the seed sprouts.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Design offers that buy what you want instead of trading time for money
- ✅ Make 10 to 30 sales calls per day until your close rate stabilizes
- ✅ Serve first, ask later when approaching mentors or high-level contacts
- ✅ Create content as your modern storefront—if nobody knows you, nobody can buy from you
- ✅ Read the Bible or another anchor text daily to keep your identity grounded
- ❌ Do not believe that time is money—time is infinitely more valuable than money
- ❌ Do not approach sales trying to convince people to buy
- ❌ Do not let other people's opinions of your content stop you from posting

## Keep In Mind

- Price is only an issue if value is absent. Myron charges $40,000 per hour for consulting and $375,000 for a VIP day because he knows the results he delivers. Raise your prices by underpricing first, then waking up to the transformation you provide.
- You do not need a large audience to monetize content. A niche audience of a few hundred ideal prospects is more valuable than a million random followers.
- Balance is not the objective when you are young; focus is. You will be out of balance during seasons of intense growth, and that is okay. Balance comes later when momentum is established.
- Your personal income will never outpace your personal development. If your business is struggling, the problem is you. Work on yourself before blaming the market.

## Security & Safety Notes

- Do not promise results you cannot deliver. Myron underpriced his training early on and realized clients were making half a million dollars from his $500 programs. Charge what the transformation is worth.
- Beware of investors or partners who want to be too involved. The best investors give you capital and then leave you alone unless you ask for help.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Quitting content creation because you only have a few thousand followers or views.
  **Solution:** You do not need a large audience. Paul from Myron's Bible study started a golf channel with 9,000 subscribers and generates $1,500 to $3,000 per month in AdSense revenue plus free equipment from brand deals. Start with the audience you have.
- **Problem:** Believing you are "broke" and that poverty is your identity.
  **Solution:** Poverty is where you are, not who you are. Change your identity by changing your inputs—read books by wealthy people, attend their events, and adopt their standards. Your environment will follow your identity.
- **Problem:** Trying to convince people instead of persuading them.
  **Solution:** Shift from "how do I sell this" to "how do I find the people who already want this." Persuasion removes resistance because you are helping someone decide what they already desire.
