# How to Build Wealth When the System Has Lied to You

## Overview

The traditional education system fails to teach essential money skills, leaving people unprepared for real-world success. This teaching reveals how to reject institutional dependence, start with nothing, and build wealth through problem-solving and relentless execution. Simon Squibb went from homeless at 15 to worth hundreds of millions by realizing that no one was coming to save him.

## When to Follow These Money Teachings

- When you feel the education system has left you unprepared for financial success
- When starting a business with no capital, connections, or certainty
- When seeking purpose-driven wealth beyond just passive income
- When building a team-based business instead of going it alone

## Steps

### Step 1: Reject the Myth That the System Will Save You

The education system teaches you to get a job, not build wealth. School spent 13 years teaching qualifications that did not help when Simon Squibb became homeless at 15. Social services told him to figure it out himself. Accept that no one is coming to save you—you must create your own opportunities.

### Step 2: Start Your First Business With Zero Capital

You do not need money to start. Simon started his first company with 200 pounds by knocking on doors and offering gardening services. Identify a problem someone has, propose a solution, and get paid for it. Wash cars, clean gardens, or offer any service people will pay for.

### Step 3: Build a Team—You Cannot Do It Alone

The school system teaches you to take exams alone, but business requires collaboration. Simon built Hard Knocks with 48 full-time team members. Success is never a solo endeavor. Find partners, hire people, and leverage collective skills.

### Step 4: Focus on Solving Problems, Not Making Money

Every successful business solves a problem. Simon's most successful investments came from founders solving personal pain points, not chasing profits. Ask yourself: what problem bothers me in the world? Build a business around fixing it.

### Step 5: Reject the Passive Income Lie

Money does not free you from work—it means you still have to work, often harder. Simon is worth hundreds of millions but still shows up daily. There is no such thing as passive income. If you love what you do, you will never want a day off.

### Step 6: Bootstrap Before Taking Investment

Build and prove your business model before bringing in investors. The best time to sell your business is when you do not need to sell it. Desperation kills valuation. Richard Harpin learned this when he took investment too early and nearly lost everything.

### Step 7: Ask the Right Question

Stop asking kids "what will you do when you grow up?" That traps minds into predetermined paths. Instead ask: "what problem will you solve?" This opens up infinite possibilities and encourages entrepreneurial thinking from an early age.

## Examples

### Example 1: From Homeless to Multi-Millionaire

Simon Squibb was a pissed-off 15-year-old when his father died and he became homeless. With no qualifications, no money, and no support, he knocked on a door in Cambridge and offered to clean a garden for 200 pounds. That single action launched his entrepreneurial career, eventually building him to worth hundreds of millions and 81 invested companies.

### Example 2: Building Hard Knocks From an iPhone

What is now a 21-million-follower media empire started with nothing but an iPhone. No fancy cameras, no microphones, no equipment. Simon printed flyers, knocked on doors, and made it happen. Today he still does the work, proving that resources follow execution, not the other way around.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Take responsibility for your own success—no one is coming to save you
- ✅ Start before you are ready—perfection is the enemy of progress
- ✅ Build a team—1 + 1 = 11, not 2
- ✅ Solve real problems that bother you personally
- ✅ Bootstrap until your model is proven before raising capital
- ✅ Ask "what problem will you solve?" instead of "what will you do?"
- ❌ Do not rely on the education system for financial literacy
- ❌ Do not chase passive income as an excuse to avoid work
- ❌ Do not build a business just to sell it
- ❌ Do not let fear of failure stop you from starting

## Keep In Mind

- The average age after retirement is five years before health problems begin because people lose their purpose
- 700,000 kids in England alone graduate from university and cannot find a job
- You can only grow to what you are exposed to—expand your environment
- Failure is not just about learning lessons; it is also about compounding knowledge and making money along the way

## Security & Safety Notes

- Do not share sensitive financial information or account credentials in pursuit of quick opportunities
- Verify any investment opportunity thoroughly before committing capital
- Be cautious of anyone selling courses on getting rich—if they are truly rich from their method, they would not need to sell a course

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Waiting for the right moment or enough money to start.
  **Solution:** Start now with what you have. Simon started with 200 pounds and an iPhone.
- **Problem:** Building a business to sell it rather than for long-term value.
  **Solution:** Build something you would never sell. The best exit happens when you do not need it.
- **Problem:** Trying to do everything alone.
  **Solution:** Hire a team, find partners, and leverage other people's skills. No one succeeds alone.
