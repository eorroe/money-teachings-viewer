# Money Teaching Title: Michael Franzese Walked Away From Millions And The Mafia

## Overview

Michael Franzese rose to power in the mafia at a young age, built a criminal empire, then walked away and rebuilt his life through legitimate business and faith. This teaching extracts his timeless principles on negotiation, risk assessment, team building, opportunity recognition, and maintaining composure under pressure.

## When to Follow These Money Teachings

- When negotiating high-stakes deals or resolving conflicts
- When building or leading a team with diverse age groups and experience levels
- When evaluating whether to enter a new market or pursue an opportunity
- When facing rejection, setbacks, or moments that test your resilience
- When mentoring younger team members or seeking reverse mentorship

## Steps

### Step 1: Master the Art of Negotiation

Negotiation is business, not personal. Franzese learned early that losing your composure means losing the argument. Stay prepared, read the room, and never let emotions dictate your response. If a deal goes south, calmly walk away. Frequently, the other party will call you back.

### Step 2: Give Respect to Earn Respect

When Franzese was a young captain in the mafia, older made men resented his authority. He overcame this by deliberately giving them more respect than required. Acknowledge experience, show deference where appropriate, but never allow disrespect to go unchecked. Balance humility with boundaries.

### Step 3: Recognize Opportunity and Bet on People

Franzese's core principle: always bet on a great person, not just a great idea. A great idea executed by the wrong person will fail. Surround yourself with people who buy into the concept and have the skills to execute. Delegate tasks to those who excel at them.

### Step 4: Take Calculated Risks

Franzese describes himself as a risk taker, but his risks are always calculated. Before leaping, assess the downside. In business, avoid get-rich-quick schemes and shiny object syndrome. Experience teaches you to spot real opportunities versus distractions.

### Step 5: Embrace Reverse Mentorship

Age does not determine value. Franzese's entire team is in their mid-20s, and he credits them with his social media success. Young people bring digital fluency and fresh perspectives; older people bring experience and wisdom. Build intergenerational teams and stay humble enough to learn from everyone.

### Step 6: Build Your Brand and Platform

After prison, Franzese spoke in churches and eventually built a massive following through YouTube and social media. He discovered that personal storytelling creates connection. Authenticity and vulnerability attract audiences and customers. Use your platform to share your message, not just to sell.

### Step 7: Anchor Your Life to Something Bigger Than Money

Franzese found faith in solitary confinement and rebuilt his life around principles, not profit. He advises young people to study successful people and notice the common thread of trauma or hardship. Use your past struggles as fuel, not baggage. Integrity and honor create long-term value that money cannot buy.

## Examples

### Example 1: Resolving a Sitdown Without Violence

When a dispute arose between families over a car dealership insult, Franzese avoided bloodshed by giving the opposing party a new car and allowing him to claim victory. He absorbed the cost to preserve peace, demonstrating that negotiation and ego management can achieve better outcomes than confrontation.

### Example 2: Pivoting to Social Media After 44 Canceled Events

When the pandemic canceled 44 speaking events, Franzese's young team convinced him to start a YouTube channel. He initially resisted, thinking he was too old for social media. Within months, the channel exploded, proving that staying open to unfamiliar strategies can unlock entirely new revenue streams.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Prepare thoroughly before any negotiation; know your facts and read the room
- ✅ Delegate to specialists; do what you do best and let others handle the rest
- ✅ Invest in relationships before you need them
- ✅ Stay humble and open to learning from people younger than you
- ✅ Build a personal brand rooted in authenticity and storytelling
- ✅ Take calculated risks, not reckless ones

## Keep In Mind

- Your reputation for fairness and composure is your most valuable asset
- Young people can teach you digital skills; older people can teach you wisdom
- The same principles that governed the mafia—honor, loyalty, preparation—apply to legitimate business
- Faith or a strong moral compass can provide stability when everything else falls apart

## Security & Safety Notes

- Do not engage in illegal activities regardless of perceived low risk or high reward
- Protect your personal safety by avoiding confrontations; use negotiation and legal channels instead
- Be cautious about sharing sensitive business or personal information on social media platforms

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Letting ego drive decisions in negotiations or team management
  **Solution:** Pause before reacting. Ask whether your response serves the long-term goal or just your pride
- **Problem:** Hiring based on credentials alone without assessing character and commitment
  **Solution:** Look for people who believe in your mission. Skills can be taught; passion and integrity cannot
- **Problem:** Ignoring new platforms or strategies because they feel unfamiliar
  **Solution:** Assign young team members to lead experimental projects. Their instincts frequently reveal opportunities you miss
