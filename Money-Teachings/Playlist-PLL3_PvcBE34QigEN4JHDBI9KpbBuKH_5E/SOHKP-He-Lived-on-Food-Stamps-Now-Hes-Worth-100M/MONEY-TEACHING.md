# How Todd Johnson Went From Food Stamps to $100M Net Worth

## Overview

This Money Teaching shares the mindset principles and actionable strategies Todd Johnson used to escape poverty, build multiple companies, and reach a $100 million net worth. From growing up on food stamps in the South during the crack epidemic to building businesses in tech services, construction, real estate, and supplements, Todd's story proves that mindset is the starting point and that hate for your current circumstances can be a powerful motivator for change.

## When to Follow These Money Teachings

- When you grew up with limited resources and want to build a different life
- When you feel stuck and need a mindset reset
- When you are afraid of critique or failure and it is holding you back
- When you want to understand how AI and technology create opportunities for entrepreneurs
- When you need proof that people from difficult backgrounds can achieve extraordinary success
- When you are building or want to build multiple income streams across industries

## Steps

### Step 1: Let Hate for Your Current Circumstances Fuel You

Todd literally hated being poor as a kid. That hatred was not negative; it was fuel. Identify what you are trying to escape and let that pain drive you. Make a commitment that your kids will never experience what you experienced. That emotional commitment creates unstoppable motivation.

### Step 2: Start With Mindset Before Money

Todd says the first step is mindset. Before you can build wealth, you have to believe you deserve it and believe it is possible. If your mindset is stuck in scarcity, no amount of tactical advice will help. Work on your internal belief system before you work on your external strategies.

### Step 3: Take Action Even When You Are Scared

Todd advises his younger self not to be afraid of critique or failure. People are going to talk about you whether you are winning or losing. That is just life. The only way to avoid failure is to never try, and that is the biggest failure of all.

### Step 4: Remember That Everyone Needs You, They Just Do Not Know You Yet

Todd shared one of the best business quotes ever: "Everybody needs you. They just don't know you yet." If you have the goods, the right audience will knock down your door once they discover who you are. Your job is to get in front of them and prove your value.

### Step 5: Embrace Technology and AI as an Entrepreneur

Todd sees massive opportunity in AI for founders and entrepreneurs. AI is not going to put creative people, founders, and entrepreneurs out of jobs. It is a tool that can multiply your output. Learn how to leverage AI to scale your business and create more value.

### Step 6: Build Multiple Companies and Income Streams

Todd has built companies in tech services, construction, real estate, and supplements. He is an entrepreneur, which means he is an artist who loves to create. Do not limit yourself to one industry or one business. Look for opportunities wherever you see them.

### Step 7: Keep Creating and Stay Hungry

Once you make a little bit of money, it feels good. But do not get comfortable. Todd's story is one of continuous creation and continuous building. The moment you stop creating is the moment you start stagnating.

## Examples

### Example 1: Growing Up on Food Stamps

Todd grew up in the South during the crack epidemic with just his mom. They were on food stamps. He hated being poor so much that it became the fuel for everything he has built since. That pain was not a limitation; it was his greatest motivator.

### Example 2: Building Across Industries

Todd has built companies in tech services, construction, real estate, and supplements. He did not stick to one lane. He saw opportunities in different industries and went after them. This diversification is a hallmark of his entrepreneurial approach.

### Example 3: The Quote That Defines His Marketing Philosophy

"Everybody needs you. They just don't know you yet." Todd says this is one of the best business quotes he has ever heard. It summarizes his approach to marketing and sales: get in front of the right audience, prove your value, and they will come running.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Let pain and hatred for your current circumstances fuel your ambition
- ✅ Start with mindset before you work on tactics and strategies
- ✅ Do not be afraid of critique or failure; people will talk about you anyway
- ✅ Remember that your target audience needs you; they just have not found you yet
- ✅ Embrace AI and technology as tools to multiply your entrepreneurial output
- ✅ Build across multiple industries instead of limiting yourself to one
- ✅ Keep creating and stay hungry even after early successes

## Keep In Mind

- Mindset is not a one-time fix. It is a daily practice. Todd had to continuously reinforce his belief that he deserved more.
- Hate for your current situation is a great starting fuel, but you need a positive vision to sustain you long-term. Know what you are moving toward, not just what you are moving away from.
- AI will not replace entrepreneurs, but entrepreneurs who use AI will replace those who do not.
- People will talk about you regardless. Let their criticism fuel you rather than stop you.

## Security & Safety Notes

- Do not let the drive for wealth compromise your ethics. Todd built his success through hard work and creativity, not shortcuts.
- When building across multiple industries, make sure you understand the regulations and legal requirements of each. Do not assume that what works in one industry works in another.
- Diversification is good, but do not spread yourself too thin. Focus on mastering one business before jumping into the next.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Getting stuck in a scarcity mindset because of your upbringing.
  **Solution:** Your past does not determine your future. Use your difficult background as fuel, not as an excuse.
- **Problem:** Being afraid of what people will say.
  **Solution:** People will talk about you whether you are winning or losing. Let their words roll off your back and keep moving forward.
- **Problem:** Limiting yourself to one industry because that is what you know.
  **Solution:** Entrepreneurs are artists who love to create. Explore different industries and find opportunities where you can add value.
- **Problem:** Thinking AI will replace you.
  **Solution:** AI is a tool for entrepreneurs, not a replacement. Learn to leverage it to multiply your output and create more value for your customers.
