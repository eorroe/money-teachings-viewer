You have been praying for a financial breakthrough. Fasting, declaring, believing, and the account is still short at the end of every month. Meanwhile, someone at your office who never opens a Bible just bought his second property. And somewhere quiet, you have been asking the question you are almost afraid to say out loud. God, did the blessing skip me? Here is what nobody told you in church. God already answered that prayer. He answered it in Proverbs. He answered it in Genesis. He answered it in the words of Jesus himself. The blueprint for building wealth is sitting in the book on your nightstand. You just never read it as a financial manual. And I am not talking about tithe and God will bless you. That is real, but that is page one. There are pages you have never turned. Practical, disciplined, step-by-step instructions that work whether you start with thousands or start with nothing. So, if you are tired of being the most prayerful person in the room and still the most financially stuck, stay here. Because what God told you to do, you simply have not done it yet. The Bible mentions money more than 2,300 times. More than faith and prayer combined. God was not being subtle. He wanted you to understand wealth. Not chase it. Not worship it. Understand it. And he gave you a system. Step-by-step, page-by-page. Most Christians have never read it as what it actually is. A financial manual written by the creator of everything that exists. Here are the biblical steps to building wealth. Not prosperity gospel. Not name it and claim it. These are disciplinary instructions from scripture that work regardless of where you start. Step one, sharpen your blade before you swing it. Proverbs 22:29 says, "Do you see a man skilled in his work? He will stand before kings." Before God gave Israel wealth, he gave Bezalel skill. In Exodus 31, the Holy Spirit filled that man not with prophecy, not with tongues, but with craftsmanship, ability, and intelligence in his hands. That is the first time the spirit of God filled a man in all of scripture, and it was for work. You do not need capital to start. You need capability. The most financially struggling person watching this right now has access to something worth more than any startup loan. Time to learn. A skill is a seed that does not cost money to plant, but once it grows, people will pay for its fruit. Ecclesiastes 10:10 says, "If the iron is blunt and you do not sharpen it, you must use more strength." You are working twice as hard because you never sharpen the tool. Fix that first. Tonight, pick one skill the market will pay for. Writing, design, coding, cooking, editing, tailoring. YouTube is free. Apprenticeships are free. Books at the library are free. You have no excuse and no reason to wait. Step two, get up and work like the ant. Proverbs 6:6 says, "Go to the ant, you sluggard. Consider its ways and be wise. No commander, no overseer, no ruler." The ant does not wait for a boss. It does not wait for motivation. It does not wait for a prophetic word. It sees summer and it moves because it knows winter is coming. James 2:26 says, faith without works is dead. Not weak, not struggling, dead. And a dead faith cannot produce a living income. Here is what broke Christians get wrong. They spiritualize laziness. They say they are waiting on God, but God is waiting on them. The widow in 2 Kings chapter 4 was drowning in debt with creditors at her door. She cried out for help, and God's miracle required her to move first. She had to go borrow the jars. She had to pour the oil herself. She had to sell it herself. God multiplied it, but she had to move. And the oil stopped flowing the moment she ran out of jars. Her preparation determined the size of the miracle. Your effort is not the opposite of faith. Your effort is the evidence of it. Step three, count the cost before you build. Jesus said in Luke 14, "Suppose one of you wants to build a tower. Won't you first sit down and estimate the cost to see if you have enough money to complete it?" Jesus told you to budget. The son of God literally said, "Sit down and count." Not pray about it first. Count. Calculate. Plan. Joseph did not just pray Egypt through the famine. He implemented a 20% savings plan across an entire nation for 7 years. That is a budget. That is a financial strategy. That is discipline. Proverbs 21:5 says, "The plans of the diligent lead to profit as surely as haste leads to poverty." You cannot manage a million if you cannot manage what is in your hand right now. If money comes in and disappears and you do not know where it went, you do not have an income problem. You have an an problem. Write down every single thing you spend for the next 30 days. Every single thing. You will find the leak. And the leak is what is bleeding you. Step four. Kill debt before it kills your future. Proverbs 22:7 says, "The borrower is slave to the lender." Slave. Not inconvenienced, not slightly limited, slave. Every debt you carry is a master you serve. Every payment you owe is a portion of your future that belongs to someone else. You cannot build wealth while you are financing someone else's. Romans 13:8 says, "Let no debt remain outstanding." Deuteronomy 28 says, "Being debt-free is a mark of God's blessing, and being in debt is listed among the curses." So, here is the practical plan. List every debt from smallest to largest. Attack the smallest first. Pay the minimum on everything else, and throw every extra dollar at the smallest balance. When it is dead, take what you were paying on it and stack it onto the next debt. Little by little, debt by debt, chain by chain, until you are free. The first four steps are about clearing the ground. Sharpening yourself, moving with purpose, planning with discipline, and breaking every chain that is holding you back. But, the next five steps are about building something on that cleared ground that actually lasts. These are the steps most people never reach because they got stuck in step four. Keep going. Step five. Gather little by little and let it multiply. Proverbs 13:11 says, "Whoever gathers money little by little makes it grow." This verse destroys every get-rich-quick scheme. Every double-your-money-in-30-days promise. Every plan that skips patience. God said the path to growth is small, consistent, faithful accumulation. Not one big moment, a thousand small ones. Jesus took this further in the parable of the talents in Matthew 25. The master gave three servants money. The first two invested and doubled what they had. The third buried his in the ground and played it safe. The master did not call him cautious. He called him wicked and lazy for doing nothing with what he was given. God expects multiplication. Not storage, not survival. Even if all you can save right now is 5% of your income, save it. Then put it to work. A hundred saved consistently becomes a thousand. A thousand becomes 10,000. This is not magic. This is the math of patience. And patience is a biblical command. Step six. Spread your portion across more than one source. Ecclesiastes 11:2 says, "Invest in seven ventures. Yes, in eight. For you do not know what disaster may come." God told you not to depend on one income. That is not hustle culture. That is scripture. Solomon, the wisest and wealthiest man who ever lived, said, "Spread it out because you do not know what tomorrow holds." The job could end. The industry could shift. The economy could turn. But if you have planted in seven places, you eat from the ones that survive. The Proverbs 31 woman ran real estate, agriculture, manufacturing, and wholesale trade simultaneously. Four income streams, one woman. And verse 18 says, she saw that her trading was profitable. She did not hope it was working. She checked the numbers. Ecclesiastes 11:4 says, whoever watches the wind will not plant. Stop waiting for perfect conditions. You will never feel fully ready. Start the side business. Offer the service. Sell the skill you sharpened in step one. The Bible does not despise small beginnings. Zechariah 4:10 says, do not despise the day of small things. Step seven. Guard your walls or lose everything you built. Proverbs 25:28 says, like a city whose walls are broken through is a person who lacks self-control. This is the step that separates the person who earns well and stays broke from the person who earns modestly and builds wealth. Without self-control, everything leaks. Impulse purchases, lifestyle inflation, emotional spending. The pressure to look blessed before you actually are. Proverbs 21:17 says, whoever loves pleasure will become poor. The designer brands, the daily coffee run, the subscription you forgot you were still paying for. These things are not sin, but they are the slow leak that empties the bucket. Galatians 5 calls self-control a fruit of the spirit. This is not just financial advice. It is spiritual warfare. Every time you say no to something you do not need, you are saying yes to the future God is building for you. Step eight. Walk with the wise and stop consulting fools. Proverbs 13:20 says, walk with the wise and become wise, for a companion of fools suffers harm. Your circle is your ceiling. If every person around you is broke bitter, you will absorb broke and bitter thinking. Not because they are bad people, because environment shapes behavior. Proverbs 15:22 says, "Plans fail for lack of counsel, but with many advisers, they succeed." You need a mentor. Not a motivational speaker. A mentor. Someone who has walked the road you are trying to walk and can show you where the pits are. And you do not need to pay for one. Ask questions of people further along than you. Listen more than you talk. Apply what you learn. Proverbs 27:17 says, "As iron sharpens iron, so one person sharpens another." Find that person. Step nine. Build with clean hands or watch it crumble. Proverbs 11:1 says, "The Lord detests dishonest scales, but accurate weights find favor with him." God used the word detests. That is the same Hebrew word used for idolatry. He places dishonest business practices in the same category as worshiping false gods. If your wealth strategy involves cutting corners, misleading people, or profiting from someone else's ignorance, you are not building. You are stacking cards on a table that will not hold. Proverbs 13:11 already told you what happens. Dishonest money dwindles away, but whoever gathers money little by little, honestly, consistently, faithfully, makes it grow. Your reputation is worth more than your revenue. Proverbs 22:1 says, "A good name is more desirable than great riches." And Proverbs 13:22 says, "A good person leaves an inheritance for their children's children." That is generational wealth. And it requires integrity as its foundation. Let me say this to you directly. If you have been sitting in church praying for breakthrough and wondering why the blessing has not landed, it is not because God is ignoring you. It is because God does not reward wishes. He rewards obedience. Deuteronomy 8:18 says, "Remember the Lord your God, for it is he who gives you the ability to produce wealth." He did not say he gives you wealth. He said he gives you the ability, the capacity, the power. But you have to use it. The seed is in your hand. The ground is in front of you. He has provided the rain and the sun. But nobody can plant it for you. You are not cursed. You are not forgotten. You are not destined to stay where you are. You are simply standing in front of a blueprint you have not followed yet. Start tonight. Pick a skill. Make a plan. Kill a debt. Save something. Start something. Just begin. Before you go, I want to ask you one honest question. Of these nine steps, which one have you been avoiding? Not the one that sounds hardest. The one you know in your gut you have been putting off. Drop just the number in the comments. Because naming the step you have been skipping is the first move toward actually taking it.
