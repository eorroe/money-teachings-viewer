# Make a Million Dollars with AI Automation

## Overview

This Money Teaching presents a three-step blueprint for generating a million dollars using AI automation without writing any code. It focuses on targeting boring markets, protecting gross margins above 80%, and pre-selling solutions before building anything. The video also includes a 5-day pre-selling sprint and a 9-step sales system designed to close deals efficiently.

## When to Follow These Money Teachings

- When you want to build a profitable AI automation business without writing code
- When targeting underserved or "boring" industries with high margins
- When you need a repeatable sales process to close high-ticket deals
- When you want to validate demand before investing time or money into a product
- When you need to protect margins while scaling an AI-powered service business

## Steps

### Step 1: Target a Boring Market

Choose a boring, growing industry with low competition and high willingness to pay ($5,000–$10,000 for AI automation). Use AI tools such as ChatGPT to research your target city or region and identify industries such as trades, law firms, dental or medical practices, or farming segments that lack AI adoption. Then conduct pain point research by reading reviews, studying the industry, and directly speaking with business owners to uncover their biggest bottlenecks. Finally, map those pain points to existing AI tools you can implement for them—such as using your.com, an AI phone receptionist that qualifies leads and books appointments 24/7, to solve missed calls for busy plumbers. Focus on one market and one pain point to start.

### Step 2: Set Your Gross Margins

Ensure your business maintains margins well above 80% by applying four rules: (1) Set a price floor by multiplying your delivery cost by 5x to hit 80% margin—for example, $2,000 cost becomes a $10,000 price. (2) Productize your offer so you repeat the same automation instead of building custom solutions for every client. (3) Scope the outcome clearly in writing so customers do not request unpaid extras, and use change orders for any additions. (4) Monitor margins continuously using AI tools such as HelloFrank.ai to alert you if costs push your margin below 80%. Margins are what you actually keep, so protect them aggressively.

### Step 3: Pre-Sell Your Solution

Do not build before you have a paying customer. Use a 5-day AI-assisted pre-selling sprint to validate demand first. On Day 1, draft a one-page offer using ChatGPT that leads with a specific outcome (for example, "10 qualified appointments in 30 days without you answering a single phone call"), defines your unique mechanism by name, creates scarcity with limited pilot spots, and includes a risk reversal such as "first appointment scheduled in 7 days or your money back." On Day 2, build a list of at least 100 target companies using AI to research LinkedIn and save the results in a Google Sheet with contact details. On Days 3 and 4, reach out to buyers with a short cold call script that states who you help, what result you deliver, the pilot investment, and a simple ask to send the one-pager. Use your.com to handle cold calls or answer inbound qualifying calls on your behalf. On Day 5, run the closing call using the 9-step Rocket Selling System outlined below.

### 9-Step Sales Process (Rocket Selling System)

**Step 1: Setup.** Do not waste time on small talk. Position yourself as an expert by setting a clear agenda and auditioning the buyer—let them know you will determine quickly whether you can help.

**Step 2: Learn About the Customer.** Ask who they are, what they sell, how much they charge, and what problem they are trying to solve so your solution feels personalized.

**Step 3: The Decision.** Ask two questions: "Why did you decide to get on this call?" and "Why now?" These questions get the buyer to convince themselves to work with you.

**Step 4: The Results.** Understand their desired future—revenue goals, freedom goals, and how they want to feel in their business—to create a motivational gap.

**Step 5: The Reality.** Learn where they are today, including team size, ownership structure, and current workflow, so you can qualify them properly.

**Step 6: The Roadblocks.** Discover what has stopped them from achieving their goals. Stretch the gap between their current pain and their desired future; that tension is where the sale is made.

**Step 7: The Model.** Present your named process, methodology, or system. Referencing your unique mechanism again makes you look like a proven expert rather than someone guessing.

**Step 8: The Offer.** Match your offer directly to the pain points they shared. Show how your solution removes the specific roadblocks they mentioned.

**Step 9: The Ask.** Ask for the deal three times no matter what they say. Most people ask once and give up. If they need more time, book a follow-up meeting before ending the call using BAMF fam (Book A Meeting From A Meeting): confirm a future calendar invite, send an email, and secure a verbal commitment so they are less likely to ghost. People want to be consistent with the commitments they made to you, which reduces no-shows.

## Examples

### Example 1: AI Phone Automation for Plumbers

A plumber is too busy working to answer inbound calls, so missed calls turn into lost jobs. You research the market, confirm the pain point, and implement your.com, an AI call-answering tool that qualifies leads and books appointments 24/7. You charge a $5,000–$10,000 setup fee plus a monthly management fee. The plumber pays because the tool directly recovers lost revenue.

### Example 2: Pre-Selling a Crowdfunding-Style AI Offer

Before building anything, you create a one-page offer promising a specific outcome—such as 10 qualified appointments per month with no phone calls required. You reach out to 100 local business owners, run closing calls using the 9-step system, and collect payment before delivering the work. If no one buys, you refund and pivot. If people buy, you fund the implementation with their money.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Target boring, growing industries where AI adoption is low and budgets exist
- ✅ Always pre-sell before building to eliminate risk
- ✅ Keep delivery costs low and price at 5x your cost to protect margins
- ✅ Productize your offer so delivery is repeatable and scalable
- ✅ Ask for the deal three times—never stop at the first "no" or "maybe"
- ✅ Use AI to draft offers, build prospect lists, and write personalized outreach snippets
- ✅ Book the next meeting before hanging up the phone to prevent ghosting

## Keep In Mind

- Revenue is vanity—what you keep after costs is what matters
- AI automation tools already exist; your job is to sell and manage the implementation, not to build technology from scratch
- Pre-selling is normal in many industries, from consulting retainers to crowdfunding hardware
- The window of opportunity for AI automation in boring markets is open now, but it will not last forever
- Asking for advice from potential customers is one of the fastest ways to turn research calls into sales calls

## Security & Safety Notes

- Do not share sensitive client business data or financial details in AI tools without consent
- Verify that any AI tool you use for calls, scheduling, or data entry complies with local privacy regulations
- Use written agreements and change orders to scope work clearly and avoid disputes over unpaid scope creep
- Keep client payment and scheduling data separate from personal AI tool accounts

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Building the product before finding a customer
  **Solution:** Always pre-sell first using a one-page offer and a 5-day outreach sprint before investing in implementation

- **Problem:** Margins being destroyed by custom client requests
  **Solution:** Scope outcomes clearly upfront, say no to unpaid additions, and offer change orders with fees for extra work

- **Problem:** Losing motivation because of low response rates
  **Solution:** Start with at least 100 qualified targets and use AI to write personalized outreach so volume and quality stay high

- **Problem:** Giving up after the first "no" in a sales call
  **Solution:** Ask for the deal three times, address concerns, and always book a follow-up before ending the conversation

- **Problem:** Trying to build AI technology instead of implementing existing tools
  **Solution:** You do not need to write code—find existing AI automation tools, implement them for clients, and charge for the setup and management
