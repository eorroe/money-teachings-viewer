# If You Want to Be Wealthy & Happy...

## Overview

This money teaching by GrindBuddy explains that lasting wealth and happiness come from personal development, not from chasing money directly. The core message is that income rarely exceeds personal development, and true happiness is found in who you become rather than what you acquire. The video provides a three-step framework for personal development and three practical methods for acquiring the ideas that can change your economic, social, spiritual, and personal life.

## When to Follow These Money Teachings

- When you feel stuck financially and need to understand why income growth has plateaued
- When you want to build lasting wealth instead of relying on lucky breaks or windfalls
- When you are ready to invest in yourself through reading, listening, and observing successful people
- When you need a framework for discipline, self-motivation, and consistent daily action
- When you believe happiness comes from external circumstances rather than internal growth

## Steps

### Step 1: Find Out How Things Work

Personal development begins with understanding how things work. The speaker explains that the major problem most people face is not a lack of money, but a lack of ideas on how to create money and wealth. To get ideas, you must engage in constant study and finding out. Study success if you wish to be successful, study happiness if you wish to be happy, and study wealth if you wish to be wealthy. Do not leave your future to chance. When you discover something that works, write it in a journal rather than relying on your memory. Repeat the information through daily review. Repetition allows ideas to take root and eventually show up in your bank account, your appearance, your personality, and your lifestyle.

### Step 2: Go to Work

Knowledge without action is insufficient. You must now take action on what you have found out. The key to discipline is to start with small disciplines and get right on them, because small disciplines lead to big ones. You cannot handle the big challenges in life unless you take on the little ones. Make a list of all the things you can do and discipline yourself for those things. Discipline yourself both for the results and for the muscle and for the practice, so that when life hands you big challenges, you will be ready. Self-motivation is also essential here. It is the only kind of motivation that exists. You cannot rely on someone else to motivate you. You must have a better plan for your life. Most importantly, do not let your learning lead only to knowledge. Let your learning lead to action, because action is what creates wealth. Wealth is not just money. Money is one of the least of all values.

### Step 3: Don't Try to Beat the System

The third step is a caution. Find out how the system works, work it, but do not try to beat it. Some people learn just enough to start slicing, shading, thinning, cutting corners, and looking for cheap answers. Do not fall for that, because you will wind up with a cheap life. Find out how it works best and do it that way. Even though it may seem to take a little longer, do it right. Do not compromise with right.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Million-Dollar Windfall

If somebody hands you a million dollars, the best thing you can do is become a millionaire quickly by working on yourself. Otherwise, the money will disappear. This illustrates the principle that income does not far exceed personal development. Without the personal development to match the income, the money returns to where it came from.

### Example 2: Dividing All the Money Equally

Somebody once observed that if you took all the money in the world, divided it up equally among everybody, it would soon all be back in the same pockets. This demonstrates that success is something you attract through personal development, not something you pursue by chasing money directly.

### Example 3: Taking Rich People Out to Dinner

One of the best pieces of advice given in the video is that poor people ought to take rich people out to dinner. If you get a successful person to eat and talk with you for two hours, they are liable to drop ideas in your lap that can change your life and multiply your income by two, by three, or by five. This is an actionable example of the "listen" method.

### Example 4: The $10,000 Walk

If a guy is making $10,000 a month, watch how he walks. Success leaves clues. Copying the habits and behaviors of successful people, even small things like how they walk, can be the beginning of your own success. This illustrates the "observe" method of learning.

## Best Practices

- Read at least 30 minutes every day. Read something positive, something challenging, something inspirational, and something instructional.
- Become self-educated rather than relying solely on standard education, because standard education produces standard results.
- Keep a journal to capture useful ideas and information. Review it regularly through repetition.
- Get around successful people and listen to what they say and how they say it.
- Learn from both successful and unsuccessful people. Take notes on both the negative and the positive.
- Take a successful person out to dinner once in a while. Spend money on access to their mind.
- Observe successful people closely. Watch how they shake hands, how they respond, and how they carry themselves. Success leaves clues.
- See with both your eyes (sight) and your mind (insight). Pay attention and do not miss anything.
- Start with small disciplines to build the muscle needed for big challenges.
- Motivate yourself. Do not rely on others to turn you on or keep you going.
- Let your learning lead to action, not just to knowledge.
- Find out how the system works and work it properly. Do not try to beat it with shortcuts.
- Do it right even if it takes longer. Do not compromise with right.

## Keep In Mind

- Income does not far exceed personal development. If you get a lucky jump in income without growing personally, it will usually come back to where you are.
- True happiness is not contained in what you get. Happiness is contained in what you become.
- Success is something you attract, not something you pursue. Work on yourself and success will follow.
- The problem is not a lack of money. The problem is a lack of ideas on how to create money and wealth.
- Wealth is not just money. There are many kinds of wealth, and money is one of the least of all values. Some people with a lot of money are very poor.
- Self-motivation is the only kind of motivation there is. You cannot outsource motivation to someone else.
- You do not have to be the same person after tonight. Change is a choice. Starting tomorrow, you can change your whole life.

## Security & Safety Notes

- Avoid the temptation to look for shortcuts, cheap answers, or ways to beat the system. These lead to a cheap life.
- Do not rely on luck or chance for financial success. Make personal development a deliberate study.
- Be cautious of people who promise quick wealth without personal growth. Quick money without personal development usually disappears just as quickly.
- Do not compromise your integrity by cutting corners. Do it right, even when it takes longer.
- Do not let the desire for fast results cause you to abandon the process of learning and disciplined action.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Waiting for luck or chance to bring financial success instead of studying and preparing.
  **Solution:** Make a study of wealth, success, and happiness. Read 30 minutes a day and take consistent action.
- **Problem:** Thinking the solution is more money when the real problem is a lack of ideas.
  **Solution:** Shift your focus from acquiring money to acquiring ideas. Ideas create money and wealth.
- **Problem:** Letting learning become an end in itself, leading to knowledge without action.
  **Solution:** Let every learning experience lead to action. Action is what creates wealth and change.
- **Problem:** Relying on other people to motivate you instead of developing self-motivation.
  **Solution:** Take full responsibility for your own motivation. Build a plan for your life that does not depend on others to inspire you.
- **Problem:** Trying to beat the system by cutting corners, slicing, shading, or looking for cheap answers.
  **Solution:** Find out how the system works best and do it that way. Do it right and do not compromise with right.
- **Problem:** Only learning enough to get by instead of committing to continuous personal development.
  **Solution:** Commit to becoming self-educated. Read books by successful people, listen to them, and observe their habits daily.
- **Problem:** Neglecting small disciplines and therefore being unable to handle big challenges.
  **Solution:** Start with small disciplines. Make a list of things you can do and get right on them. Small wins build the muscle for big wins.
