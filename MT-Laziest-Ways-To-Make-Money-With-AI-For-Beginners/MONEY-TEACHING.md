# Laziest Ways to Make Money with AI (For Beginners)

## Overview

This guide covers seven beginner-friendly AI-powered money-making methods based on Mark Tilbury's video "Laziest Ways to Make Money with AI (For Beginners)." Each method is ranked from most competitive (Method 1) to least competitive (Method 7). All seven methods allow you to leverage AI tools that perform 70–95% of the work, requiring minimal prior skills or startup capital.

## When to Follow These Money Teachings

Follow these teachings when you are a beginner looking to start an online side income or one-person business using AI tools, have limited startup capital (under $50/month), want to work from anywhere, and prefer methods where AI handles most of the execution. These methods are appropriate when you have 1–3 hours per day to invest and are willing to learn basic AI tool workflows. Do not follow these teachings if you expect to earn a full living income in week one — each method requires consistency and some client or audience development.

## Steps

### Method 1: AI Clipping

AI clipping involves getting paid to chop long-form videos into short viral clips for platforms like TikTok and Instagram Reels. Big creators have hours of podcast, YouTube, and livestream content and need short clips to reach new audiences. They will pay you per view because the exposure earns them more than they pay out.

**Step-by-step instructions:**

1. Find creators or campaigns that will pay you to clip their content. Use a marketplace such as Content Rewards to browse available campaigns. Look for campaigns with viral potential and clear instructions on what content to clip.
2. Download the source long-form video footage provided by the campaign.
3. Drop the video into an AI clipping tool. Recommended free tool: Opus Clip. Alternatives: Crayo or CapCut. The AI will identify the best moments, cut the clips, add captions, and format them for each platform.
4. Post the clips daily to TikTok Shorts and Instagram Reels.
5. Submit your clip links on the marketplace and get paid based on view count. For example, a campaign may pay $1 per 1,000 views.
6. Double down on whatever clip style or content type is performing best.
7. Take on additional campaigns and post across multiple accounts to scale earnings.

**Learning curve:** ~3 days
**AI contribution:** ~90%
**Startup capital:** ~$15/month for a premium AI editing platform (free tools available to start)
**Tool stack:** 2 tools — a marketplace to find campaigns + an AI clipping tool (Opus Clip, Crayo, or CapCut)
**Competition level:** Very high

### Method 2: AI Ghost Writing

AI ghost writing involves using AI to write social media posts, newsletters, and ads for businesses, creators, or entrepreneurs. Clients know they need content but lack the time to write it. You provide the service by combining AI speed with human judgment and niche expertise.

**Step-by-step instructions:**

1. Pick your lane and niche. Choose one content type (social media posts, newsletters, website copy, or Facebook ads) and one niche (finance, fitness, tech startups, real estate, etc.). Specialization allows you to charge higher rates.
2. Set up a dedicated AI workspace for each client. Sign into Claude, create a new Project, name it after the client, and upload all gathered materials (old social posts, past newsletters, podcast appearances, interviews). Click "Set Project Instructions" and write detailed prompts so the AI outputs content that sounds exactly like the client, not a generic robot.
3. Use a research tool like Perplexity to pull the latest industry news each week. Example prompt: "What are the biggest stories in electric vehicles this week?" Use the results to keep content fresh and relevant.
4. Prompt Claude to draft content using your research, then add a human touch by editing out generic AI phrasing, selecting the best angles, and ensuring the tone matches the client's voice.
5. Use a scheduling tool to deliver or post content. For social media: Typefully. For email newsletters: Kit.

**Learning curve:** 1–2 weeks
**AI contribution:** ~75%
**Startup capital:** ~$20–$40/month in AI subscriptions
**Tool stack:** 3 tools — Perplexity (research), Claude (writing), Typefully or Kit (scheduling)
**Competition level:** High

### Method 3: AI Websites

AI websites involves building high-converting landing pages for businesses using AI page builders with no coding required. A landing page is a one-page site designed to sell one offer. Course creators, coaches, agencies, and software companies use them to convert ad and content traffic into customers.

**Step-by-step instructions:**

1. Pick an AI page builder. Beginner-friendly options: Wix or Hostinger AI. Learn the tool by building a sample landing page for an imaginary offer (for example: "landing page for a personal trainer selling an online coaching program, clean and modern with a bold call to action"). Use this as proof of your ability.
2. Find businesses that have something to sell and would benefit from a high-converting landing page. Target course creators, coaches, agencies, or software companies that run ads or content funnels.
3. Charge clients per page initially. After delivering the page, offer ongoing optimization as a monthly retainer. Some AI builders include A/B testing features that automatically test different page versions and optimize for conversions.

**Learning curve:** 2–4 weeks
**AI contribution:** ~80%
**Startup capital:** ~$30/month (many builders offer free trials)
**Tool stack:** 1 tool — an AI website builder (Wix, Hostinger, or similar)
**Competition level:** Medium

### Method 4: AI Built Shopify Store

An AI-built Shopify store is an automated e-commerce setup where AI picks products, builds the shop, writes listings, and manages order fulfillment. The store runs largely passively with no inventory management or packaging required.

**Step-by-step instructions:**

1. Go to Build Your Store (free AI tool) and click "Start Your Free Store."
2. Enter basic details (name, email) and choose a niche from popular options such as fashion, electronics, or pets.
3. Follow the guided setup prompts. The tool walks you through every step of building the store.
4. Connect AutoDS to your Shopify store. AutoDS uses AI to populate the store with winning products, keep prices and images updated, and ship orders directly to customers.
5. Once the store is live, direct traffic to it through social media, ads, or organic content.
6. Monitor performance and let the AI handle ongoing product management and fulfillment.

**Learning curve:** Near zero
**AI contribution:** ~95%
**Startup capital:** ~$0.99 to start (Build Your Store is free, Shopify offers a 3-day free trial, AutoDS starts at $0.99)
**Tool stack:** 3 tools — Build Your Store (store setup), Shopify (hosting), AutoDS (product sourcing and fulfillment)
**Competition level:** Medium

### Method 5: AI Agents

AI agents involve setting up automated digital employees for local businesses. The simplest and most in-demand type is an AI receptionist that answers calls, books appointments, and captures leads 24/7. Small businesses miss over 50% of incoming calls daily, making this a high-value service.

**Step-by-step instructions:**

1. Pick one type of local business to target. Examples: dentists, hair salons, plumbers.
2. Use an AI agent tool to build a demo AI receptionist with its own phone number. Recommended tool: Dialzara. Create an account, describe the business and its services, and generate the receptionist.
3. Test the AI receptionist with a live call to verify it answers questions, collects lead information, and books or summarizes appointments correctly.
4. Contact local businesses in person or online. Let the business owner hear the AI receptionist answer a live call. The demonstration typically sells the service on its own.
5. Charge a monthly recurring fee to keep the AI receptionist running.
6. Upsell additional AI agents such as chatbots.
7. Repeat the process with more businesses to scale.

**Learning curve:** 1–2 months
**AI contribution:** ~85%
**Startup capital:** ~$50/month depending on the tool and usage
**Tool stack:** 1 tool to start (Dialzara or similar); add more tools as you expand agent offerings
**Competition level:** Low

### Method 6: AI Avatars

AI avatars involves creating virtual AI influencers that look like real people but do not exist. These avatars can be used to sell products, build niche audiences, and earn through sponsorships and affiliate income. They eliminate the need to film, maintain a personal brand appearance, or deal with on-camera consistency.

**Step-by-step instructions:**

1. Go to Higgsfield AI and open the AI Influencer Studio.
2. Design your avatar. Select human as the character type, then adjust gender, ethnicity, skin color, eye color, and other features using sliders and dropdowns. No complicated prompting is required.
3. Generate the influencer and review the output. Iterate on the design until the avatar looks realistic and consistent.
4. Create a content strategy for the avatar. Decide on a niche, content style, and platform(s) to target.
5. Use the AI avatar tool to generate photos and videos of the avatar. Add scripts and personality to the avatar to create engaging content.
6. Post consistently to build an audience. Once the avatar has traction, pursue sponsorship deals and affiliate promotions.

**Learning curve:** 2–3 months
**AI contribution:** ~70%
**Startup capital:** ~$19/month (varies based on video volume)
**Tool stack:** 1 tool — Higgsfield AI (or similar AI avatar platform); optional additional tools include Claude for script writing and a content scheduler
**Competition level:** Low

### Method 7: AI App Building

AI app building involves creating real applications and software without writing code by describing the app to an AI builder. The AI writes all the code, designs the screens, and fixes bugs you identify. You can either build apps for clients on a project-plus-retainer basis or launch your own app on a subscription model.

**Step-by-step instructions:**

1. Pick an AI app builder. Recommended tools: Lovable or Emergent.
2. Learn the tool by recreating a simple existing app such as a calculator, booking form, or quote generator.
3. To build a client project or your own app, enter a detailed prompt describing what the app should do. For example: "Create an app that helps people track their investments in one place, a portfolio tracker."
4. Answer the builder's clarifying questions and wait for the app to generate.
5. Test the app thoroughly from a user's perspective. Identify bugs, missing features, or UX issues.
6. Prompt the AI to fix issues. Iterate with back-and-forth prompting and testing until the app is ready.
7. For client work: deliver the app, charge a project fee, and offer monthly maintenance. For your own app: launch it and charge a monthly subscription.

**Learning curve:** 2–3 months
**AI contribution:** ~80%
**Startup capital:** ~$25–$50/month for the AI app builder
**Tool stack:** 1 tool — an AI app builder (Lovable, Emergent, or similar)
**Competition level:** Very low

## Best Practices

1. **Consistency beats intensity.** For AI clipping and AI avatars, posting daily compounds results. Most competitors quit within one to two weeks.
2. **Specialize to charge more.** In AI ghost writing and AI agents, focusing on one niche (finance, dental, fitness) positions you as an expert and justifies premium pricing.
3. **Always proof AI outputs.** AI handles 70–95% of the work, but the final 5–30% of human editing, taste, and quality control is what makes clients pay and audiences engage.
4. **Build proof before pitching.** For AI websites, AI agents, and AI app building, create a sample or demo first. A working demo sells the service far better than any pitch.
5. **Start with one tool stack.** Do not overcomplicate your workflow with five tools on day one. Master one AI clipping tool, one AI writer, or one AI builder before expanding.
6. **Track what works.** In AI clipping and AI avatars, double down on content formats that gain traction and drop what does not perform.
7. **Use free trials before paying.** For AI website builders and Shopify tools, practice with free tiers before committing monthly capital.
8. **Combine methods for compounding income.** Once profitable in one method, use earnings to fund experimentation in others.

## Keep In Mind

1. **Low barriers attract crowds.** The easier a method is to start, the more competition it has. AI clipping is the most competitive; AI app building and AI agents are the least competitive.
2. **AI does not replace judgment.** The human element — choosing campaigns, picking niches, editing AI output, and communicating with clients — is where you capture most of your value.
3. **Results take time.** AI agents require 1–2 months to land the first clients. AI avatars require 2–3 months to build an audience. Do not expect instant income.
4. **Pricing depends on results.** Charge per view for clipping, per page for websites, per project plus retainer for apps and agents, and per month for stores.
5. **Automation is not fully passive.** Even at 95% AI contribution, you must monitor campaigns, respond to clients, and optimize performance.
6. **Client trust matters.** For ghost writing, websites, and agents, the relationship and reliability of delivery determine whether clients stay long term.

## Security & Safety Notes

1. **Protect client data.** When using Claude Projects or any AI workspace for ghost writing, do not upload sensitive client information such as passwords, financial records, or private customer databases. Use only public or redacted materials.
2. **Verify AI-generated content before publishing.** AI avatars, ghost-written posts, and AI-generated clips can contain hallucinations, incorrect facts, or off-brand messaging. Always review content before it goes live under your name or a client's name.
3. **Use reputable tools.** Stick to well-known AI platforms (Opus Clip, Claude, Perplexity, Wix, Shopify, AutoDS, Dialzara, Higgsfield, Lovable, Emergent) rather than unvetted tools that may misuse data or fail to deliver.
4. **Secure payment and account credentials.** When managing client Shopify stores or app deployments, use strong, unique passwords and two-factor authentication. Do not share admin credentials publicly.
5. **Respect copyright and platform rules.** For AI clipping, only use content you have explicit rights or licenses to clip. Posting copyrighted long-form content without permission can result in takedowns or account bans. For AI avatars, ensure generated likenesses do not infringe on real people's identities.
6. **Keep software and integrations updated.** If you connect AutoDS, Shopify, or other e-commerce tools, regularly review permissions and disconnect unused integrations to reduce attack surface.

## Common Pitfalls

1. **Relying on AI without editing.** Posting raw AI-generated clips, ghost-written content, or unpolished landing pages will hurt your reputation and client results. Always add the human touch.
2. **Ignoring platform-specific formatting.** TikTok clips, Instagram Reels, LinkedIn posts, and landing pages each have different best practices. Formatting content for the wrong platform reduces performance.
3. **Targeting too broad a niche.** In ghost writing, websites, and AI agents, trying to serve every industry makes you uncompetitive. Pick one niche and dominate it.
4. **Underpricing services.** Beginners often charge too little for AI websites, ghost writing, or AI agents because they fear losing clients. Price based on the results you deliver, not the time you spend.
5. **Skipping the demo or sample.** In AI agents and AI websites, pitching without a working demo wastes time. Build proof first, then sell.
6. **Giving up too early.** For AI clipping, most people quit within one to two weeks. For AI agents, most give up before landing the first client. Consistency separates success from failure.
7. **Over-investing in tools at the start.** Paying for expensive subscriptions before generating any income increases risk. Use free tiers and trials until you validate demand.
8. **Not tracking performance.** Without view counts, conversion rates, or client retention data, you cannot optimize. Measure results from day one and adjust your approach based on data.
