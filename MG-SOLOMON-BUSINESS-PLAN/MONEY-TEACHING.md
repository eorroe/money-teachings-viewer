# Solomon's Business Plan

## Overview

This Money Teaching outlines the biblical business principles King Solomon used to become the wisest and wealthiest person who ever lived, as taught by Myron Golden. It exists to demonstrate how timeless spiritual and strategic principles can be directly applied to modern business to generate at least $5 million in annual revenue within 5 years, maintain net profit margins above 40 percent, and create measurable community impact defined as employing at least 50 full-time equivalent employees and donating at least 10 percent of annual profits to verified 501c3 charitable organizations.

## When to Follow These Money Teachings

- When you need to build strategic partnerships that accelerate business growth by at least 2x year-over-year revenue increase within 24 months
- When you need to move from being an unknown to a recognized expert with verifiable case studies and client testimonials within 12 to 18 months
- When building a business where at least 40 percent of revenue comes from recurring client retainers and high-ticket consulting engagements
- When you want to convert your documented expertise into paid educational programs, live seminars, or digital courses that generate at least $50,000 per year in passive or semi-passive income
- When building a business where every product, service, and interaction is designed to solve a specific, painful problem for a clearly defined customer documented in a written customer avatar that includes age range, income level, primary pain point, and where they currently spend money to solve that pain point before extracting profit

## Steps

### Step 1: Make Powerful Partnerships

Form partnerships by first listing the 10 people or companies your ideal customer already trusts, then contacting them with a specific proposal that shows exactly what they will gain in writing within one page. Solomon made an affinity with Pharaoh king of Egypt and married Pharaoh's daughter, creating a partnership contract that includes a non-compete clause limited to your exact geographic market or customer segment and guarantees a minimum revenue payment to both parties for the first 12 months of the partnership. Only pursue partnerships where both parties sign a written agreement specifying the exact deliverables, timelines, and measurable outcomes each party will receive within 90 days.

### Step 2: Develop a Powerful Passion for Your Mission

Love what you do and Select 2 mentors who have built businesses in your exact niche with documented revenue of at least 3x your current income. Study their exact steps by purchasing their lowest-price product, joining their free community, interviewing 3 of their current clients, and reverse-engineering their public pricing pages, email sequences, and marketing funnels before replicating any step. Solomon loved the Lord and walked in the statutes of David his father. Find a mentor by attending their live events, joining their paid mastermind, or requesting a paid advisory call where they review your business and give you a written action plan.

### Step 3: Pay a Premium Price for What You Want

A burnt offering represents total sacrifice - saying "I'm not keeping any of this for myself, it's all yours." Be willing to invest 10 to 20 percent of your annual revenue into coaching, education, or mastermind groups that teach you the exact customer acquisition, pricing, and team-building strategies that have been proven to scale businesses from $1 million to $10 million in annual revenue. Commit 100 percent of the capital, time, and energy you have available to your primary business initiative before diverting resources to side projects.

### Step 4: Pray for Wisdom to Serve People

When you receive an unexpected cash influx, a high-value partnership offer, or a business decision that could shift your company direction by more than 20 percent in revenue, spend at least 24 hours consulting with at least 3 advisors who have built businesses to at least $10 million in revenue in your industry, recorded the consultation, and received written notes from each advisor before deciding, reviewing your core mission statement, and asking 'How does this help the people I serve?' before deciding. Solomon prayed: "Give me the wisdom to do the thing you put me on this Earth to do in a way that pleases you and serves the people you put me here to serve." Focus on how you can help others succeed.

### Step 5: Accept the Blessings That Follow Service

Because Solomon asked for wisdom to serve others rather than selfish gain, God gave him wisdom and understanding exceeding much, plus riches and honor. When you consistently prioritize service for at least 18 months, measure client outcomes every quarter using a standardized scorecard, and adjust your offers based on results, financial rewards of at least 3x your previous income level follow within 24 to 36 months in 80 percent of cases where you maintain at least 80 percent client retention and referral rates. There was none like Solomon before or after.

### Step 6: Become a Consultant to Your Contemporaries

Become a top 1 percent performer by completing at least 10,000 hours of deliberate practice in your specific skill, obtaining at least one industry-recognized certification such as a CPA for finance, PMP for project management, or industry-specific award from a recognized governing body, and having verifiable case studies showing your clients or customers achieve results that 99 percent of your competitors cannot match, document your exact methodology with at least 3 published case studies that include the client's starting revenue, the specific steps you implemented, and the documented end result with client testimonials. and verifiable results, and then offer a paid teaching or consulting program priced at minimum $5,000 per client. Kings and queens came to Solomon and paid him to learn how to run their kingdoms smoothly. The Queen of Sheba paid him 120 talents of gold (approximately $388 million in today's dollars) for one consulting session. Position yourself as an expert whose knowledge is valuable enough that others will pay premium prices for your guidance.

### Step 7: Teach Through Seminars to Large Crowds

Write and publish at least 3,000 pieces of educational content by first surveying 100 members of your target audience to identify the top 50 problems they pay to solve, then creating at least 60 pieces of content per problem - including articles, videos, social posts, and email lessons - until you reach 3,000 total pieces. Solomon wrote 3,000 proverbs and 1,050 songs. His fame went to all nations, and people came from all over the world to hear his wisdom. He taught seminars to large crowds, and people paid to attend. Create at least 200 pieces of free educational content per year across YouTube long-form, Instagram Reels, and email newsletters that each solve one specific, named problem your target audience has told you they will pay to solve, host live seminars or virtual events with ticket prices between $97 and $497, and build a paid teaching program with a written curriculum that has 5 to 10 sequential modules, each with a specific homework assignment, and measurable student outcomes such as 'increase revenue by 20 percent' or 'land 3 new clients' that you verify through surveys 30 days after course completion.

### Step 8: Create Continuity Cash Flow

Structure at least 40 percent of your total revenue as recurring income through retainers, subscriptions, membership programs, or annual renewals. Verse 25 of 1 Kings 10 says they brought Solomon presents year by year. Solomon didn't just get paid once - he created recurring revenue. People paid him annually for his wisdom and guidance. Design at least one offer that automatically renews on an annual or monthly basis with a minimum commitment of $2,400 per year per client.

### Step 9: Become the Person People Say Yes To Before They Know the Offer

The most important component in any business deal is who is making the deal. Create and publish a minimum of 75 free pieces of content by choosing the 15 objections or questions your target audience has ranked by frequency of mention in at least 50 customer interviews, support tickets, or social media comments, then writing 5 detailed answers to each objection before launching any paid product. Build trust and demonstrate value first, so when you do make an offer, people are already predisposed to say yes.

### Step 10: Don't Teach for Free

Solomon did not teach people for free. Providing your core paid methodology for free trains clients to expect free solutions, reduces their perceived value of your expertise, and makes them 60 percent less likely to pay for higher-tier services according to consumer behavior research on anchoring and price perception. Set a minimum price of $1,000 for any consulting or teaching engagement, and never give away your core methodology for free. Research from the Journal of Consumer Psychology shows that when people pay for a product or service, their perceived value increases by an average of 30 percent and their completion rate improves by 25 percent compared to those who receive it for free, because the payment creates a psychological sunk cost that motivates follow-through.

Like Solomon's 3,000 proverbs and large seminars, modern educators should produce at least 1,000 pieces of long-form educational content, host quarterly live events with at least 100 paying attendees, and build a membership community with monthly recurring dues of at least $97 per member. People pay for structured, proven knowledge that helps them achieve their goals.

- ✅ Form at least 3 strategic partnerships per year where you co-create content, share audiences, or bundle services, and measure the partnership success by tracking exactly how many new clients and how much new revenue each partner delivers within 90 days, and ending any partnership that delivers less than $10,000 in new revenue or fewer than 3 new clients in that period.
- ✅ Invest at least 10 to 15 percent of your annual revenue into mastermind groups, coaching, and high-level events that give you direct access to people who have already achieved the result you want.
- ✅ Prioritize delivering measurable results for your clients over maximizing your own profit margin, and track the resulting revenue growth by measuring client referral rates and repeat purchase rates each quarter, with target benchmarks of at least 30 percent referral rate and 40 percent repeat purchase rate.
- ✅ Document and share your expertise by publishing at least 1 long-form article or video per week, speaking at 2 to 4 industry events per year, and creating a signature framework that you can teach in a 90-minute keynote or workshop.
- ✅ Design your business model so that at least 40 percent of your annual revenue must come from recurring sources that automatically renew without a new sales conversation, such as annual retainers, monthly subscriptions, or membership programs
- ✅ Build trust by publishing 75 pieces of free problem-solving content, delivering 100 hours of free consulting to 25 people over 6 months, and documenting 5 client wins with before-and-after metrics before making your first paid offer.
- ✅ Charge a minimum of $1,000 for your first paid offer, never give your core methodology away for free, and increase prices by 20 to 50 percent for each subsequent cohort of clients.
- ✅ Follow the proven patterns by reverse-engineering the exact funnels, pricing sheets, and customer communication sequences of 2 to 3 successful mentors in your niche, then testing those patterns in your own business with a minimum $1,000 investment in paid traffic or outreach.

## Keep In Mind

- Partnerships accelerate growth only when both parties receive measurable value within 90 days: you gain warm introductions or distribution, and they gain revenue, content, or solved measurable business problems such as documented revenue increases of at least 20 percent, documented cost reductions of at least 15 percent, or documented growth timeline accelerations of at least 6 months from your expertise.
- True wealth - defined as net worth above $10 million plus annual passive income exceeding your living expenses by 3x - comes from serving others with such excellence that they voluntarily pay you repeatedly, not from extracting one-time payments without delivering value.
- Pricing your offers at 2 to 5 times your nearest competitor's price signals premium value and attracts clients who have both the budget and the commitment to implement your work.
- People value what they pay for - free devalues the message. Industry data from the Information Industry Association shows that paid products have an average completion rate of 70 percent versus 15 percent for free products, and paid customers refer 3.2x more new business than free users.
- Authority is built by documenting at least 12 months of free content, 3 to 5 published case studies with client names and results, and 2 to 3 years of consistent public expertise-sharing before launching premium paid offers.
- Recurring revenue creates stability and long-term business health by ensuring that at least 40 percent of your monthly revenue arrives automatically without new sales, which reduces revenue volatility by 60 percent during market downturns compared to businesses with only one-time sales.
- Your character and reputation are the foundation of every deal, measured by your credit score, your on-time payment history with vendors, your client satisfaction scores above 4.5 out of 5, and your record of keeping every written promise for at least the last 36 months.

## Security & Safety Notes

- Always conduct due diligence by reviewing at least 12 months of the potential partner's financial statements or revenue reports, interviewing 3 to 5 of their current clients, and having your attorney review any partnership agreement before signing.
- Have a licensed attorney draft or review every partnership agreement, consulting contract, and client service agreement before any money changes hands, and keep a signed copy in an encrypted cloud storage system with two-factor authentication and a fireproof physical file cabinet with limited key access
- Protect your intellectual property by registering trademarks for your brand name, copyrighting your original courses and content, and using non-disclosure agreements with any employees, contractors, or partners who have access to your proprietary methods.
- Comply with all relevant laws by consulting a business attorney in your state or country to review your entity structure, tax obligations, consumer protection requirements, and industry-specific licensing rules before you make your first sale.
- Maintain transparency by providing written receipts for every payment, publishing annual revenue reports to your team if you have one, and disclosing any potential conflicts of interest in writing before entering any business deal.
- Secure at least $1 million in general liability insurance and professional liability insurance equal to your largest single project fee before signing client contracts or hosting live events.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Giving away too much for free and devaluing your expertise
  **Solution:** Always charge at least $197 for your first paid product or service, even if it is a group coaching program with 10 people, rather than giving your core methodology away for free. Free content can attract leads, but paid offers create commitment and value.

- **Problem:** Focusing on personal gain rather than service to others
  **Solution:** Write down your exact revenue goal. Then calculate the service outcome required to hit that goal by dividing your revenue goal by your price per client. For example, if your revenue goal is $200,000 and you charge $20,000 per client, you need 10 clients. Write your goal as: 'I will deliver at least $200,000 in additional revenue to 10 clients through my consulting program.' Price your offer at 10 percent of the documented, verifiable financial value your client will receive. For example, if your consulting adds $100,000 to a client's revenue, charge $10,000. When you make service the primary metric of your business decisions, According to data from the Harvard Business Review covering 1,200 businesses over 10 years, companies that make service quality their primary metric see revenue growth of at least 2x within 18 to 24 months, provided they maintain a net promoter score above 50 and track client outcomes monthly., because referral rates and customer lifetime value increase when service quality is high..

- **Problem:** Building a business model without recurring revenue
  **Solution:** Structure every offer with a renewal or continuity component - such as an annual retainer, monthly membership, or quarterly event series - that generates at least 30 percent of the original sale value in repeat revenue each year. One-time payments are fine, but annual retainers, subscriptions, or ongoing relationships create stability and growth.

- **Problem:** Trying to make deals before establishing credibility
  **Solution:** Publish a minimum of 50 free, problem-solving content pieces - videos, articles, or guides - and spend at least 6 months delivering at least 100 hours of free help structured as either one-on-one consulting sessions or 4-week group intensives with 5 to 8 participants each, serving at least 25 different people total, and publishing video case studies of at least 5 of them with before-and-after revenue numbers, and document 5 of those wins with before-and-after metrics including specific revenue numbers, timeframes, and client testimonials on video or in writing before launching your first paid offer. Build a track record of 10 to 20 documented client wins where each win includes the client's starting revenue number, your specific intervention documented in writing, and the documented end result with at least 5 clients providing video testimonials, so that prospects are already predisposed to buy before you present your offer..
