# I Slept In a Garage... Now I'm Worth $100 Million ｜ Brent Franklin

## Overview

This money teaching distills actionable principles from Brent Franklin's journey from high school dropout and roughneck to oil and gas mogul worth tens of millions. The content covers the power of sales skills, solving problems instead of chasing trends, hiring A-players, and building competitive advantages through self-education.

**Video:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gqRTUYwxmjM

## When to Follow These Money Teachings

- When entering a blue-collar industry with massive wealth potential
- When you lack formal education but want to build a business
- When learning how to find distressed assets and solve problems
- When hiring and building a team as a first-time entrepreneur
- When the user asks about oil and gas, sales careers, or building from nothing

## Steps

### Step 1: Master Sales if You Want to Make Millions

Brent's core advice is simple: if you want to make millions, go to sales. Sales is the highest-income skill because it directly generates revenue. If you want to be wealthy and make billions, become good enough at sales that you can hire and scale. Everything starts with the ability to persuade someone to act.

### Step 2: Solve Problems That People Already Have

Brent made his first money in oil and gas by finding distressed assets, getting them under contract, and flipping them to buyers without using his own capital. The formula is universal: find a painful problem, solve it, and get paid. Do not chase trends. Solve problems that already exist.

### Step 3: Use Self-Education to Accelerate

Brent reads a book a day and listens to Audible instead of waiting for formal education. He learned about hiring from "Who Not How" and about team dynamics from "Rocket Fuel." The best mentors are the authors who have already done what you are trying to do.

### Step 4: Hire A-Players, Not Just Friends

Brent's first mistake was hiring friends because they had good energy. Good energy does not equal results. Write down exactly what you need, then find the person who can deliver it without you micromanaging. An A-player can outperform 30 B-players.

### Step 5: Find Your Competitive Advantage and Double Down

Brent leveraged his Navy background, his willingness to work harder than anyone else, and his ability to learn fast. He turned each disadvantage into an advantage. Identify what makes you different and use it as leverage in every negotiation and sale.

## Examples

### Example 1: The Roughneck to Entrepreneur Path

Brent started as a roughneck in West Texas making $23 an hour with well bonuses. He worked 12 to 16 hour days, seven days a week, until his body could not take it. That grind gave him the credibility and network to move into land and asset acquisition.

### Example 2: The Distressed Asset Flip

Brent found a distressed real estate asset, got it under contract, and flipped it to a buyer without paying for it himself. That experience taught him that solving problems is more profitable than working for hourly wages.

### Example 3: The Hiring Pivot

After hiring friends who were good people but wrong for the role, Brent read "Who Not How" and changed his entire hiring process. He started writing down exactly what he needed and finding people who could deliver results without being told how.

## Best Practices

- ✅ Master sales as your first and most important skill
- ✅ Solve existing problems instead of chasing new trends
- ✅ Read books and learn from those who have already succeeded
- ✅ Hire based on results and DISC assessment, not just friendship
- ✅ Turn disadvantages into competitive advantages
- ❌ Don't hire friends just because they have good energy
- ❌ Don't wait for the perfect opportunity; create one by solving a problem
- ❌ Don't stay in a negative environment that drains your energy

## Keep In Mind

- Entrepreneurship is not for everyone, and that is okay
- Quick success creates ego; sustained success requires resilience
- Anything you want to learn has already been written by someone who has been there
- Your network is built one problem solved at a time

## Security & Safety Notes

- Always disclose material facts in any asset purchase or sale
- Keep all contracts and agreements in writing
- Consult with a lawyer before entering into any partnership or purchase agreement
- Never invest in oil and gas or real estate without proper due diligence

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Hiring based on personality instead of results
  **Solution:** Use assessments and track record, not just vibes

- **Problem:** Chasing the next trend instead of solving persistent problems
  **Solution:** Focus on pain points that have existed for years, not weeks

- **Problem:** Relying on hourly wages instead of building equity
  **Solution:** Move from labor to asset acquisition as quickly as possible

- **Problem:** Staying in negative environments because of comfort
  **Solution:** Quit doing things you hate. Surround yourself with people who elevate you.
