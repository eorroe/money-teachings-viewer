# He Dropped Out in 9th Grade… Now He Has a $90M Business ｜ Joshua Crisp

## Overview

This teaching distills the financial and business principles from Joshua Crisp, who dropped out in 9th grade with no GED, no high school diploma, and no college degree — and built a $90 million business through digital marketing. His story demonstrates that formal education is not a prerequisite for financial success. The key skills he developed — identifying winning products, crafting irresistible offers, and executing fast — are not taught in traditional classrooms. The single most important action you can take when starting out is simply to begin, regardless of your credentials or background.

## Steps

### Step 1: Refuse to Let Credential Gaps Define Your Limits

Do not allow the absence of a diploma, degree, or formal qualification to stop you from pursuing business. Joshua Crisp has no GED, no high school diploma, and no college degree — yet he has built a $90 million business and even taught at Harvard. The market does not pay for credentials; it pays for results. Your job is to produce results, not to collect paper.

### Step 2: Start Before You Feel Ready

Take action immediately, even if you feel unprepared or unqualified. Joshua's core advice is simple: "The biggest piece of advice, and it's free to do, quick to implement, and will change your life and trajectory of your business profitability in any niche or vertical is to start." Waiting for the right moment, the right skills, or the right opportunity is a form of procrastination. The learning that happens in execution is far more valuable than any course.

### Step 3: Master the Craft of the Irresistible Offer

Learn how to craft offers so compelling that customers feel foolish saying no. In digital marketing, the quality of the offer matters more than the quality of the traffic. A mediocre offer with great copy will outperform a great offer with poor copy. Study what makes offers convert: urgency, specificity, risk reversal, and clear value articulation.

### Step 4: Identify and Test Winning Products Rapidly

Build a process for finding products that have natural market demand before investing heavily in promotion. Joshua became a millionaire through bath bombs — a product category he identified as having viral potential and low competition. Test multiple products quickly rather than falling in love with a single idea. Let the market tell you what works through sales data, not your intuition.

### Step 5: Accept That Sacrifice Is the Price of Extraordinary Results

Recognize that achieving a financial outcome that is dramatically different from your current situation requires a level of sacrifice most people are not willing to make. Joshua hit a low point where his kids were asking why Santa never brought them what they wanted and his wife was asking why they had to eat ramen every day. That pain was the fuel for the $11.3 million annual income he later achieved. If you are not willing to sacrifice the comfortable present for an uncertain future, you will get the predictable result — mediocrity.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Problem:** Using lack of credentials as a reason not to start
  **Solution:** Start now using whatever resources you have — execution beats credentials every time

- **Problem:** Waiting for the perfect product or idea before launching
  **Solution:** Test multiple offers quickly; the market will tell you what works faster than your analysis

- **Problem:** Over-learning without executing
  **Solution:** Take one small action today — launch one product, write one ad, make one offer. Learning happens through doing

- **Problem:** Giving up when early results are slow
  **Solution:** Commit to a minimum viable effort period — most digital marketing businesses require 6-12 months to gain real momentum
